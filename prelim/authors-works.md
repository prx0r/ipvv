# Abhinavagupta (Abhinavagupta)
Dates: {'flourished': 'c. 975-1025 CE', 'note': 'dates approximate per Sanderson/Dyczkowski; do not present as exact'}
Place: Kashmir  Status: dates-approximate
Summary: Kashmir Shaiva master (fl. c. 975-1025). Systematiser of Trika/Kaula/Krama/Pratyabhijna: encyclopaedic Tantraloka, its distillation Tantrasara, the two Pratyabhijna commentaries (IPV/IPVV), the Paratrishika exegesis, and early devotional stotras. Doctrine in one line: recognition (pratyabhijna) — the self, already established, re-cognises itself as the Lord; vimarsha (reflexive awareness) = para-vac = the throb.
Lineage:
  - pupil of: Laksman Gupta (Pratyabhijna; pupil of Utpaladeva)
  - initiated by: Sambhunatha (Kaula/Trika)
  - studied Krama under: Bhutiraja
