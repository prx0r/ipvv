## pan-p02 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Speech becomes the offering: the word crying victory at the threshold is itself to become bliss-giving; having Maheśa as protector is to become being protected.

*Rasa: bhakti (devotional praise) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion expressed as praise (jaya); the threshold (dvāra) is vibhāva, speech (vāk) is the offering. No sorrow of absence; rasa is joy of praise.*
