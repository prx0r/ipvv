## pan-p03 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Five refusals charged — speak, see, hear, come, support — and scripture converts the charge into doctrine: the Lord is sense-free.

*Rasa: karuṇa (compassion-plea) shaded by adbhuta (wonder at doctrine) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of complaint (vijñapti); the vyabhicāri is despair (duḥkha), integrated by the turn to śruti's declaration of sense-freedom.*
