## bodh-p01-v01 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Oṃ to Netra; constant salute to five-act Śiva, supreme-self bliss manifest. Consciousness free, world's cause so-called; knower freedom-formed, powers bestowed. Own ground, own will, world's unfolding; fit variety divides seer-seen. World's variety stands by object-difference, manifold-yoked high-low-numbered. Tightened knowing becomes mind, knower still; banyan-seed holds world-form, wise-known. Self's stainless knowing, rule-bound, named mind-knowing. Object-faced awareness rests in mind; the wise know mind as knowing. Knower knowing-formed abides embodied, clinging, discernment-void. Mind ever-one established through listed means and counts. Lord everywhere-pure; mis-knowing I-ness brings freedom palm-close.

*Rasa: maṅgala-bhakti of invocation; vīra of free cause; adbhuta of unborrowing unfolding; adbhuta of arranged variety; adbhuta of knower-tightened; śānta of stainless naming; śānta of rested awareness; karuṇa of bound knower; vīra of established oneness; adbhuta of palm-close freedom — The sthāyibhāva is love-address: Oṃ, salute, salute. Invoking relished. The sthāyibhāva is exultation: free, cause, bestowing. Naming relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: own ground, own will, divided fitness. Unfolding relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: difference arranging high-low. Arrangement relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: tightened yet knower, seed-contained. Tightening relished. The sthāyibhāva is peace: stainless, bound, still knowing. Naming relished. The sthāyibhāva is peace: rested, known by wise. Knowing relished. The sthāyibhāva is compassion: knowing-shaped, discernment-void. Binding beheld. The sthāyibhāva is exultation: ever-one, means-counted. Establishment relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: mis-known, palm-close. Nearness relished.*
