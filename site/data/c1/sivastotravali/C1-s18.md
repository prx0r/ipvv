## s18 — C1 (song essay)

**SUMMARY.** It watches the hidden become manifest without ceasing to be hidden: found inside the world, never far (1); He here, She everywhere, no division at the top (2); the ungrateful blind, the risen mind unseeing — ah, destroyed (3); needing nothing, I bow (4); burning world, make me worship-joy (5); spontaneity, touch, world-tour (6); things coming and going, may they shine (7); ever in His city, never defeated (8); cool nectar-floods of Your limb-touch filling all round (9); tree-shade, forest, self: three dwellings (10); appear before me, three-eyed, trident-graced (11); pride offered, devotion weighed (12); tasting everything everywhere (13); Your thing, Your truth — who can disclose (14); no desire for peace, no begging remembered (15); waking or beyond, festival in the ever-present mind (16); loosened yet unswerving (17); nothing unmade, yet all suffers — bow to the wonder (18); doubt-foe crushed, sight unbroken (19); enter me plainly, worship at speed (20); no one's is praise-power, yet it exists since You are beautiful — may I see unwearied (21).

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of manifesting (āviṣkāra); the vyabhicāri is wonder (camatkāra) at the hidden becoming manifest without ceasing to be hidden. The rasa is the tranquility of manifesting — the Absolute shown without being exhausted. Lakshmanjoo: revelation (āviṣkāra) — the hidden becomes manifest without ceasing to be hidden.
