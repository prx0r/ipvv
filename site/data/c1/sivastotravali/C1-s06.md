## s06 — C1 (song essay)

**SUMMARY.** Separation itself becomes the method: burning to be seen (1), unparted-in-Him while parted-from-world (2), all goings as Him (3), speech matching His bliss (4), roaming desireless in His possession (5), contentment ending toil (6), things melting skyward (7), the jewel blazing from His middle (8), arrival everywhere by effortlessness (9), embrace by mere will (10) — then the single imperative: appear, with all other prayers renounced (11).

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with vīra (heroism) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of burning (tāpa) and seeing (paśyan); the vyabhicāri is effortlessness (anāyāsa) and contentment (santuṣṭi). The rasa is the tranquility of the path bursting forth — burning to be seen, then effortlessness.*
