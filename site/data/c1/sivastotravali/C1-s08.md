## s08 — C1 (song essay)

**SUMMARY.** Grace-drop above and devotion rising below: it watches their meeting produce unworldly upsurge — chewing lordship (2), thirsting with unconstructed sight (3), demanding plainness in both registers (4), seating the faculties (5), exceeding all dealings in a possession-body (6), blooming and joining into untellability (7), flooding the dread gorge (8); then the wager — union-drops against world-pleasures, with double-fall risk named (9); hamsa-and-stalk botany joining below, inside, above (10); all things under thought's scope without seizure (11); practice for catastrophe — limb joined, joy-mad (12); and the closing epistemology: woven in Him, shown by Him, certain — shine (13).

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with vīra (heroism) and adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of upsurge (udbala); the vyabhicāri is wonder (camatkāra) at the grace-devotion meeting and vertigo of the possession-body. The rasa is the tranquility of unworldly upsurge — not worldly excitement but the peace of exceeding all dealings. Abhinava's rasa theory frames this as śānta: the relish (camatkāra) of the Absolute's unworldly upsurge.*
