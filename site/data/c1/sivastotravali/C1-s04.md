## s04 — C1 (song essay)

**SUMMARY.** It is direct address under unfitness: the fickle mind praised for turning (1), the lowest ground kept after arrival (2), reveal-and-hide demanded with use-me-now (3), a spell of names (4); then the world re-seen — Brahmā laughed at (5), nothing apart from His body (6), two tastes of one dust (7), flash-devotion (8), discernment demanding plain manifestation (9), placed-unasked complaint (10); then love's evidence — blooming gods (11), separation-talk fruiting (12), the sideways-entering word (13), winning faces (14); then the impasse and its passage — finished world, unknocked door (15), just-a-little asked (16), knowing self-accusation (17), tasting-frequency fitness (18), others blind but MY cry heard (19); then exchange and arrival — pleasures recalled to be exchanged (20), hundreds of omens (21), the shining night (22), empire tasteless (23), the knot He tied (24), and the final inversion: the worshipped stays the worshipper, shining in seers' bodies (25).

*Rasa: bhakti (devotion) with karuṇa (compassion) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of demand (prārthanā) and confession (āvedana); the vyabhicāri is compassion (karuṇa) at the devotee's unfitness. The rasa is the devotion of the unfit — demanding, complaining, crying out — proving that unfitness is no bar to address. Abhinava's rasa theory frames this as bhakti: the relish (camatkāra) of the Absolute addressed by one who is unworthy yet addresses anyway. The stotra negotiates: demand (dehi, kuru, prakaṭaya), complaint (placed-unasked), and crying out (MY cry heard).*
