## anu-p05 — C1

*Rasa: ānanda (bliss) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of delight (rati); the vyabhicāri is joy (harṣa) of seeing variety as one-self-natured consciousness. The rasa is bliss as delight, not as possession — seeing variety as consciousness-form, delight is the only consistent response.*
