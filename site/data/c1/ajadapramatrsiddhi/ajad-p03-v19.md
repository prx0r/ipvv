## ajad-p03-v19 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Breath joined with knowing still never proves: breath and kin are knowables, never knowers — else delusion. Locked in the living though things stand, curbed by breath and eightfold city, even there they abide in the supreme self. Breath proves no hold on that self-nature; recognition answers by living-ness past non-showing. No ordering sits in breath by I-blocking; shining's self-rest is sung as I-ness. Told already, that rest, all dependence blocked: freedom, doership foremost, lordship with it. Called Māyā, of his power alone, no other proven — world-maker lordship is non-showing-made. One alone through linking, proclaimed mother-principle of all awarenesses, self-feeling's synonym, beginningless. Pervasion and linking of the I-sharer fit nothing inert. So in difference shining's shining holds — recognition's word: this world that-nondual-made.

*Rasa: vīra (triumph) of declaration — The sthāyibhāva is exultation: four verbs of proclaiming — sung (v22), told (v23), proclaimed (v25), said (v27) — carry the passage to its verdict. The vyabhicāri is wonder at rest unfolding into lordship. Abhinava's vīra as the relish of truth declared after dispute — homecoming spoken aloud.*
