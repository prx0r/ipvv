## param-p02-v11 — C1

**SUMMARY.** The supreme principle, free of thought, pure and at peace, shines the thirty-six-fold world as towns shine in a mirror — undivided yet divided, lit by the five powers in divided nature. Supreme freedom effects what is hard; Māyā-power veils Śiva's self. Grasped knowing stains and the person turns beast, sixfold fettered; the atom knows but little here and now, six-sheathed within. As husk on rice, separate yet one, purity comes by the Śiva path; mere pleasure-pain-delusion proceeds from resolve into the ordered inner organ.

*Rasa: śānta of taught order — Doctrinal enumeration settling into peace: tattvas, organs, elements told in order.*
