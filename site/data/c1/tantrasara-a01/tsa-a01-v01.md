## tsa-a01-v01 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Mother of stainless art and new great creation, full-bodied; Father of five hidden faces: may their yamal-burst-forth Heart — deathless kin beyond all — flash forth. The Tantrāloka spreads too wide to dive; therefore its essence in plain words — hear it. The heart-lotus, its narrowness gone by the feet-fall splendor of Śambhunātha the Sun, is gathered for the Great Lord's worship. Knowledge frees as opponent of bondage-cause ignorance, twofold: intellect-located doubt-nature and wrong-conviction-nature, personal thought-nature and contracted manifestation — root of saṃsāra. The personal washes by initiation, but initiation needs prior shun-and-take determination, impossible while intellect cannot decide; hence determinative intellect-dwelling knowledge leads, and practiced kills the personal too, ending in thought-free. Only Lord-spoken scripture fully authorizes and fully frees — five streams, sixfold scriptures, Mālinīvijaya whose contained content no gathering can expound. Own-nature alone is adoptable: one autonomous light-consciousness, all-pervading, eternal, form and formless; autonomy is bliss, wonder is will, light is consciousness, grasp is knowing, all-fitness is action. Resting in own bliss it is Śiva; contracting itself, called atom; re-manifesting with or without means — will, knowing, action: three immersions, plus a fourth taught here in order.

*Rasa: adbhuta-śānta of invocation settling into teaching — Opening wonder (maṅgala flash, unplungeable ocean, gathered lotus) settling into taught peace (determination, hierarchy, one light): invoked, asked in effect, answered in full.*
