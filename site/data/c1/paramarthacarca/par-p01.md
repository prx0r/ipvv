## par-p01 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Distinct from every lit thing, light itself does not shine as an object — too pervasive to appear; illumined-vs-illuminator is mere convention.

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is knowledge (jñāna) in the mode of demotion of subject-object; the vyabhicāri is wonder (camatkāra) at light's over-pervasion.*
