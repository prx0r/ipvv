## par-p02 — C1

**SUMMARY.** No thing stands dually apart from knowledge; in consciousness there is no difference; the individual, in the one abode of self-luminosity, stays undivided.

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is knowledge (jñāna) in the mode of non-duality; the vyabhicāri is wonder at the individual undivided.*
