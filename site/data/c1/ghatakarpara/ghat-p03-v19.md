## ghat-p03-v19 — C1

*Rasa: vira/adbhuta (heroic skill + wonder at craft), resolving from śṛṅgāra — The rasa shifts: the lover's voice yields to the poet's. The sthāyibhāva (love) transforms into the poet's delight at his own craft (camatkāra). The potsherd is both vessel and namesake. Flagged as application: Abhinava does not explicitly name this shift.*
