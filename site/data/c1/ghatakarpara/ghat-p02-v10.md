## ghat-p02-v10 — C1

*Rasa: śṛṅgāra (erotic, landscape as vibhāva) — The grove is vibhāva, not backdrop: 'for Kāma's sake the groves shine.' The sthāyibhāva transforms the landscape (rajas obscuring sun/moon) into its own body. Kuṭaja mockery and nīpa release are anubhāva. The rasa overwhelms perception.*
