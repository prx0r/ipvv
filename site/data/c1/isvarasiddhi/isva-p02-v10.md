## isva-p02-v10 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Fitness shows the higher self in eye and the rest, the way combination is seen for another's sake. Fire comes proved as khadira-wood through mere knowing, the way smoke proves from afar. The hetu is fenced to bare locus-form, refusing bare variety the particular's mark. Others sift reasons the same way in the pot's self. The mutual-fit whole stands rightly Lord-made. Helpers answer strangely — some joining with stuff — picturing effects by entering-as-other, like earth-saps. Outward-turned others cannot yoke form-variety. Fancied spread, named but unshown, orders nothing alone.

*Rasa: vīra (confidence) of marshalled examples — The sthāyibhāva is exultation: eye, fire, pot, helpers parade and each is turned. The vyabhicāri is wonder that mere knowing proves wood-kind from afar. Proof by examples, relished.*
