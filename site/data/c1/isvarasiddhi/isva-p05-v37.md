## isva-p05-v37 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Since intellect's arising stands unperceived by any spirit, no purpose follows — unspeakable, of spirit or of nature, though mind's motions come conditioned each in private ways. Will — how serve every soul? Give it to nature-in-general, and every soul turns all-knowing. One light would mix all shinings unless some One, in all undivided, keeps each apart. No world-plan makes seed put forth sprout; blind nature, kit and cause, brings green about — the wise join part to part, calling it spirit's purpose by courtesy. Granted — still, when purpose ends, the Root would churn its effects and never cease: sevenfold nature grants no release.

*Rasa: raudra-adbhuta of reductio pressed to the turn — The sthāyibhāva is reasoning-press: unperceived will failing, generic will exploding into omniscience, nature-alone churning without release. The vyabhicāri is astonishment at the One undivided holding all spirits. Press relished.*
