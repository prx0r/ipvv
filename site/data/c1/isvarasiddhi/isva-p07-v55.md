## isva-p07-v55 — C1

**SUMMARY.** A thousand blazing reasons prove — yet not for minds astray; palm-held Maheśvara flees the fated, Jewel slipping away. Your own Self flashes — Īśvara in every breather's heart, Maker and Knower — yet Recognition stands apart; we wrought its joining elsewhere, and in duality too it stands — fit, and fuller: midnight fire burns brighter in our hands.

*Rasa: śānta-adbhuta of closing praise — The sthāyibhāva is wonder: thousand-proved yet ungrasped, palm-held yet fled — and Self flashing within every breather, midnight fire. Closing marvel relished.*
