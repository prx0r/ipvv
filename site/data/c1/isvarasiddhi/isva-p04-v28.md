## isva-p04-v28 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Without that, no pot without a potter — how else? From clay-lump nature, own-nature watches potters. Entering matter with helpers never arrays — able though they be. Wisdom linked to world-traffic: never without. No will, no wonders — mere gross, never world-good. Rule-marvels only if mere potter-help bore pots. Never bodies otherwise — end-led array excels. Neither Lord nor un-Lord atomically taken — yet need fulfilled. Pradhāna will-less — person-aim-ness stands.

*Rasa: vīra (triumph) of workshop disputation — The sthāyibhāva is exultation: potter needed, helpers failing, will required, ends leading. The vyabhicāri is admiration for ends-first reasoning. Disputation relished.*
