## bh-p05 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Darkness already burned away by awareness; four terrors then met with salutation alone, yielding absolute fearlessness.

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of fearlessness (abhaya); the vyabhicāri is wonder (camatkāra) at darkness dispelled. The rasa is the tranquility of no-fear, not the thrill of confrontation — the terrors are met with salutation, not defiance.*
