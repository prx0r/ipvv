## bh-p03 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Fear-talk denied while suffering conceded: no discussion of transmigration-fear, yet affliction stands granted — denial and concession in one breath.

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with karuṇa (compassion) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of fearless recognition; the vyabhicāri is concession (anukampa) of suffering without fear. The rasa is the tranquility of non-dual recognition facing suffering — not the joy of denial but the peace of no-fear.*
