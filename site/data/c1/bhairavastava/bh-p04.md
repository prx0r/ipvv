## bh-p04 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Death's gaze turned aside by prayer; service and firm contemplation produce the power-filled state, testified with asmi — I am.

*Rasa: vīra (heroism) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of prayer (prārthanā); the vyabhicāri is courage (dhairya) filled with Bhairava-power. The rasa is heroic, not tranquil — the devotee declares power against death's gaze. Abhinava's rasa theory frames this as vīra: the sthāyibhāva of utsāha (energy) aimed at the Absolute.*
