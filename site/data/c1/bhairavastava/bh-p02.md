## bh-p02 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Grace first discloses the world as His; then identity declared outright: Maheśa, always my self — and all mine through Him.

*Rasa: śānta (tranquility) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of recognition (pratyabhijñā); the vyabhicāri is wonder (camatkāra) at grace disclosing the world as His. The rasa is the tranquility of non-dual identity, not the joy of acquisition — all is mine already, through Him.*
