**SUMMARY.** In being-full in the Lord, the self enters bliss (nirvṛti). The condition is
pūrṇatā (fullness) in bhāva (the Lord's state); the result is entry (emi, "I enter") into
a state the self was not already in.

**FUNCTION.** The result of v4 (method) and v6 (nondifference): bliss as entered, not
discovered. Names nirvṛti as the stotra's central soteriological term.

**KEY TERMS.** nirvṛti (entering-without-movement / bliss) · pūrṇatā (fullness) ·
bhāva (state/being) · ātmani asmātman (in my self) · emi (I go/enter).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Verse 6 of 10. The first result-claim after the method (v4) and
fearlessness (v5): bliss enters through fullness.

**EXPLANATION.** The verb emi (go/enter) is movement, not stasis — the self enters a
state, it does not discover one it already occupies. The condition is pūrṇatā in bhāva
(the Lord's being-full), which links back to v1 (mind already His) and v4 (substance
made of power). The compound asmātman ("my self") keeps the subject devotional — this
is not the universal ātman entering the universal Ānanda, but the devotee's self entering
bliss through fullness-in-You.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Not claimed: that nirvṛti = mokṣa, kaivalya, or any named liberation
state. The grammatical ambiguity between "in my self" (devotional) and "in the Self"
(universal) is preserved — both levels are operative in Trika.

**RELATED.** v1 (achieved-state), v7 (nondifference through hymn), v4 (the method that
produces pūrṇatā).
