## bh-p06 — C1

**SUMMARY.** True awakening discloses things as they are; bliss is entry into the Lord's fullness — ingress, not feeling.

*Rasa: ānanda (bliss) with adbhuta (wonder) — The sthāyibhāva is devotion (bhakti) in the mode of entry (praveśa); the vyabhicāri is bliss (ānanda) of awakening. The rasa is bliss as ingress, not as feeling — the devotee enters what the Lord already is.*
