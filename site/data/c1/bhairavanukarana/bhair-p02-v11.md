## bhair-p02-v11 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Light-bodied thought-power supreme goddess; hers the spreading of all. Bearing shown; world-opposed in me true-formed unopposed. Thread dissolved, sapless crowd; garland shown as-if to us. Look doubled at expelled nooses; gut-holding as-if appears speaking. Passionate cloth cast in highest heaven; bloodied hide head-borne told. Bared splitting by sword; world-taken heroes best. Fear-slayer lorded standing; shield-clash pointing us. Self-noosed, knowing-bodied, I count time; noose-held shown death's death. Difference-made hurled to unmake by own power; goad-held plain by supreme Bhairava. Bow-shown, lords arrayed; cause-deer pierced deer-like by power.

*Rasa: adbhuta of spreading goddess; vīra of unopposed-in-me; adbhuta of as-if showing; vīra of commanded seeing; adbhuta of cast cloth; vīra of best heroes; abhaya-vīra of fear-not; vīra of counted time; vīra of hurled distinction; vīra of deer-pierced cause — The sthāyibhāva is wonder: bodied, spread, led.
