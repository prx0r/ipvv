## bhair-p05-v41 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Cremation-girt shown measuring midst; Bhairava am I. Alone-nectar-knower; worlds'-life shining heartward. Sound-bodied eightfold-ward outer retinue, finest-split led. All sealed by total-transmission; throng gladdened by fullness-shown. Noose-net loosed, distinction melted; shape seals knowing-wealth: seal called. Conquered ground excellent, draught-become dwelling clearly; stain known by truth-showing. Each moment knowing rises flashing below; glory-formed world known by rite-standers. Thus flashed in me always; rite-to-mantra-end complete effortlessly.

*Rasa: raudra-vīra of ghoul-girt am-I; amṛta-bhakti of heart-shining; adbhuta of eightfold retinue; mudrā-vīra of sealed all; mudrā-vīra of called seal; vīra of conquering draught; adbhuta of momently rising; śānta of sealed rite — The sthāyibhāva is fierce exultation: girt, shown, am. Being relished. The sthāyibhāva is nectar-devotion: alone, life, heartward. Shining relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: bodied sound, circled eight, led. Retinue relished. The sthāyibhāva is seal-exultation: sealed all, gladdened throng. Sealing relished. The sthāyibhāva is seal-exultation: loosed, melted, sealed, called. Calling relished. The sthāyibhāva is exultation: conquering, dwelling, known. Conquering relished. The sthāyibhāva is wonder: rising momently, flashing below. Rising relished. The sthāyibhāva is peace: flashed in me, complete effortless. Sealing relished.*
