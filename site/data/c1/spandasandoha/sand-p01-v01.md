## sand-p01-v01 — C1

*Rasa: adbhuta (wonder at Śiva's spanda) with śānta (tranquility of self-established nature) — The sthāyibhāva is wonder (vismaya) at Śiva's unmeasured greatness (akalitamahimā), resolving into tranquility (śama) — the ocean unmoved by world-waves. Application: Abhinava does not explicitly name a rasa for this verse; the adbhuta/śānta reading is mine.*
