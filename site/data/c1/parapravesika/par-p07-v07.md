## par-p07-v07 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Pure bliss-nature throbbing with hundred-thousand reflections wills emission; will-first, knowing-first, doing-first dim by this-portion faintness; illusion spreads difference, stuffs art-to-earth, and veils by freedom's synonym into personhood — deemed-bound, wandering. The mask never touches the wearer (magician). Recognition restores sovereignty: dense-conscious, free-bodied, Śiva at fall. Five glories, uncontracted, accept contraction into five littles on robbed-king terms.

*Rasa: karuṇa-vīra of fall and return — The sthāyibhāva is exultation: the whole fall traversed without despair — mask, immunity, recognition, dealt-back powers. Return relished.*
