## par-p05-v05 — C1

**SUMMARY.** Indicators cannot touch the pre-proved; the knower's likeness exempts his standing; the two proof-questions answer themselves through self-feeling. Proof is appointed for illusion's traffic only; all else proves nothing, leaning on everything. Proof leans on Him; He, proof's life, has no use for it beyond body-sense-pleasure knowables. The quoted verse enthrones the further Life.

*Rasa: śānta-vīra of proof retired — The sthāyibhāva is steadfastness: pre-proved standing, self-answering, demotion without rancor, enthronement. Assurance relished.*
