Thompson's model and modern physics share only *structure* (extra dimensions,
boundedness). They do NOT share:
- the *reason* for extra dimensions (string theory: mathematical consistency;

  Thompson's inhabited higher realms are not);

Therefore the correspondence is **C**, and — crucially — it does not go the
other direction either: modern physics does NOT confirm the Vedic/Bhagavata
inhabited-worlds claim. A C is always a C.

# CLAIM: Thompson's critique of the expanding-universe / Big Bang model and of Newtonian gravitation at galactic scale.

STATUS: E for the scientific criticisms that are real; the Vedic alternative is H.
LABEL: mixed — established (some criticisms are real science) + speculative (the Vedic replacement).

SOURCE:
  Text: Thompson, ch. 7 (red shifts, Hubble's constant, quantized red shifts, Tifft/Arp); ch. 6b (gravitation)

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Thompson surveys genuinely-published anomalies (Arp's discordant red
  shifts, Tifft's quantized red shifts, the non-Newtonian gravity evidence,
  the 'missing mass'/dark matter problem) as evidence that the standard
  cosmological model is incomplete.

MAPPING (the honest split):
  - **Established:** Thompson is reporting real, published scientific
    controversies (Arp, Tifft, Vigier, dark-matter problems). These are
    genuine open questions in astrophysics — the data are real.
  - **Interpretive/speculative:** his inference that these anomalies
    support the Vedic/Bhagavata model is *not* established. Anomalies in
    standard cosmology do not confirm a Vedic cosmology; they only show
    the standard model is incomplete. The leap from 'standard model has
    problems' to 'therefore the Vedas are right' is a non-sequitur that
    the ochema METHOD explicitly forbids.

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — the anomalies are real and worth tracking. But the honest frame is:
  'standard cosmology has open problems (established)' + 'a Vedic model
  provides an alternative explanation (speculative, not confirmed by the
  anomalies).' The anomalies neither confirm nor refute the Vedic model.

# CONCLUSION (the honest verdict)

Thompson's book is valuable to the ochema project in three ways:
1. **As a scholarly Vedic-cosmology source** (the inhabited-lokas model, the
   higher-dimensional reading) — a real T-source for the Bhagavata view.
2. **As a genuine skeptic's survey** of open problems in modern cosmology
   (Arp, Tifft, dark matter) — these are real (E-status).
3. **As a cautionary case** for the ochema METHOD: it shows the temptation to
   leap from 'science is incomplete' to 'my tradition is confirmed.' That
   leap is forbidden. The Vedic model is a coherent alternative (C/H), not
   a confirmed one.

The highest-value contribution to the unified framework: Thompson gives the
**clearest statement of 'dimensionality as a function of consciousness'** —
a consciousness-fundamental hypothesis that converges with Abhinavagupta
(kañcukas) and Ra (density). That three-way convergence is the genuine
research lead. Everything else stays labelled C/H/speculative.
