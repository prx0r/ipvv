# CLAIM: The Bhagavata's 14 inhabited lokas (Thompson) and the Law of One's density-octave (Ra) both model the cosmos as a graded hierarchy of inhabited worlds through which consciousness evolves.

STATUS: C (correspondence) — both are hierarchical-cosmology frameworks.
LABEL: **SPECULATIVE** (the Law of One is channeled material; per ochema METHOD this is a speculative layer, and a C never upgrades to E/T by accumulation).

SOURCE:
  Text: Thompson, Vedic Cosmography (SB 5); the Ra/Law of One RO (ro-law-of-one, sessions 13, 27, 40)
  Translation: Prabhupāda; Ra transcripts (1981–1984)

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Both systems order reality as a vertical, graded hierarchy of inhabited
  worlds; both hold that a being's place is determined by its consciousness/
  karma; both treat "ascent" as the movement of consciousness, not spatial
  travel.

MAPPING (the correspondence):

| Dimension | Thompson / Bhagavata | Law of One (Ra) | Status |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|--------|
| The gradient | 14 lokas: 7 upper, 7 lower (SB 5.24) | 8 densities in a great octave (1st mineral → 8th, which loops to 1st of next octave) | C — both are octave/ladder cosmologies |
| Beings | Demigods, Siddhas, Gandharvas, Nāgas, Dānavas, Yakṣas | Density-4+ beings, the Confederation, the Council of Saturn (in the octave) | C — both have advanced beings |
| Access to higher realms | Higher-dimensional; requires sensory/karmic qualification | Requires vibration/density of consciousness; 'higher density' beings can reach lower | C — both: access depends on consciousness |
| Consciousness | A conditioned soul in a graded realm, per karma | A mind/body/spirit complex evolving through densities via free will | C — shared evolutionary-soul frame |
| The 'veil' | The boundary between ordinary senses and higher-dimensional realms (Thompson) | The veil between 3rd and 4th density (the loss of direct memory of previous lives) | C — both posit a cognitive barrier |
| The ceiling | Liberation = transcendence of the material universe (to the spiritual world) | The octave's absorption into intelligent infinity, then potentiated anew | C — both have a final unity, then a return |

THE HONEST DIVERGENCES (NOT to be conflated):

1. **Source authority.** Thompson writes from within the Vaiṣṇava/Bhagavata
   tradition (T-status for SB 5 claims). The Law of One is channeled (no
   textual-verification status; H/C at most). Per METHOD, Ra claims cannot
   inherit T from the Bhagavata, nor can SB be 'confirmed' by Ra.
2. **The ontology of the worlds.** Bhagavata = 7+7 discrete worlds, part of
   one material universe within the brahmāṇḍa. Ra = a musical octave where
   density is a *quality of consciousness*, more like TĀ's tattva-model than
   like SB's geography. Ra's density is closer to Abhinavagupta's interior
   model than to Thompson's spatial model.
3. **Free will / karma.** Ra makes 'free will' (the first distortion) the
   generative principle; the Bhagavata centers *karma* and *bhakti*. Both
   allow for choice, but the framing differs (ontological principle vs
   moral/causal law).

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — and this is where it gets interesting for a unified framework.
  The most striking convergence: **both posit that 'access to higher realms
  depends on the state of consciousness,' which is exactly Thompson's
  'dimensionality as degree of access' AND Abhinavagupta's kañcuka-model.**
  Three independent frameworks (SB/Thompson, Ra, TĀ) converge on one
  structural claim: *the world you can access is determined by the
  consciousness you are.* That three-way convergence is significant —
  but per the inheritance rule it stays a C/H, never a proof.

# CLAIM: The Ra "intelligent infinity → intelligent energy" model and Thompson's "higher-dimensional reality relative to consciousness" share a root claim: reality is consciousness-relative, not matter-relative.

STATUS: C (correspondence) at the level of structure.
LABEL: **SPECULATIVE** (both are metaphysical; Ra is channeled).

SOURCE:
  Text: Ra, Session 27 ('intelligent infinity is a potential; tapping it yields intelligent energy'); Thompson, chs. 2–3
  Translation: Ra transcripts; Thompson

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Ra: unity (intelligent infinity) is a *potential*; free will 'taps' it
  into intelligent energy, which forms all creation.
  Thompson: the universe is not one fixed 3D matter-stuff; it is
  dimensional relative to the consciousness of the observer.

MAPPING:
  Both make **consciousness (or a conscious potential) prior to matter.**
  - Ra: intelligent infinity (potential) → intelligent energy (work) → matter.
  - Thompson: space/matter are relative to the observer's consciousness
    (dimensionality = access).
  This aligns with the ochema source-metaphysics work (the
  'consciousness-fundamental' family) and with Doyle's information
  philosophy already in the density files (information is causally primary).

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — this is the strongest cross-framework bridge. Ra's
  'consciousness-first' + Thompson's 'dimensionality-relative-to-consciousness'
  + Abhinavagupta's 'consciousness is intrinsically reflexive (prakāśa-vimarśa)'
  all point to a **consciousness-fundamental** metaphysics. But:
  - This is the *speculative* layer (ontological, unproven).
  - It does not make any single tradition 'right.'
  - The correspondence is real; the proof is not.

# CONCLUSION (honest verdict)

The Bhagavata/Thompson model and the Law of One/density material share a
**consciousness-fundamental, graded, inhabited-cosmos** grammar, and both
reject the materialist-interstellar account of other beings. There is a
genuine three-way convergence (with Abhinavagupta) on the core claim:

> *The world you can access is determined by the consciousness you are.*

Per the ochema METHOD this is a **C (correspondence)** at best, and the
channeled/density layer is **speculative** — never elevated to empirical or
textual status. The convergence is a productive *research direction*, not a
proof. It is most valuable as a *generative hypothesis* for the ochema
consciousness-fundamental framework, not as a confirmation of any tradition.
