# RATIÉ CH. 6 — THE MIRROR, THE DREAMER, AND THE YOGIN
*Per-chapter analysis for the thesis. Source lines 17193–22105. The refutation of
Sautrāntika externalism + Vijñānavāda idealism. THE decisive claim: consciousness CREATES
(like a yogin), not merely REVEALS (like a lamp/mirror).*

---

## 1. THE CHAPTER'S THESIS
Both rivals fail: the Sautrāntika cannot establish the external object (perception or
inference); the Vijñānavāda cannot ground diversity in vāsanās. **The only remaining model
is the yogin's creation: consciousness produces its objects freely, by its own will,
without material cause.** This freedom is established by self-awareness (svasamvedana),
not mere hypothesis.

## 2. EXTENDED QUOTES (French verbatim + English translation)

### Q1 — The goal: freedom as the cause of diversity (lines 17239–17246)
> "La cause de la diversité phénoménale n'est rien d'autre que cette 'souveraineté'
> (aiśvarya) de la conscience – autrement dit, elle n'est rien d'autre que sa liberté
> (svātantrya) de se manifester comme diverse tout en demeurant une."
> **"The cause of phenomenal diversity is nothing other than this 'sovereignty' (aiśvarya)
> of consciousness — in other words, nothing other than its freedom (svātantrya) to
> manifest itself as diverse while remaining one."**

### Q2 — The Sautrāntika mirror model (lines 17596–17603)
> "C'est au modèle du miroir que le sautrāntika recourt : de même que la diversité des
> aspects du miroir n'a pas sa cause dans le miroir… mais dans la diversité des objets
> externes qu'il reflète."
> **"It is to the model of the mirror that the Sautrāntika resorts: just as the diversity
> of the mirror's aspects has its cause not in the mirror… but in the diversity of the
> external objects it reflects."**

### Q3 — The yogin kārikā (ĪPK 1.5.7, lines 18829–18834)
> "cidātmaiva hi devo 'ntaḥsthitam icchāvaśād bahiḥ / yogīva nirupādānam arthajātam
> prakāśayet // Car tel un yogin, le Seigneur… manifeste extérieurement l'ensemble des
> objets qui [lui] sont intérieurs, en vertu de sa libre volonté (icchā), sans cause
> matérielle (nirupādāna)."
> **"For like a yogin, the Lord, who consists in nothing other than consciousness, manifests
> externally the whole set of objects that are internal [to him], by virtue of his free
> will, without any material cause."**

### Q4 — Two rival models: mirror vs yogin (lines 19719–19725)
> "ce sont deux modèles de la conscience qui s'affrontent – celui du miroir, et celui du
> yogin… la question n'est pas… de savoir si les yogin manifestent bel et bien les objets
> sans cause matérielle, mais si la conscience manifeste ses objets selon ce modèle."
> **"it is two models of consciousness that clash — that of the mirror, and that of the
> yogin… the question is not whether yogins truly manifest objects without a material
> cause, but whether consciousness manifests its objects according to this model."**

**→ THE THESIS.** Perception-as-construction: consciousness *creates*, not reveals. The
"lamp vs yogin" distinction.

### Q5 — The freedom is established by self-awareness, not hypothesis (lines 20447–20492)
> "la liberté ou la spontanéité de la conscience est un fait d'expérience – de l'expérience
> la plus immédiate, à savoir la simple conscience de soi (svasamvit)."
> **"the freedom or spontaneity of consciousness is a fact of experience — of the most
> immediate experience, namely simple self-awareness (svasamvit)."**

**→ THE FELT.** Freedom is *felt* (svasamvedana) — not merely hypothesized. This is the
felt-is-primary again.

### Q6 — The conclusion: only the yogin's creation remains (lines 22069–22084)
> "La relation de la conscience à ses objets n'est donc à comprendre ni sur le modèle du
> miroir, qui reflète passivement un donné extérieur à lui, ni sur le modèle du rêveur…
> Le seul modèle encore en lice est donc celui de la création du yogin."
> **"The relation of consciousness to its objects is therefore to be understood neither on
> the model of the mirror, which passively reflects a given external to it, nor on the
> model of the dreamer… The only model still in the running is therefore that of the
> yogin's creation."**

## 4. THESIS LITERATURE-REVIEW VALUE
- **Perception is construction** (§10 of the paper): consciousness creates (yogin) rather
  than reveals (lamp/mirror) — the strongest statement of post-recognition re-rendering.
- **The felt of freedom** (Q5): freedom is *felt* in self-awareness (svasamvit) — pairs
  with Solms' felt-is-primary and active inference (perception as constructive
  hypothesis-testing).
- **The refutation of passivity** (Q6): consciousness is not a passive mirror — the paper's
  account of why perception re-renders.

## 5. IPVV TRANSLATION HOOKS
- **svātantrya / aiśvarya** (freedom / sovereignty) — the cause of diversity.
- **icchā** (will) — the yogin's creative will.
- **nirupādāna** (without material cause) — creation's essence.
- **vāsanā / saṃskāra** (disposition) — the Buddhist rival.
- **pratibimba / bimba** (reflection / source) — the mirror model.
- **dṛṣṭānta** (example) — the yogin as analogy, not proof.
