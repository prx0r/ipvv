# RATIÉ CH. 7 — THE HEART OF PRATYABHIJÑĀ IDEALISM: FREEDOM (SVĀTANTRYA) ⭐
*Per-chapter analysis for the thesis. Source lines 22105–26274. THE LOAD-BEARING CHAPTER:
freedom is the essence of consciousness; vimarśa = freedom + felicity + wonder (camatkāra);
identity = freedom to become Other; alienation = misrecognition of freedom.*

---

## 1. THE CHAPTER'S THESIS
The essence of consciousness is **freedom (svātantrya)**. This freedom is the
self-apprehension (vimarśa) by which consciousness becomes Other without ceasing to be
itself — and it is intrinsically **felicitous** (ānanda) and **wondrous** (camatkāra).
Alienation is the misrecognition of this freedom.

## 2. EXTENDED QUOTES (French verbatim + English translation)

### Q1 — Freedom is the essence of consciousness (lines 22931–22932)
> "ils mettent en évidence le fondement même de l'idéalisme de la Pratyabhijñā – à savoir
> l'affirmation que l'essence de la conscience est liberté."
> **"they bring to light the very foundation of the Pratyabhijñā's idealism — namely the
> affirmation that the essence of consciousness is freedom."**

### Q2 — vimarśa = the essence of manifestation (ĪPK 1.5.11, lines 23295–23301)
> "prakāśasya mukhya ātmā pratyavamarśaḥ… Sans elle, cette manifestation… n'aurait que la
> limpidité [d'un miroir], mais pas la spontanéité… parce qu'elle ne comporterait pas
> d'émerveillement (camatkṛti)."
> **"Apperception is the fundamental essence of conscious manifestation. Without it, this
> manifestation… would have only the limpidity [of a mirror], but not spontaneity, because
> it would not involve wonder (camatkṛti)."**

**→ THE THESIS NODE.** The essence of consciousness is vimarśa, and vimarśa is *felt*
(camatkṛti). Without the felt, consciousness would be inert crystal.

### Q3 — ⭐ THE CAMATKĀRA DEFINITION (lines 23332–23338) — THE THESIS PASSAGE
> "Les termes sanskrits camatkṛti et camatkāra désignent un plaisir particulier, une variété
> de 'félicité' (ānanda) qui implique un choc esthétique : c'est la divine surprise que l'on
> éprouve devant le beau, l'émerveillement dans lequel on s'étonne moins de l'objet
> lui-même que de la satisfaction qu'on se découvre éprouver à son égard – une satisfaction
> que l'on n'attendait pas."
> **"The Sanskrit terms camatkṛti and camatkāra designate a particular pleasure, a variety
> of 'beatitude' (ānanda) that implies an aesthetic shock: it is the divine surprise one
> feels before the beautiful, the wonder in which one is astonished less at the object
> itself than at the satisfaction one discovers oneself feeling toward it — a satisfaction
> one did not expect."**

**→ THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT QUOTE FOR THE PAPER.** "The satisfaction one discovers
oneself feeling" = recognition's felt register = camatkāra = Solms' felt-is-primary. This
is the verbatim citation to pair with report7's pleasure-at-certainty.

### Q4 — ānanda = manifestation of one's full nature (lines 23370–23375)
> "ce qu'on appelle 'félicité' (ānanda), c'est la manifestation de sa propre forme, de
> soi-même, de sa propre nature qui est pleine."
> **"what is called 'felicity' (ānanda) is the manifestation of one's own form, of oneself,
> of one's own nature which is full."**

### Q5 — The felt is plenitude, not lack (lines 23383–23409)
> "ce désir n'est pas le résultat d'un manque fondamental… cette variété sourd d'un désir
> créateur qui n'a d'autre origine qu'une plénitude, un trop-plein… la liberté de la
> conscience est conscience d'être liberté – c'est-à-dire complète ou pleine."
> **"this desire is not the result of a fundamental lack… this variety springs from a
> creative desire whose only origin is a plenitude, an overflowing… the freedom of
> consciousness is consciousness of being free — that is, complete or full."**

**→ THE FELT IS POSITIVE.** The felt (ānanda) is plenitude, not lack-reduction. (Nuance
for the Solms pairing: Solms' pleasure is uncertainty-reduction; here it is also
plenitude-overflow.)

### Q6 — vimarśa = paramānanda = nirvṛti = camatkāra (lines 23717–23731)
> "la prise de conscience… de la liberté même… [c'est ce qu']on appelle 'félicité suprême'
> (paramānanda), 'satisfaction complète' (nirvṛti), 'émerveillement' (camatkāra)."
> **"apperception… of that very freedom… this is what is called 'supreme beatitude'
> (paramānanda), 'complete satisfaction' (nirvṛti), 'wonder' (camatkāra)."**

**→ THE CONVERGENCE NODE.** vimarśa = freedom = felicity = wonder — one reality under
different names. This is the felt-is-primary stated in four registers.

### Q7 — Knowledge and action converge in vimarśa (lines 24443–24454)
> "toute connaissance est une forme d'action… seule la conscience agit… c'est vimarśa… qui
> constitue le véritable point de convergence entre les pouvoirs de connaissance et
> d'action."
> **"all knowledge is a form of action… only consciousness acts… it is vimarśa… that
> constitutes the true point of convergence between the powers of knowledge and action."**

### Q8 — The freedom to become Other (lines 25016–25050)
> "vimarśa… a une nature telle qu'elle transforme en Soi ce qui est pourtant Autre, et
> qu'elle transforme en Autre le Soi… être conscient, ce n'est pas seulement être soi, ni
> seulement devenir autre : c'est se transformer en l'Autre."
> **"apperception (vimarśa)… has a nature such that it transforms into Self what is yet
> Other, and transforms into Other the Self… to be conscious is not merely to be oneself,
> nor merely to become other: it is to transform oneself into the Other."**

**→ THE IDENTITY-THROUGH-FREEDOM THESIS.** The felt (vimarśa) is the free movement between
self and other.

### Q9 — Alienation is the misrecognition of freedom (lines 26239–26243)
> "l'aliénation [est] un faux problème, ou un problème mal posé… dont le véritable contenu
> est la méconnaissance de la liberté."
> **"alienation is a pseudo-problem, or a badly-posed problem… whose true content is the
> misrecognition of freedom."**

**→ THE THESIS.** Suffering = misrecognition; the felt is the mis-recognized freedom.
