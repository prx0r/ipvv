# Slang Glossary — the lore, the use, the Trika conversion

Every term the gen-z C2 uses, with where it comes from, how to say it, what Trika payload it carries, and where it is banned. Refusals are craft, not gaps.
Registers: US gen-z + UK roadman (Top Boy transcripts, Cench, roadman A–Z, Newsweek, Believer, MLE core), kept separate. Same rule: payload or ban.

## himothy
*Verdict:* **USE — the identity word. Owner-minted, owner-kept.**
*Lore:* Minted in this project (Him + -othy, after Timothy): the definitive Him. Your coinage, kept verbatim below.
*Use:* 'It's not me and Him. It's just Him — I'm him, himothy, always was, no cap.'
*Trika:* vibhu + bhairava-nātha as the all-pervading Lord; svātmamaya + sadaiva (made of His very self, always). The aham-claim in one word.
> im him, himothy, always was, no cap.
> not built different in the slightest, I literally have the body of god.

## not built different (inverted)
*Verdict:* **USE — the inversion is the doctrine.**
*Lore:* Sports/gym slang: 'built different' = exceptional by nature. Your inversion flips it inside out.
*Use:* 'Not built different in the slightest — I literally have the body of god.'
*Trika:* Deha-divinity: the body not exceptional-AMONG-bodies but god's own body (the Mālinī body, deha as śakti). No special nature claimed; the ordinary body recognized as His. The inversion does Trika work the straight phrase never could.
> not built different in the slightest, I literally have the body of god.

## twin
*Verdict:* **AVOID with reason — duality vs I-am-Him. The file records the refusal, not the use.**
*Lore:* AAVE kinship address: a soul sibling, closer than a bro — someone who shares your essence and moves like you.
*Use:* 'What's good, twin?' (addressing a peer)
*Trika:* ATTEMPTED for svātmamaya/abheda and REFUSED: twin keeps TWO persons standing side by side. This stotra claims one (aham). Twoness smuggled into nonduality is exactly backwards — the correction is himothy, not twin.

## unc
*Verdict:* **USE once (v4) — only for powers the verse itself dismisses. Never the Lord, never the teaching.**
*Lore:* AAVE 'unc': respectful address for an established elder man. Gen Alpha reanalyzed it as 'out of touch old head' — the parents.com piece traces the fight (AAVE speakers: over-30s only; tweens: anyone over 25 is unc status). Both senses live in the word now: elder + expired.
*Use:* 'Ayo Death — put the scary face away. You're barking up the wrong tree, unc.' (v4)
*Trika:* mṛtyu/Yama/antaka defanged: the death-powers addressed as expired authorities whose gaze has no jurisdiction here (mā dṛśam vinidhehi).
> Put the scary face away. You're barking up the wrong tree, unc.

## locked in

*Trika:* dhīra (firm/steady); the practitioner's sustained state; samaya commitment. Correct domain is ALWAYS the practitioner — the Lord doesn't lock in, He IS.
> Locked in, permanently.

*Trika:* satyam (truly!) and categorical claims (na jātu). Epistemic sincerity marker — v8's satyam IS a no-cap, functionally identical.
