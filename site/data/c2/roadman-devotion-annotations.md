# Roadman devotion: annotated examples for Trika C2

Wake-up calls, street prayers, and gospel grime — each with what it proves for our work.
Sources banked in `/tmp/opencode/genz/`; glossary payloads in `slang-glossary.json`.

## 1. Stormzy, Gang Signs & Prayer + "Blinded By Your Grace" (2017, UK No. 1)

**What it is:** A grime album in Last-Supper pastiche with a full gospel-choir anthem about grace.
"You came and saved me. I said a prayer this morning; I prayed I would find the way."
MNEK crooning *"Wagwaaaan, you coool?"* into the mic. Depression named plainly
("dark clouds of energy"), humor as doctrine-carrier ("Shut Up" got him here).

**Trika annotation:** The existence proof that street register + sincere devotion scales
to No. 1 — not as parody, as confession. Stormzy's move (grime cadence, gospel content)
is structurally identical to our C2: the voice is road, the claim is devotion, neither
apologizes. His "moist" rule ("if man sees you busting jokes on the corner, you're moist")
is itself a glossary-grade payload: softness-as-exposure, the exact inverse of v3's steel.
Note for v3 work: Stormzy *admits* the dark clouds first — concession before testimony,
same structure as bh-p03 (satsvapi: suffering present, flinch refused).

## 2. XXXTentacion, "Prayer (Skit)" (2017)

**What it is:** 2:32 of unaccompanied street prayer on a hardcore rap album:
"I come to you hungry and tired… Lord why is that I go through so much pain?…
give me pain 'til I die! But please Lord, treat em right."

**Trika annotation:** The anātha (helpless one) speaking straight: hunger first, doctrine
never. "Come as you are" is Matthew 11:28, which our Baeble file renders "come to Me,
everyone who's exhausted" — same verse, three registers (KJV, Baeble, X), all true.
For C2: hunger is a rasa with a fixed payload (v1's anāthaśaraṇya). X proves hunger
needs no translation — only a microphone. "Treat em right" is namas-level: the entire
petition in four words.

## 3. Rebillet, "Get the fuck out of bed" (2024) + Uth Ja Re Bhai (Hindi alarm)

**What they are:** The secular wake-up call (Rebillet: profane imperative, "GET UP,
bitch") and its desi twin ("O aalsi maharaj — O lazy KING — leave the cot!").
Same genre as our MANDEM piece: loving aggression at dawn.

**Trika annotation:** The prabodhana (awakening-call) is a genre, not a joke — Vedic
suprabhātam wakes the *Lord*; Rebillet/Uth-Ja wake the *devotee*; ours does both at
once (God is calling AND mandem must rise). "O lazy king" is unc-energy done right:
status mocked, sleeper loved. Our "allow the lie-in" sits in this exact lineage.

## 4. The Believer Grim Glossary (2011) — duppy, nang, par, boyed, wasteman

**What it is:** The earliest print codification of grime slang. Key entries:
- **duppy**: Caribbean ghost → in grime, to hurt/kill *lyrically or literally*.
- **nang**: excellent (Hackney legend: named for a Thai schoolgirl in the late '90s).
- **par**: anything unfortunate — the antonym of "a lot" (which means *good*).
- **boyed**: belittled, embarrassed. **wasteman**: untermensch, complete contempt.

**Trika annotation:** Duppy's double meaning (ghost → kill-by-verse) is the closest
English model for how mantra works: the word that *does* what it says. Nang enters
the bliss ladder as intensity-via-Hackney (behind fire). Par/a-lot gives us a
polarity pair the glossary lacked: praise and blame as single syllables.
Wasteman's "untermensch" severity confirms our AVOID verdict — contempt is a
magnitude, not a flavor, and the stotra never reaches for it.

## 5. The God Squad (Evening Standard, 2018) — Novelist, Lady Leshurr, Desperado

**What it is:** Documented wave of grime gospel: Novelist ("God sent me on the Earth
with a plan"), Lady Leshurr ("gonna do this fast, Ramadan"), Desperado ("Some mans
turn to Quran, some mans turn the Bible" — humour as ecumenism).

**Trika annotation:** "Turn to" is conversion vocabulary happening live in-verse —
the lexicon recording redemption arcs as they occur (cf. Brunel 'on road' glossary:
*ackee = a former roadman converted to Islam*). For C2: transformation language
already lives inside roadman; we don't import it, we point at it. Desperado's
couplet is also the pluralism precedent: multiple scriptures, one cadence — the
same move as our multi-faith field file (Japji, Magnificat, Bible).

## 6. President T's morning routine + Dizzee's Slang 101 (2008)

**What they are:** Grime morning content (r/grime's favourite) and the OG decoder
(Dizzee teaching America roadman, 2008).

**Trika annotation:** Morning + roadman + instruction is an established triple —
routine as teaching, teaching as routine. Dizzee 2008 → Cench 2023 → our C2:
a straight lineage of street-to-world translation. When anyone asks why sacred
verse belongs in roadman, the answer is fifteen years of precedent: the ends have
been translating themselves for the world since Dizzee held the seminar.

## 7. Stormzy, "Blinded By Your Grace" Pts. 1–2 — bar-by-bar for C2

Full lyrics banked in `/tmp/opencode/genz/stormzy-blinded.json`. The annotated bars:

- **"I stay prayed up then I get the job done"** — method → result in one line.
  This is bh-p04's sevanā→śakti logic with the serial numbers filed off. Prayer as
  practice, outcome as consequence. Use as the proof that roadman already speaks
  instrumental devotion (practice-*works*), not just praise.
- **"Yeah I'm Abigail's yout, but I'm God's son"** — dual identity resolved upward.
  Mum's boy vs God's son is *structurally identical* to our v2 move (human self vs
  made-of-Him self). Stormzy did the aham-claim on a No. 1 album: not twoness,
  sonship. Cite whenever anyone flinches at "I'm Him."
- **"It's not by blood and it's not by birth"** — grace-not-heredity. Direct sibling
  to v9's samaya-not-merit: access by covenant, not by resume. Same doctrine,
  different scripture.
- **"I can't escape you / I can't replace you"** (Pt. 1 coda) — inescapable presence
  as *experienced* pervasion. This is vyāpta from the inside: not "He is everywhere"
  (doctrine) but "I cannot get away" (testimony). Strongest v1-adjacent line in the
  mainstream catalogue — pervasion as pursuit, not geography.
- **"I said a prayer this morning… I was so afraid / 'Til you came and saved"** —
  fear → rescue arc in four lines. Sibling to v5 (fearlessness), different mechanism:
  here rescue removes fear; in v5, salutation alone suffices. The pair teaches the
  difference between deliverance devotion and recognition devotion.
- **"This is God's plan, they can never stop this"** — na jātu energy (categorical,
  unblockable). The never-claim with a roadblock metaphor built in.
