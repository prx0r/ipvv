## 1. genz-bible.com — the full-chapter proof (Matthew 6, KJV beside)

**What it is:** Whole Bible, chapter by chapter, Gen Z paraphrase beside KJV. The only project here that sustains the voice past one-liners — 34 verses of Matthew 6, not just highlight clips.

**Killer lines (all Matthew 6):**
- "don't flex your good deeds in front of people to get clout" (hypocrisy as clout-chasing — exact)
- "don't blast a trumpet like the fake peeps" (hypocrites → fakes, no doctrine lost)
- "keep it lowkey" (secrecy as discretion, not shame)
- "hit up your closet, shut the door" (prayer as direct message)
- "your name is lit" / "let your will pop off" (hallowed be thy name / thy will be done — praise as hype, precise)
- "Hook us up with our daily bread, fr" (petition + sincerity marker doing real work)
- "save us from the evil vibes" (evil as ambient field — genuinely good metaphysics)
- "Where your treasure is, that's where your heart's vibin', no cap" (locus claim intact)
- "if your eye's sus, your whole body is in the dark... that's a major red flag" (diagnostic slang carrying a diagnostic verse)
- "vibe with one and ghost the other" (two masters — ghosting nails the asymmetry)
- "Peep the lilies... even Solomon in all his drip wasn't dressed as fly" (drip for glory — taste-domain, exact)
- "Don't worry about tomorrow; it's got its own drama" (anxiety as plot — the verse's point survives)

**What works:** stamina. The voice doesn't collapse at verse 20. Lowkey/no-cap/fr do structural work (secrecy, sincerity, totality), not decoration.
**What fails:** nothing gated — no visible fidelity discipline; quality rests on the paraphraser's ear alone.
**Trika relation:** proof that sustained register holds across a chapter. Our bhairavastava v2 (10 verses) is the same bet, different stakes — and ours carries the gate + packet behind it, which this project never shows.

## 2. Magnificat: Gen Z Spill the T (Wabash Center, Dayton scholars)

**What it is:** Luke 1:46–55 rendered by a scholar team over months, devotional framing (tea party service, LGBTQ+ assembly), Espanglish inflect. The closest thing to our register with institutional weight behind it.

**Killer lines:**
- "It's giving food that is bussin' to the starving; and ghosting the peeps who were already living large by leaving them hangry and mid" — they independently landed **bussin for nectar-food**, same port as our v7. Two teams, same mapping: the taste-domain is IN the text (esurientes/fames), not imposed.
- "They totally canceled the powerful influencers and boosted up the SIMPS" (deposuit potentes — cancellation as deposition, exact function)
- "They flexed their arm – BIG YIKES for those opps" (divine power + dread in one breath)
- "God has got Abraham and his fam for all time — no cap!" (covenant certainty + sincerity marker)
- "High key, shoutout to the snatched chica who trusted the process" (beatitude as recognition)

**What works:** team methodology over solo slang-sticking; devotion and scholarship in the same room; process documented (shared doc → group revision).
**What fails:** Espanglish narrows the audience by design; no machine-readable packet behind it.
**Trika relation:** the team-process precedent for our pipeline (months of revision ≙ our floors + review discipline). And the bussin convergence is evidence-grade: independent arrival at the same mapping means the mapping is discovered, not invented — like two recensions agreeing.

## 3. Japji Sahib poetic translation (Nanak Naam, 8 years, verse by verse)

**What it is:** Complete Japji Sahib in rhyming modern English. Not slang — poetry. Eight years of recrafting. Fundraiser states the goal outright: *"moved from recitation to understanding, reflection and personal transformation."*

**What works:** the mission sentence IS our C2 transmission criterion, stated better than we stated it. Devotion-first, beauty as the vehicle, patience as method (8 years vs our weeks — humility note).
**What fails (for us):** rhyme forces padding; modern-poetic is a narrower voice than any-voice.
**Trika relation:** the category proof — devotion-first translation across a faith boundary (Sikh, not Shaiva) with identical structure: recited daily, half-understood, unlocked by beauty. C2's rasa test ("does the reader get the savor") is their mission sentence operationalized. Different faith, identical category.

## 4. Baeble Gen Z mode — the labeling precedent

**What it is:** Toggleable AI-assisted paraphrase beside KJV/NIV/ESV/NLT; every rendering labeled "Gen Z paraphrase (AI-assisted), not a translation."

**Killer lines:** "gets eternal life for real" (John 3:16 — 'for real' doing the amen-work); "no cap" closing Philippians 4:13; "Don't spiral — I'm your God" (Isaiah 41:10 — anxiety slang meeting a fear-not verse, exact emotional payload); "peace, which doesn't even make sense" (Phil 4:6-7 — nonsense-peace kept paradoxical, not explained away); "Don't freeze in fear" (Joshua 1:9 — freeze as the physiology of fear, precise).

**What works:** the label. "Paraphrase (AI-assisted), not a translation" beside the canon is our "machine rendering" instinct, independently arrived at. Toggleable = reader sovereignty.
**What fails:** AI-assisted with no visible grounding packet; quality varies by verse.
**Trika relation:** the ethics precedent. Quote it whenever anyone asks why C2 sits next to L1/L2 labeled and never alone: the industry already learned this rule.

**What they are:** comedian plot-translations teachers use in classrooms ("an absolute snack," "beefing hard," "it's giving bruh, just check your messages"); anonymous TikTok gospel ("top G," "holy tea," "passed God's vibe check," "mother of the main character," "live rent-free," "bet," "left on read," "let the Holy Spirit cook").

**Trika relation:** appetite proof (millions of views = starving audience) stacked against the fidelity warning. The field's lesson in one line: voice without roots goes viral and rots; roots without voice sit unread. C2 is the graft.
