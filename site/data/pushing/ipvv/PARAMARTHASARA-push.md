## ROUND 1 — the hound

> **PENETRATION 1 — Q1 (unity/manifold):** v1 stacks paradox attributes: gahanād
> (depthless), anādim (beginningless), ekaṃ (one), niviṣṭaṃ bahudhā guhāsu (resident
> manifoldly in caves). The tension is textual, not imported: ekaṃ and bahudhā sit in
> the same line. The text's own candidate resolution is sarvālayaṃ (all-abode): the ONE
> is not *divided* into caves, it is the *house of* caves — manifold residence without
> partition. Claim status: STATED (both words in v1), ESTABLISHED (sarvālayam supplies
> the glue), requires unstated premise: that abode-relation ≠ partitive relation.

> **PENETRATION 2 — Q2 (answer before question):** The sequence refusal: refuge first
> (v1), question second (v2). Two rival readings — (a) devotional grammar: one arrives
> already sheltered, then understands why (refuge is the epistemic condition, not its
> result); (b) editorial: the opening verse is a frame (invocation), the "real" text
> begins at the question. Rival unresolved: the colophon/structure evidence lives in
> p12 (owner to hound the ending against the beginning).

> **PENETRATION 3 — Q3 (refuge from everywhere):** prapadye presupposes displacement
> toward; sarvālayam and sarvacarācarastham (dwelling in all moving-unmoving) say there
> is nowhere outside. If Śambhu is the all-abode, resorting to Him is not travel — the
> text's own gloss would be: the "resort" is the recognition that displacement was
> never possible. Unstated premise: prapada (refuge-feet) is a relation of
> acknowledgment, not locomotion. ESTABLISHED within the verse's own vocabulary,
> unstated-premise flagged.

> **PENETRATION 4 — Q4 (essence vs showing):** paramārtha-sāra promises the *sāra* —
> the pressed-essence. Yet the first move is an act (refusal), the second a story
> (student). An essence that opens with performance is claiming to *show* what it
> names. Status: STATED by the title; unresolved whether any verse in p01–p12
> *states* rather than *enacts* — owner's hound, branch B1.

**Claim statuses (this round):** Q1 stated+established (premise flagged) · Q2 rival
unresolved · Q3 established (premise flagged) · Q4 stated, unresolved.
