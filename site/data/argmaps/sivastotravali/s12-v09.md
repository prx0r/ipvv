## s12-v09 — argument map

Dissolved (parilīno) in Your foot-lake (bhavadaṅghrisaroruhodare), melted seeking (galitaparaiṣaṇaḥ), honey-drunk to excess (atimātramadhūpayogataḥ). May I roam (vicareyam) satisfied (paritṛpto), at will (icchayā). Dissolved, drunk, wandering at will: the devotee melted in His foot-lake.

### Open
- Whether 'paraiṣaṇa' means 'seeking' or 'desire' is unresolved.
- Whether 'madhūpayoga' means 'honey-drunk' or 'honey-use' is unresolved.
