## s12-v05 — argument map

Not then (na tadā), not always (na sadā), not once (na caikadā) — where no time-thought (yatra na kāladhīr) is: such is Your vision (bhavadīyadarśanaṃ), neither eternal (na ca nityaṃ) nor otherwise (na ca kathyate 'nyathā). Timeless vision: neither eternal nor otherwise.

### Open
- Whether 'kāladhī' means 'time-thought' or 'thought of time' is unresolved.
- Whether 'anyathā' means 'otherwise' or 'different' is unresolved.
