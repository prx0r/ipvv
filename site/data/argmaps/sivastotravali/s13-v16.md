## s13-v16 — argument map

Yo 'vikalpam idam arthamaṇḍalaṃ paśyatīśa nikhilaṃ bhavadvapuḥ: he who sees this whole world-circle as Your body, without alternative. Svātmapakṣaparipūrite jagaty asya nityasukhinaḥ kuto bhayam: in a world filled with self-nature, where is fear for the eternally happy? The verse: seeing all as Your body, no fear for the eternally happy.

### Open
- Whether 'avikalpam' means 'without alternative' or 'without doubt' is unresolved.
- Whether 'svātmapakṣa' means 'self-nature' or 'own side' is unresolved.
