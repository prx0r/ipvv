## s13-v20 — argument map

Tvām agādham avikalpam advayaṃ svaṃ svarūpam akhilārthaghasmaram: You, unfathomable, without alternative, non-dual, own form, devourer of all things. Āviśann aham umeśa sarvadā pūjayeyam abhisaṃstuvīya ca: entering, I will worship and praise always. The verse: entering You, the unfathomable non-dual, worship and praise always.

### Open
- Whether 'agādham' means 'unfathomable' or 'bottomless' is unresolved.
- Whether 'āviśann' means 'entering' or 'having entered' is unresolved.
