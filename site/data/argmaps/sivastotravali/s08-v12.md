## s08-v12 — argument map

Lord (parameśvara), in crashing hardships (teṣu teṣu kṛcchreṣv api), as names bend down (nāmopanamatsv) — may I be (ahaṃ bhaveyam), fear gone (na paraṃ gatabhīs) only while limb-joined (tvadaṅgasaṅgādupajātādhikasammado), exceeding-joy-mad ('pi yāvat). Practice for catastrophe: fear gone only while limb-joined, exceeding-joy-mad.

### Open
- Whether 'gatabhīs' means 'fear gone' or 'gone fear' is unresolved.
- Whether 'yāvat' means 'while' or 'as long as' is unresolved.
