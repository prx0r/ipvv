## s13-v19 — argument map

Īhitaṃ na bata pārameśvaraṃ śakyate gaṇayituṃ: the Lord's will cannot be counted. Tathā ca me dattam apy amṛtanirbharaṃ vapuḥ svaṃ na pātum anumanyate tathā: even the nectar-filled body given to me is not allowed to be drunk. The verse: the Lord's will is uncountable; even nectar given is not to be drunk.

### Open
- Whether 'īhitaṃ' means 'will' or 'desire' is unresolved.
- Whether 'anumanyate' means 'allowed' or 'permitted' is unresolved.
