## s13-v11 — argument map

Śaktipātasamaye vicāraṇaṃ prāptam īśa na karoṣi karhicit: at the time of grace-descent, You never investigate. Adya māṃ prati kim āgataṃ yataḥ svaprakāśanavidhau vilambase: what has come to me today that You delay in self-manifestation? The verse: grace-descent needs no investigation; why delay self-manifestation?

### Open
- Whether 'vicāraṇaṃ' means 'investigation' or 'deliberation' is unresolved.
- Whether 'vilambase' means 'delay' or 'linger' is unresolved.
