## s08-v06 — argument map

Light, soft, white-pure, cool (laghumasṛṇasitācchaśītalaṃ) — contemplating (bhāvayan) by possession-force (bhavadāveśavaśena) a body (vapur). May I exceed (ativartayeya) those dealings (vyavahārān) of the all-thing-road (akhilapadārthapaddhater). Possession-body exceeds transaction: the world is left, not refuted.

### Open
- Whether 'laghumasṛṇasitācchaśītalaṃ' describes the body or the contemplation is unresolved.
- Whether 'vyavahāra' means transaction or worldly dealing is unresolved.
