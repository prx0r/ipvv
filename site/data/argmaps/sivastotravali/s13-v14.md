## s13-v14 — argument map

Yatsamastasubhagārthavastuṣu sparśamātravidhinā camatkṛtim: touching beautiful things, wonder (camatkāra). Tāṃ samarpayati tena te vapuḥ pūjayanty acalabhaktiśālinaḥ: offering that wonder, the devotees worship Your body. The verse: touching beautiful things gives wonder; offering it, devotees worship Your body.

### Open
- Whether 'sparśamātravidhinā' means 'by mere touch' or 'by the method of touch' is unresolved.
- Whether 'camatkṛtim' means 'wonder' or 'relish' is unresolved.
