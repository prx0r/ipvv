## s01-v11 — argument map

*bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādād bodhasya syāt parāpi yā daśā.* Even the supreme state of knowledge tastes, to me, like vinegar-sourness after wine beside devotion-nectar. Knowledge is not denied; it is out-tasted. (Lakshmanjoo's "Have you ever tasted liquor?" — the verse demands experience, not assent.)
