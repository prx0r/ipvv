## s12-v28 — argument map

Things as things (bhāva) by His being (bhavatā): things are things by His being. The devotee sees things as things by His being. Things as things by His being: ontology becomes benediction.

### Open
- Whether 'bhāva' means 'thing' or 'being' is unresolved.
- Whether 'bhavatā' means 'by His being' or 'by His existence' is unresolved.
