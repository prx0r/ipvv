## s12-v10 — argument map

Whose resolve (yasya) to worship (bhavatpūjāsaṅkalpa) arose even from pretense (utthitaḥ tasyāpy avaśyam uditaṃ sannidhānaṃ tava) — his presence (sannidhānaṃ) is assured (avaśyam), fitting (ucitam). Even pretense suffices: the resolve to worship assures presence.

### Open
- Whether 'dambhād iva' means 'even from pretense' or 'as if from pretense' is unresolved.
- Whether 'ucitam' means 'fitting' or 'appropriate' is unresolved.
