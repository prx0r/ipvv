## s01-v13 — argument map

*āmulād vāglatā seyaṃ kramavisphāraśālinī.* The speech-creeper, unfolding in sequence from the root, watered by devotion-nectar — may it fruit for me. Speech itself grows and fruits; mantra is organic, not mechanical. (The four levels parā/paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī are Lakshmanjoo's gloss — NOT in the text; mark supplied in L2.)
