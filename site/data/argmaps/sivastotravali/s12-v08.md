## s12-v08 — argument map

Dwelling (nivasann) between Your feet (bhavanbhavadīyapādayor antara eva), fearless (nirbhayaḥ). In those and those world-grounds (bhavabhūmiṣu tāsu tāsv ahaṃ) may I worship (prabhum arceyam) the Lord, unbarred in act (anargalakriyaḥ). Fearless dwelling between His feet: worship unbarred.

### Open
- Whether 'antara eva' means 'between' or 'in the interior' is unresolved.
- Whether 'anargalakriyaḥ' means 'unbarred in act' or 'unimpeded in action' is unresolved.
