## s13-v05 — argument map

Devadeva bhavadadvayāmṛtākhyātisaṃharaṇalabdhajanmanā: O Lord of Lords, by the touch of oneness, senses nectarized. Tad yathāsthitapadārthasaṃvidā māṃ kuruṣva caraṇārcanocitam: make me worthy of worshipping Your feet. The verse asks for senses nectarized by oneness, so worship becomes possible.

### Open
- Whether 'labdhajanmanā' means 'born of' or 'attained through' is unresolved.
- Whether 'yathāsthitapadārtha' means 'things as they stand' or 'things as they are' is unresolved.
