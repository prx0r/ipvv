## s08-v05 — argument map

May these thrilled faculties (imāḥ karaṇavṛttaya ullasitā) fall into their own seats (nijanijeṣu padeṣu patantv). Never, Lord (kṣaṇamapīśa), even slightly (manāg api), the daring of non-difference-rupture (tvadavibhedarasakṣatisāhasam). One fear named: rupture of non-difference. The faculties self-seat; the daring is refused.

### Open
- Whether 'kṣaṇamapīśa' means 'never, Lord, even for a moment' or 'Lord, even for a moment' is unresolved.
- Whether 'tvadavibhedarasa' means 'the rasa of non-difference' or 'the non-difference-rasa' is unresolved.
