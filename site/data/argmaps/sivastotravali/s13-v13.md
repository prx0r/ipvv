## s13-v13 — argument map

Svāmisaudham abhisandhimātrato nirvibandham adhiruhya sarvadā: ascending the Lord's abode by mere resolve, without obstacle, always. Syāṃ prasāda paramāmṛtāsavāpānakeliparilabdhanirvṛtiḥ: may I attain the bliss of the nectar-drinking play of grace. The verse: ascend by mere resolve; attain the bliss of grace's nectar-drinking play.

### Open
- Whether 'abhisandhimātrato' means 'by mere resolve' or 'by resolve alone' is unresolved.
- Whether 'prasāda' means 'grace' or 'favor' is unresolved.
