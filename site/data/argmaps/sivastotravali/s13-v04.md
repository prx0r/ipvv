## s13-v04 — argument map

Īśvaro 'ham aham eva rūpavān paṇḍito 'smi subhago 'smi ko 'paraḥ: I am Īśvara, beautiful, learned, charming — who else is parallel? Matsamo 'sti jagatīti śobhate mānitā tvadanurāgiṇaḥ param: this ego shines beautifully only in those devoted to You. The verse presents the devotee's ego as appropriate only when devoted to You.

### Open
- Whether 'mānitā' means 'ego' or 'pride' is unresolved.
- Whether 'param' means 'supreme' or 'ultimately' is unresolved.
