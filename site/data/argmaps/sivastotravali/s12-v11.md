## s12-v11 — argument map

Lord (bhagavann), with one-taste mind (ekarasena cetasā) seeking nothing else (itarān apekṣiṇā nitarām) — the all-laying Lord (sakalopaśāyinaṃ prabhum), easy (sulabhaṃ) — may I drink (ātṛpti pibeyam) to fullness (ātṛpti): am I what (asmi kim)? Drinking to fullness: one-taste mind, nothing else sought.

### Open
- Whether 'ekarasena' means 'one-taste' or 'single-flavored' is unresolved.
- Whether 'asmi kim' means 'am I what?' or 'what am I?' is unresolved.
