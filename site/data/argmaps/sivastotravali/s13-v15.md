## s13-v15 — argument map

Sphārayasy akhilam ātmanā sphuran viśvam āmṛśasi rūpam āmṛśan: You spread all by Yourself, shining; You touch the form, touching. Yat svayaṃ nijarasena ghurṇase tat samullasati bhāvamaṇḍalam: what You whirl by Your own rasa, that world-circle dances. The verse: You spread all by Yourself; the world dances by Your rasa.

### Open
- Whether 'sphārayasy' means 'You spread' or 'You extend' is unresolved.
- Whether 'ghurṇase' means 'You whirl' or 'You stir' is unresolved.
