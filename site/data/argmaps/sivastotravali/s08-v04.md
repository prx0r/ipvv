## s08-v04 — argument map

In body-grounds (dehabhūmiṣu) and in mind (manasi), Lord (tvaṃ), and in breath's course (prāṇavartmani) where difference (bhedam) has come (upete) — and in those consciousness-paths (saṃvidaḥ pathiṣu teṣu ca) — by Yourself (tena svātmanā) be plain (sphuṭarūpaḥ) to me (mama). Plainness demanded in both registers: body-grounds and consciousness-paths.

### Open
- Whether 'sphuṭarūpaḥ' means 'plain' or 'manifest' is unresolved.
- Whether 'tena' refers to Yourself or to the path is unresolved.
