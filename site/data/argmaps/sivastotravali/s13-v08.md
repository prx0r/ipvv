## s13-v08 — argument map

Tattadindriyamukhena santataṃ yuṣmadarcanarasāyanāsavam: through each sense, the worship-rasa-nectar. Sarvabhāvacaṣakeṣu pūriteṣv āpibann api bhaveyam unmadaḥ: drinking, even, from filled cups of all things, may I be mad. The verse asks to drink worship-rasa through every sense, becoming mad with it.

### Open
- Whether 'rasāyanāsava' means 'worship-rasa-nectar' or 'rasa-nectar of worship' is unresolved.
- Whether 'unmadaḥ' means 'mad' or 'intoxicated' is unresolved.
