## s13-v18 — argument map

Tvadpralāpamayaraktagītikāni tya yuktavadanopaśobhitaḥ: adorned with speech full of Your prattle. Syām athāpi bhavadarcanakriyāpreyasīparigatāśayaḥ sadā: may I be one whose mind is always intent on the beloved act of Your worship. The verse: adorned with Your prattle, mind intent on worship.

### Open
- Whether 'pralāpamaya' means 'full of prattle' or 'full of Your speech' is unresolved.
- Whether 'preyasīparigatāśaya' means 'mind intent on the beloved' or 'mind pervaded by love' is unresolved.
