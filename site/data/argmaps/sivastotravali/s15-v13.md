## s15-v13 — argument map

Growth itself is the attainment (v.13): not a means to an end but the end itself. The devotee grows; growth is the attainment, not a step toward it. Growth-itself-as-attainment: the devotee does not seek exit but grows.

### Open

- Whether 'upacīyamānā' means 'growing' or 'accumulating' is unresolved.
