## s12-v25 — argument map

Mind roaming wherever, by its own rhythm, in its fields (manasi svarasena yatra tatra pracaraty). Yet spread out, unswerving, skilled in Thy service always (prasṛto 'py avilola eva yuṣmatparicaryācaturaḥ sadā bhaveyam).

### Open
- Whether 'manas' means 'mind' or 'thought' is unresolved.
- Whether 'sevā' means 'service' or 'worship' is unresolved.
- Swamiji (Academy, ch12 v25): don't control the swift mind — skill within roaming, not restraint of it.
