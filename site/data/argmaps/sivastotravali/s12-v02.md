## s12-v02 — argument map

From things (bhāvagaṇād), senses (indriyapracayād), awareness-middle (avabodhamadhyataḥ) — ever self-born (prabhavantam api svataḥ sadā). May I see Him (paripaśyeyam), world-veiled (apoḍhaviśvakam). The origin is self-born, not from things, senses, or awareness-middle.

### Open
- Whether 'avabodhamadhyataḥ' means 'from the middle of awareness' or 'from awareness's middle' is unresolved.
- Whether 'apoḍhaviśvakam' means 'world-veiled' or 'with world removed' is unresolved.
