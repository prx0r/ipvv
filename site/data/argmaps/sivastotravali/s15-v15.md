## s15-v15 — argument map

The buried gem (v.14): the devotee confesses the buried gem that will not spread. The worry over missing powers (v.15): the supreme-attainment claim with the powers-worry attached. The one place Utpala doubts aloud, and the doubt is kept inside it.

- Whether 'missing powers' means siddhis or abilities is unresolved.
