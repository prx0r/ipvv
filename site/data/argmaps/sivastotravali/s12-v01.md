## s12-v01 — argument map

No helper is wanted at all, no hinderer is seen in Your vision (sahakāri na kiñcid iṣyate; na pratibandhakaṃ dṛśi). Yet all is flooded by You alone (bhavataiva hi sarvam āplutaṃ). How is it even so that You do not see (katham adyāpi tathāpi nekṣase)?

### Open
- Whether 'sahakāri' means 'helper' or 'co-cause' is unresolved.
- Whether 'nekṣase' means 'You do not see' or 'You are not seen' is unresolved.
