## s01-v07 — argument map

*tvam evātmeśa sarvasya... iti svabhāvasiddhāṃs tvadbhaktiṃ jānañ jayej janaḥ.* THE CENTER. All desire the self; You alone are everyone's conscious self; hence devotion to You is self-established — and one who knows it **by immersion's means** (*samāveśayuktyā*, Kṣemarāja) conquers, i.e., lives supreme. The liṅ (*jayet*) is injunctive (Kṣemarāja: *niyoge liṅ*). Argument: since the object is the self, love of it needs no external ground.
