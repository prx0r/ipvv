## s01-v02 — argument map

*ātmā mama bhavadbhaktisudhāpānayuvāpi san.* The self stays young drinking devotion-nectar while the body greys with world-dust. Youth = the self's nature; age = the journey's dust. (Lakshmanjoo: internally young.)
