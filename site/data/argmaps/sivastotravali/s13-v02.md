## s13-v02 — argument map

Antar apy atitarām aṇīyasī yā tvadaprathanakālikāsti me: a minute impurity in the internal state, keeping me from perceiving You. Tām apīśa parimṛjya sarvataḥ: remove that impurity completely. Svaṃ svarūpam amalaṃ prakāśaya: reveal Your pure form. The verse asks for removal of the subtle impurity (āṇavamala) that blocks perception.

### Open
- Whether 'aṇīyasī' means 'minute' or 'subtle' is unresolved.
- Whether 'parimṛjya' means 'wiping away' or 'removing' is unresolved.
