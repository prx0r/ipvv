## s17-v35 — argument map

The world's dignity as worship-gear is theirs (pūjopakaraṇībhūtaviśvaveśena gauravam). Ah, something of devotees — and just lightness (aho kim api bhaktānāṃ kim apy eva ca lāghavam).

### Open
- Swamiji (Academy, ch17): worship no longer confined to ritual acts — the whole universe felt as the substance of worship itself; this verse is that doctrine in miniature.
