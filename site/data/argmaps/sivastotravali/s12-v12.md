## s12-v12 — argument map

By You denied (tvayā nirākṛtam): all where You're absent (sarvaṃ heyam etat) is to be left (heyam). Where it's You (tvanmayaṃ), to be taken (samupādeyam). This is the essence-extract (ity ayaṃ sārasaṃgrahaḥ): the one portable rule.

### Open
- Whether 'nirākṛtam' means 'denied by You' or 'rejected by You' is unresolved.
- Whether 'samupādeyam' means 'to be taken' or 'to be adopted' is unresolved.
