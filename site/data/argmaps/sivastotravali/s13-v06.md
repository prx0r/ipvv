## s13-v06 — argument map

Dhyāyate tad anu dṛśyate tataḥ spṛśyate ca parameśvaraḥ svayam: meditated, then seen, then touched — the Lord Himself. Yatra pūjanamahotsavaḥ sa me sarvadāstu bhavato 'nubhāvataḥ: where worship-festival is, let it always be mine by Your grace. The verse sequences meditation → vision → touch → festival.

### Open
- Whether 'dhyāyate' is passive or active is unresolved.
- Whether 'anubhāvataḥ' means 'by grace' or 'by experience' is unresolved.
