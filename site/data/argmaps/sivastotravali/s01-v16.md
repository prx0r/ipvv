## s01-v16 — argument map

*kadācit kvāpi labhyo 'si yogenetīśa vañcanā.* Attained sometime, somewhere, by yoga (thought-constriction, e.g., in the heart-wheel — Kṣemarāja) — this is **the deceit**. Proof: otherwise how do You shine in ALL states, including post-samādhi emergence, for devotees? The polemic verse: localization refutes itself.
