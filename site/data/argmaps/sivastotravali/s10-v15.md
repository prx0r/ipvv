## s10-v15 — argument map

Vision granted (dṛṣṭo 'pi): seen in supreme-bliss-nectar, in the world-self. Hunger remains (sutkaṇṭhito 'smi): greatly longing for touch-rasa with You. Sight does not satiate touch: the verse holds vision and contact as distinct desires.
