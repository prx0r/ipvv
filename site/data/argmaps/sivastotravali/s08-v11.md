## s08-v11 — argument map

May all things (samastavastūny api) come under thought's scope (cintāviṣayaṃ) and vision's footing (dṛśaḥ padaṃ ca) for me (mama). May vision-thought-light-nectar-essences (darśanacintanaprakāśāmṛtasārāṇi) blaze supreme (paraṃ parisphurantu). Scope, not seizure: all things under thought's scope without seizure.

### Open
- Whether 'cintāviṣayaṃ' means 'under thought's scope' or 'as thought's object' is unresolved.
- Whether 'parisphurantu' means 'blaze' or 'manifest' is unresolved.
