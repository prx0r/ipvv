## s13-v12 — argument map

Tatra tatra viṣaye bahirvibhāty antare ca parameśvarīyutam: there and there, in things outside, shines, and inside, with Pārvatī. Tvāṃ jagattritayanirbharaṃ sadā lokayeya nijapāṇipūjitam: may I always see You, filling the three worlds, worshipped by my own hand. The verse: see You filling all three worlds, worshipped by my own hand.

### Open
- Whether 'bahirvibhāty' means 'shines outside' or 'is manifest outside' is unresolved.
- Whether 'nijapāṇipūjitam' means 'worshipped by my own hand' or 'worshipped by own hand' is unresolved.
