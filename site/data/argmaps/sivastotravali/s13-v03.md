## s13-v03 — argument map

Tāvake vapuṣi viśvanirbhare citsudhārasamaye niratyaye: residing in Your universal body, filled with supreme nectar of consciousness. Tiṣṭhataḥ satatam arcataḥ prabhuṃ: residing, worshipping the Lord constantly. Jīvitaṃ mṛtam athānyad astu me: let me die, let me go to hell or heaven — I don't care, so long as I have Your worship. The verse states the devotee's sole longing: reside in Your body and worship, indifferent to life/death.

### Open
- Whether 'jīvitaṃ mṛtam' means 'let me live or die' or 'whether living or dead' is unresolved.
- Whether 'anyad' means 'other' or 'anything else' is unresolved.
- Swamiji: jīvitam/mṛtam/anyad spans hell, heaven, even mokṣa — all indifferent against constant worship; the verse's only desire is satatam arcana.
