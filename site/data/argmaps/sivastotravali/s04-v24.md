## s04-v24 — argument map

*bata nātha dṛḍho 'yam ātmabandho.* Alas, Lord, strong is this self-bond, made of non-recognition of You, fashioned by You alone — first ignored by me, it never loosens a bit. Bond made BY Him OF non-recognition: akhyāti is the Pratyabhijñā technical term (failure to recognize the Self), not fame. The knot He tied. Held, not solved.
