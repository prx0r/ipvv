## s14-v24 — argument map

The cries themselves run to thirteen and end in the three-eyed One (tryakṣa). Number ends in seeing: the list's deliberately visual close. Acclamation stops counting and starts seeing.

### Open
- Whether 'tryakṣa' refers to Śiva's three eyes or to the three-eyed One as a title is unresolved.
- Whether the thirteen cries are counted or piled is unresolved.
