## s08-v09 — argument map

If ever (api kadācana) by the thinnest union-drops (tāvakasaṅgamāmṛtakaṇācchuraṇena tanīyasā) — may I turn (parāṅmukho) from all world-pleasures (sakalalokasukheṣu); or shall I, fallen from both (ubhayacyuta eva), be...? (kim). The wager: union-drops against world-pleasures, with double-fall risk named.

### Open
- Whether 'ubhayacyuta' means 'fallen from both' or 'fallen from both sides' is unresolved.
- Whether 'kim' is a question or a rhetorical exclamation is unresolved.
