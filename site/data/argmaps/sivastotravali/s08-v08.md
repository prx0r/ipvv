## s08-v08 — argument map

May there rise (samudiyād), looking at such Yours (tādṛśatāvakānana viloka), a flood of supreme nectar (parāmṛtasamplavaḥ). So my filling (ghaṭeta) of non-duality's dread gorge (bhavadadvayāprathanaghoradarīparipūraṇam) happens (mama). The abyss is flooded: non-duality's dread gorge filled by supreme nectar.

### Open
- Whether 'tādṛśatāvakānana' means 'such Yours' or 'such a forest of Yours' is unresolved.
- Whether 'ghoradarī' means 'dread gorge' or 'terrible cave' is unresolved.
