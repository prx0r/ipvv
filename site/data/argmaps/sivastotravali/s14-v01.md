## s14-v01 — argument map

Before my own master, treasure of victory-fortune (jayalakṣmīnidhānasya nijasya svāminaḥ puraḥ) — may I taste for a moment the nectar of victory-proclamation (jayodghoṣaṇapīyūṣarasam āsvādaye kṣaṇam). The verse opens with tasting, not describing: the devotee asks to taste the victory-proclamation.

### Open
- Whether 'jayalakṣmīnidhāna' means 'treasure of victory-fortune' or 'fortune's victory-treasure' is unresolved.
- Whether 'āsvādaye' is optative or imperative is unresolved.
