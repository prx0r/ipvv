## s13-v01 — argument map

Pain defined as separation, pleasure as union: saṃgrahena sukhaduḥkhalakṣaṇaṃ māṃ prati sthitam idaṃ śṛṇu prabho. Saukhyam eṣa bhavatā samāgamaḥ: just to be in Thy presence is pleasure. Svāminā viraha eva duḥkhitā: being carried away from Thy presence brings deep pain. The verse defines pain/pleasure before proving the definition across twenty moves.

### Open
- Whether 'sukhaduḥkhalakṣaṇaṃ' means 'definition of pleasure and pain' or 'characteristic of pleasure and pain' is unresolved.
- Whether 'viraha' means 'separation' or 'absence' is unresolved.
