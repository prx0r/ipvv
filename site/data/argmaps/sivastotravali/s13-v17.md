## s13-v17 — argument map

Kaṇṭhakoṇaviniviṣṭam īśa te kālakūṭam api me mahāmṛtam: the poison in the throat-corner, O Lord, is great nectar to me. Apy upāttam amṛtaṃ bhavadvapur bhedavṛtti yadi rocate na me: even if Your body is nectar, division does not please me. The verse: poison becomes nectar; division does not please.

### Open
- Whether 'kaṇṭhakoṇa' means 'throat-corner' or 'corner of the throat' is unresolved.
- Whether 'bhedavṛtti' means 'division' or 'differentiating activity' is unresolved.
