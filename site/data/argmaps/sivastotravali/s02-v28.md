## s02-v28 — argument map

Thunderbolt to all doubt, world-end fire to all misfortune, world's-end to all joylessness — we bow the Māheśvara path. The path itself praised as weapon; protection by awe. (Swamiji, Hymns to Shiva ch2 v28: alakṣmī is poverty; stepping on the path ends doubt, misfortune, joylessness at once.)
