## ajad-p02-v18 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What shows at the limit?

**Premises.**
- In I-knowing (ahaṃvidi) whose self is non-separation grasp (avyatirekātma-vimarśātman), limit-dwelling (paryantavartinyām).
- Nothing inert beyond shining (bhāsanād adhikaṃ jaḍam) appears (bhāti).
- Vṛ: in I-light non-separate from the world — seat of all knowing — the inert has no being past shining's measure; the lone non-inert knower flashes, never fallen from self-seat, outshining and reabsorbing the world-heap.

**Inference.** Seal of the passage: at the limit, shining exhausts the inert; one knower flashes, world outshone and reabsorbed.
