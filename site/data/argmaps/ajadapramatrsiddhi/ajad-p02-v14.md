## ajad-p02-v14 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is there experience without grasp?

**Premises.**
- No experience (anubhavaḥ) stands apart from distinct-and-indistinct certainty (bhinnābhinnaviniścayam).
- None apart from connected shining, grasp-less in its self (vimarśarahitātmanaḥ).
- Vṛ: cut from joining-severing autonomy, dull shining yields no relish-experience (saṃrambhātmānubhava); grasp's greatness is housing the world inside as self-one, then outshining it as this, then resting it in full I.

**Inference.** Experience just is grasping certainty; grasp-less shining experiences nothing.
