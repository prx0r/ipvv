## ajad-p03-v26 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What fits the inert?

**Premises.**
- Whatever grounding (niṣṭhā) of thing-ordering (bhāvavyavasthā) — pervasion (vyāpitvam) and linking (anusaṃdhānam) of the I-sharing him (tasyāhaṃbhāvabhāginaḥ).
- That (taj) fits (yujyate) nothing inert (jaḍasya na).
- Vṛ: where all things stand settled — awareness-self of full-I-grasp pith, freedom-never-parted — pervasion-as-linking holds everywhere; the unfree inert, grasp-pith-less, cannot host it.

**Inference.** Pervasion and linking belong to I-sharers only: the inert cannot host them.
