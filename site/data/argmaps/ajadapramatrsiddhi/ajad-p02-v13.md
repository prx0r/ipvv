## ajad-p02-v13 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What being have inert things?

**Premises.**
- In self near-unreal (asatkalpāḥ), these inert ones belong to shining alone (prakāśasyaiva).
- Shining alone is — of own self, through selves own and other (svaparātmabhiḥ).
- Vṛ: without rest in awareness they are near-unreal; shining alone swells, non-separate from its form, in both knower-known heaps — the autonomy-teaching (svātantryavāda) dawns here.

**Inference.** Inert things have only borrowed being: shining alone is, owning both heaps — autonomy's first dawn.
