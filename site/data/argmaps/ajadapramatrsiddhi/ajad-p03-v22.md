## ajad-p03-v22 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Where lies the ordering of things?

**Premises.**
- No thing-ordering (nārthavyavasthā) lies in breath-and-kin through I-blocking (ahaṃbhāvanirodhataḥ).
- Shining's self-rest (prakāśasyātmaviśrāntiḥ) is sung (kīrtitaḥ) as I-ness (ahaṃbhāvaḥ) — indeed (hi).
- Vṛ: blocking the grasp (parāmarśa) that is mere self-shining, no thing-order holds, since order rests in awareness; self-rest of awareness is full-I-grasp by nature — the thing-orderer is sung.

**Inference.** Order is not in breath but in I-ness: shining's self-rest, sung as the orderer.
