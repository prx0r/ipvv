## ajad-p01-v01 — argument map

**Speaker.** pūrvapakṣin (Vijñānavādin)

**Question.** What in things themselves distinguishes real from unreal, conscious from unconscious?

**Premises.**
- Real and unreal show no difference in their own self (nijātmani).
- Inert and non-inert likewise show none.
- Hence the certainty: no distinguishing mark sits in the thing itself.

**Inference.** The opponent's opening certainty: distinctions have no seat in things themselves.


### Open
- Scope of the collapse (all things, or only cognized ones) stays open; vv2-8 grant it arguendo.
