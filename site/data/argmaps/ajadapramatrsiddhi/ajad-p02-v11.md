## ajad-p02-v11 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How does consciousness take on grounding?

**Premises.**
- Consciousness, apt (ucita) to ground another thing's showing by saying 'this' (idam iti).
- And again, by itself (ātmanā), by saying 'I' (aham iti).
- Vṛ: the split thing merits showing through this-grasp, then settlement through I-grasp; without rest in I, nothing becomes usable (vyavahārya).

**Inference.** Consciousness fits itself to both poles: 'this' for the other's showing, 'I' for itself.
