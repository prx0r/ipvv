## ajad-p02-v17 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What holds both measures?

**Premises.**
- For both, this (eṣaḥ) is the end-ground (paryantabhūmiḥ) of all object-awarenesses (sarvārthasaṃvidām).
- One alone (eka eva) through continuity (anusaṃdhānāt) — before-after joining makes flashing one.
- So no other is possible (ato 'nyo nopapadyate).
- Vṛ: resting-seat of all being-and-non-being awarenesses; difference cannot hold.

**Inference.** Continuity alone gathers both measures into one end-ground; no second stands.
