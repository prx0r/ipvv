## ajad-p01-v04 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Granted no difference — does the dispute collapse?

**Premises.**
- Let distinction be denied outright (mā bhūd).
- Still, from appearing's presence and absence a working difference succeeds (siddhyaty eva).
- That much serves the end (prayojanam); Vṛ: function (arthakriyā) succeeds on that basis.

**Inference.** Concession costs nothing: a purpose-serving difference survives the grant.


### Open
- Whose purpose — the disputant's or the world's? Read as the opponent's own pragmatic end, per Vṛ's arthakriyā gloss.
