## ajad-p01-v02 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Do appearing, describing, or being touch a thing's essence?

**Premises.**
- Showing (prakhyā), telling (upākhyā), being, and non-being its reverse contact nothing of own-form.
- Own-form faces the knower alone (jñātṛgāmin); Vṛ: showing and kin are the knower's property (jñātur dharmas).

**Inference.** Essence is knower-facing, never predicate-fixed.
