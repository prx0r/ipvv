## ajad-p02-v16 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How is the self both bound and free?

**Premises.**
- The same self (sa eṣa evātmā) twofold: measured (mitaḥ) and unmeasured (aparimitaḥ).
- Breath-bound (prāṇādinā niruddhaḥ): atom (aṇuḥ); as supreme self: unbroken (akhaṇḍitaḥ).
- Vṛ: the Lord Himself, longing to sport the world-play, shows as breath-selves and contracts into living; uncontracted, He stays full, free awareness-self.

**Inference.** One self, two measures — contracted atom, unbroken whole: play (krīḍā), not split.
