## ajad-p02-v15 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Where does cut-off 'this'-grasping complete?

**Premises.**
- For this 'this'-grasping cut off (vicchinnavimarśasya), fulfillment (kṛtārthatā) is rest in own form (svasvarūpe viśrāntiḥ).
- And that grasping — 'I am this' (so 'ham ity ayam) — is this itself.
- Vṛ: the life-principle (jīvitasthānīya) — through it even inert stuff, one with the grasper-self, comes to rest as I; the non-inert are in truth one with the higher knower.

**Inference.** Severed grasping completes in self-rest — 'I am this,' the done-ness that quickens even the inert.
