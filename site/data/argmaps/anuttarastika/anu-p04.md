## anu-p04 — argument map

Bliss (ānandaḥ) here (atra) is not (na) wealth-middle-intoxication (vittamadhyamadavana). Nor (naiva) like enjoying women (aṅganāsaṅgavat); nor lamp-sun-moon-made (dīpārkendukṛta) splendour-multitude (prabhāprakaravat); nor (naiva) even the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ) as commonly pictured. Joy (harṣaḥ) with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground (saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr), like burden-lowering (bhārāvatāropamaḥ), is the attainment (prāptiḥ) of the forgotten treasure (vismṛtanidheḥ) of the all-nonduality state (sarvādvaitapadasya): the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ). Bliss = prakāśodaya: difference gathered, burden set down, treasure remembered.

### Open
- Whether 'saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr' is one compound or several is unresolved.
- Whether 'prakāśodaya' in the first half and second half refers to the same or different light-rise is unresolved.
