## anu-p01 — argument map

Six techniques denied: contemplation, speech-connection, discussion, visualization, steadying, japa-practice-effort. The question replaces the hunt: what stands well-decided? Speak it, hear it. The answer is two refusals and one command: not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness, wholly, as staying. Staying (yathāvasthita) is the method that is no method — anupāya named by performance, not by label.
