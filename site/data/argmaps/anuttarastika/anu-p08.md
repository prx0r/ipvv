## anu-p08 — argument map

What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — shines (rājate) in the consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇe). All opposites, one mirror; the mirror doesn't sort them. That all (tat sarvaṃ), from own-grasping-consciousness-rise (svavimarśasaṃvidudayāt), form-light-natured (rūpaprakāśātmakam), having known (jñātvā) — — own-experience-risen (svānubhavādhirūḍha) glory (mahimā): Lordhood (viśveśvaratvam) — worship (bhaja).

### Open
- Whether 'svavimarśasaṃvidudaya' is one compound or several is unresolved.
- Whether 'rūpaprakāśātmaka' modifies 'tat sarvam' or 'mahimā' is unresolved.
- Whether 'bhaja' is imperative or indicative is unresolved.
