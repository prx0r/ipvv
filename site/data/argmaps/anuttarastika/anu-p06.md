## anu-p06 — argument map

Former non-being (pūrvābhāva), being (bhava), action (kriyā) — indeed (hi) suddenly (sahasā) things (bhāvāḥ) always (sadā) in this existence (asmin bhave) — of middle-formed (madhyākāra) transformation (vikāra), confusion (saṃkaravatāṃ) of them (teṣām): whence (kutaḥ) truth (satyatā)? In non-truth (niḥsatye), unsteady (capale), manifold (prapañcanicaye), dream-delusion (svapnabhrame), clever (peśale) — From doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-contriving transcended (śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ): awakened (prabuddhaḥ), become (bhava). Four obstacles — doubt, fear, stain, reasoning — with reasoning listed last among the fetters.

### Open
- Whether 'madhyākāra' means middle-formed or of middle-form is unresolved.
- Whether 'peśala' means clever or cunning is unresolved.
- Whether 'bhava' (become) is imperative or indicative is unresolved.
