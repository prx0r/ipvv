## isva-p06-v48 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Needlessness — whose?

**Premises.**
- By the freed spirit — done with tasks, need-free — he stands alone (mukta-puṃsā kṛtārthatvād anarthitvāt sa kevalaḥ).
- Not so: needlessness belongs to no spirit ever, nor to mind (naitan nārthitvam asyāsti jātu puṃso'tha cetasaḥ).

**Inference.** The objector's need-free spirit denied on both sides.
