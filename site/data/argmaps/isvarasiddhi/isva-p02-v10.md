## isva-p02-v10 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How is the higher self shown in eye etc.?

**Premises.**
- As (yathā) combination-ness (saṃghātatva) is seen (anvitekṣite) merely for another's sake (pārārthyamātreṇa).
- In eye etc. (cakṣurādau), by that fitness (tadaucityāt): the self-dear (ātmeṣṭaḥ), uncompounded (asaṃhataḥ), higher (paraḥ).
- Prose: Sāṅkhya's own eye-example turned — fitness, not assemblage, shows the self-cherished uncompounded.

**Inference.** The higher self shows in eye etc. by fitness, as combination seen for another's sake.
