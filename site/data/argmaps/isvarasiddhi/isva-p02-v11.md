## isva-p02-v11 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How is fire proved as khadira-wood?

**Premises.**
- As (yathā) from afar (dūrataḥ), from smoke (dhūmāt), proving (siddhiḥ) in a khadira-grove (khadirakānane).
- Fire (vahniḥ) is proved (siddhyati) just by knowing it (tatpratipattyā), as khadira-wood (khādiratvena).
- Stock smoke-fire example redeployed: mere knowing-it suffices for wood-kind — no need to see the logs.

**Inference.** Fire is proved as khadira-wood by mere knowing: the stock example turned for fitness.
