## isva-p04-v29 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What of clay-lump nature?

**Premises.**
- From the pradhāna-made clay-lump (pradhānamayamṛtpiṇḍāt) indeed (eva) — of such nature (tādṛksvabhāvataḥ).
- Now (atha): own-nature (svabhāvaḥ) — this (eṣaḥ) of this (asya) — which (yat) looks to potter and the rest (kulālādi apekṣaṇam).

**Inference.** Own-nature looks to potter etc.: clay-lump nature potter-watching.
