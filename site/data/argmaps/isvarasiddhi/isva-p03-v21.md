## isva-p03-v21 — argument map

**Speaker.** Sāṅkhya (pūrvapakṣin)

**Question.** What moves pradhāna?

**Premises.**
- Hence indeed (ata eva), for ever-becoming (mahattvapariṇāminaḥ) pradhāna (pradhānasya).
- No (na) nimitta-cause (nimittakāraṇaṃ) other (anyat) but (ṛte) person-aim-striving (puruṣārthodyamāt).

**Inference.** Only person-aim-striving moves ever-becoming pradhāna.
