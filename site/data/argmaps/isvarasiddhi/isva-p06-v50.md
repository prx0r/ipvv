## isva-p06-v50 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Mokṣa — nature or karma's end?

**Premises.**
- If from perceptible-only-nature of that, not from karma's waning (saṃvedyaikasvabhāvatvāt tasya karmakṣayān na cet).
- Mokṣa would be mere nature, resting on karma's waning (tat svabhāvatvam eva syān mokṣaḥ karmakṣayāśrayaḥ).

**Inference.** The view collapses mokṣa into nature.
