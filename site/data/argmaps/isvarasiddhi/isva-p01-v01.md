## isva-p01-v01 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (maṅgala, praise voice)

**Question.** Under what auspice does the Lord's proof open?

**Premises.**
- He who makes (karoti) — him (tam), I (aham) praise (naumi): the one (ekam) God (devam), Maheśvara the Lord.
- The auspice names the conclusion in advance: maker, one, Lord.

**Inference.** Auspice: praise of the one maker-Lord whose proof follows.
