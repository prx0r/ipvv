## isva-p05-v37 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Unperceived arising — whose purpose?

**Premises.**
- Buddhi's arising-or-not (saṃbhavan apy asaṃbhavī).
- Without spirit-perceptibility (vinā saṃvedyatāṃ puṃsaḥ).
- In such a state (tathātve).

**Inference.** No puruṣārthatā where no spirit perceives (sentence completes in v38).
