## isva-p07-v56 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (signature)

**Question.** Who shines within — and who signs?

**Premises.**
- One's own self indeed — this — flashes forth: Īśvara within all breathers (svātmaivāyaṃ sphurati sakalaprāṇinām īśvaro'ntaḥ).
- Maker and knower too (kartā jñātāpi ca).
- Yet recognition of the beyond is to be effected (yadi paraṃ pratyabhijñāsya sādhyā).
- And the joining is made by us elsewhere (sā cāsmābhir vihitaghaṭanānyatra).
- But Īśvara-proved stands even in duality (kiṃ tv īśasiddhir dvaite'py asti).
- Fitting, and more: fire shines at midnight (ity ucitam adhikaṃ bhāti vahnir niśīthe).

**Inference.** Self is Īśvara within all; the proof stands even dualist; signature and forward glance.
