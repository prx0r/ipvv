## isva-p03-v22 — argument map

**Speaker.** Sāṅkhya (pūrvapakṣin)

**Question.** What is own-nature for?

**Premises.**
- Own-nature (svabhāvaḥ) indeed (eva) — and (ca) this (eṣaḥ) of this (asya).
- From seeing the root-cause then (mūlahetoḥ tatekṣaṇāt); below mind-ground (cittabhūmer adharataḥ), toward body-ordaining (śarīrādi vidhim).

**Inference.** Own-nature faces downward: from root-cause-seeing toward body's ordaining.


### Open
- tatekṣaṇāt parsed then-seeing (tat+ekṣaṇāt); at-the-moment (tate+kṣaṇāt) equivalent in force — settled.
