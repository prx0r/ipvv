## isva-p05-v40 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Perceived — by whom?

**Premises.**
- Even unperceived by spirit, could it be? Not will (puṃso'pi syāt…asaṃvedyā na…icchā).
- Perceived — by whom would it be (vedyā ca kasya sā)?
- If by all: omniscience of each follows (yadi sarveṣāṃ sārvajñyam ekaikasya prasajyate).

**Inference.** Generic will forces omniscience on each — reductio.
