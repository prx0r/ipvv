## isva-p02-v16 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How do helpers picture effects?

**Premises.**
- Helpers (hetumantaḥ), entering (praviśya) as others (anye), reaching (prāpya) material-form (upādānarūpatām).
- They picture (citrayanti) effects (kāryāṇi) — like earth-water-juices (bhaumodakarasā iva).

**Inference.** Helpers picture effects by entering-as-other and reaching material-form — like earth-juices.
