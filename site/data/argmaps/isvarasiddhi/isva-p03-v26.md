## isva-p03-v26 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What of Sāṅkhya?

**Premises.**
- The mahad-first-change-torrent-natured (mahadādivikāraughapariṇāmasvabhāvakam), three-natured (triguṇātma) pradhāna-self (pradhānaṃ).
- And (ca) thereby (tena): godless (anīśvaram) Sāṅkhya (sāṃkhyam).

**Inference.** Verdict on Sāṅkhya: three-natured pradhāna — thereby godless.
