## isva-p06-v52 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Abandoned — what stands?

**Premises.**
- Thus view-abandoned — that nature-view is unjust (tathā ca darśanatyāgas tan na yuktā svabhāvadṛk).
- Hence body-and-rest, famously, have sentient-maker as prior (tasmāt prasiddhaṃ tanvādi buddhimatkartṛpūrvakam).

**Inference.** The siddhānta: bodies bespeak a knowing maker.


### Open
- svabhāvadṛk agrees with yuktā (the abandoning is nature-viewed).
