## isva-p05-v39 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How could will serve all?

**Premises.**
- Will, indeed — how serve all spirits' purposes (icchā nāma kathaṃ sarvapuruṣārthopayoginī syāt)?
- The escape: it belongs to generic pradhāna, for all (kiṃ tu sā pradhānasya sāmānyasya akhilān prati).

**Inference.** The how-question marks the generic-will escape, demolished in v40.
