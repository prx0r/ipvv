## isva-p01-v09 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What answers the anvaya dilemma?

**Premises.**
- Merely (kevalam) even from showing (saṃdarśanāt) pervasion (vyāpti) with the conscious alone (buddhimanmātra).
- By thesis-property force (pakṣadharmabalāt), somewhere (kvacit), someone (kaścit) wise (dhīmān) is understood (pratīyate).

**Inference.** Reply: bare showing of conscious-only pervasion suffices — by pakṣadharma-force alone, some wise one is grasped. Minimalism against the dilemma.
