## isva-p04-v28 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Without what no pot?

**Premises.**
- Without that (tat vinā) — without potter (kumbhakāreṇa) — even a pot (ghaṭo'pi).
- How (katham) could it be (bhavet)?

**Inference.** No pot without potter: how else could it be?
