## isva-p01-v06 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Can matter arrange?

**Premises.**
- There is (asti), in function-yoking (arthakriyāyoge), among bodies and worlds (śarīrabhuvanādiṣu).
- This distinct arrangement (ayam saṃniveśaviśeṣaḥ) — not possibly from material (anupādānasaṃbhavī).

**Inference.** Arrangement in function-fit things cannot come from matter alone.
