## isva-p02-v17 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Are outward-changers capable?

**Premises.**
- Others (anye), again (punar), in material (upādāne), outward-changing (bahirviparivartinaḥ).
- For form-variety-yoking (rūpavaicitryayogāya) in effects (kāryeṣu) — not so (na tathā) capable (kṣamāḥ).

**Inference.** Outward-changers in matter can't yoke form-variety in effects.
