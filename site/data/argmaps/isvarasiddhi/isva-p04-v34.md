## isva-p04-v34 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What of bodies and arrangement?

**Premises.**
- Never (na) would there be (syāt) body etc. (śarīrādi) — long clay-lump (dīrghamṛtpiṇḍa) or (vā).
- Hence (atas) indeed (eva): by being-led-to-the-end (arthanīyatvāt), arrangement (saṃniveśaḥ) excels (viśiṣyate).

**Inference.** No bodies otherwise; end-led arrangement excels.
