## isva-p05-v43 — argument map

**Speaker.** Pūrvapakṣin (svabhāva-vādin)

**Question.** Purpose-speech — by what right?

**Premises.**
- What is born, fit — the wise man joins to one another (yaj jātaṃ yogyam anyonyaṃ yojayed buddhimāñ janaḥ).
- By this courtesy, puruṣārthatva is said of it (asya tena upacāreṇa puruṣārthatvam ucyate).

**Inference.** Purpose is courtesy-speech — the thread v44 pulls.
