## isva-p01-v05 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How is the maker marked?

**Premises.**
- In the varied (vicitre), the conscious alone (buddhimān eva) is marked (lakṣyate) — as among pots (ghaṭādiṣv iva).
- The maker (kartā), wise (vijñaḥ), having resolve for varied-this-use (vicitraitadupayogābhisaṃdhimān).
- Prose: variety (vaicitrya) is the mark editors of Nyāya-examples read for intelligence.

**Inference.** Variety marks the wise maker: resolved on varied use, visible among pots.
