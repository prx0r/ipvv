## isva-p05-v45 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Ceaseless churning — whose liberation?

**Premises.**
- From own-effect-change the root-cause would not cease (svakāryapariṇāmāt na viramen mūlakāraṇam).
- Oh — it does not cease (āho na viramati).
- This beyond, in nature's seven (etat paraṃ prakṛtisaptake).

**Inference.** Nature-alone never releases — hence a perceiving maker (v52).


### Open
- āho ironic — the objector's triumph voiced to collapse it; drives to v52's buddhimatkartṛ.
