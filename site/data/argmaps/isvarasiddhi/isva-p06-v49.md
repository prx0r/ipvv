## isva-p06-v49 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** No reunion — and yet born?

**Premises.**
- With need gone, no meeting with spirit again would be (arthitvavigamān na syād bhūyaḥ puṃsā samāgamaḥ).
- This too is unjust, once body-dwelling mind is born (tad etad api na nyāyyaṃ jāte dehasthacetasi).

**Inference.** Liberation-by-needlessness contradicts rebirth.
