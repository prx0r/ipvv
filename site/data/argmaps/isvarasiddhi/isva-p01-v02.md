## isva-p01-v02 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What is the inference to be proved?

**Premises.**
- Bodies and the rest (tanvādi) have conscious cause (buddhimaddhetu), having distinct arrangement (saṃniveśaviśeṣavat).
- Like pots (ghaṭa ... like atoms, paramāṇuvat — mid-verse lacuna).
- The Nyāya shape is unmistakable: pakṣa (bodies), hetu (arrangement), dṛṣṭānta (pot).

**Inference.** Thesis stated in Nyāya dress: arranged bodies bespeak a conscious maker, like pots.
