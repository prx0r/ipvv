## isva-p03-v20 — argument map

**Speaker.** Sāṅkhya (pūrvapakṣin)

**Question.** How easy is change?

**Premises.**
- Through that (tena), person's (puruṣasya) enjoyment-proving (bhogasaṃsiddhiḥ) — again and again (yathā yathā).
- So (tathā tathā), change (pariṇatiḥ) would be (syāt) nothing (na kiṃcana) hard (durghaṭam).

**Inference.** Change comes easy, again and again, for spirit's enjoyment.
