## samb-p01-v08 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What makes error stick?

**Premises.**
- Clinging-to-shining (ābhāsalagnatvam) is not thing-resembling-shining (ābhāsasadṛśārthatā).
- It is following-after-shining (ābhāsānusāritvaṃ): error is just that — an erred shining (bhrānta ābhāsa eva tat).
- Prose (p.5): all errors concern resembling — but beyond resembling, errors hold by shining-clinging; else the silver-shell could not guide. Transaction-guiding seeming must follow the shining.

**Inference.** Error sticks by following shining, not resembling things: seeming that guides must trail being.
