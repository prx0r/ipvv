## samb-p01-v09 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Can this justification be stated?

**Premises.**
- Thus (ittham), in this very shining (asmin), such unity-seeing of the many (anekasyaikatādṛśi).
- Its statable justification (vācyopapattiḥ) too is told (uktā), clearly (sphuṭam), in recognition's path (pratyabhijñānaye).

**Inference.** Warrant claimed: the many-seen-as-one is statable inside the Pratyabhijñā, plainly.
