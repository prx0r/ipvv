## samb-p01-v07 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is the joining only thought's fabrication?

**Premises.**
- This (etat) is no mere thought (na kalpanāmātram): it cannot be warded off (avāraṇāt).
- Like snake-error (sarpabhrānteḥ iva): its remedy fails (nivāraṇam) through clinging to a shining (ābhāsalagnatvāt).
- Prose (p.5): even your side admits error clinging to thing-shining, unuprootable by refutation.

**Inference.** Retorsion begins: unwardability proves the joining is no mere fabrication.
