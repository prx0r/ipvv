## samb-p02-v12 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Does blocked activity touch another thought?

**Premises.**
- Activity (vyāpāraḥ) by this (anena) blocked (ruddhaḥ) — it (asau) should touch (spṛśet) no other thought (vikalpāntaram).
- For on touching another thought (vikalpāntarasaṃsparśe) follows knower-difference (mātṛbhedaprasaṅgataḥ).

**Inference.** One reflection's business touches no other thought, or knowers multiply.
