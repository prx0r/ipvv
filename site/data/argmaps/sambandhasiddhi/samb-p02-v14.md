## samb-p02-v14 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is there another relation?

**Premises.**
- Thus (iti) there is (asti) also (tāvat?) another (anyaḥ): qualifier-qualified (viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyayoḥ) relation (saṃbandhaḥ).
- For helping (upakārārthaḥ); it serves (upayujyate) for knowing (jñātum).

**Inference.** A second relation stands: qualifier-qualified, for helping, serving knowledge.
