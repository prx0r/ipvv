## samb-p02-v15 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is there twoness in manyness?

**Premises.**
- Even in manyness (bahutve'pi), twoness (dvitvam) would be (bhavet).
- From double-reflection birth (dvivimarśabhuvaḥ) — as the king's horses-chariots-men (rājño'śvarathapattayaḥ).

**Inference.** Manyness still yields twoness, double-reflection-born — like the king's retinue.


### Open
- dvivimarśabhuvaḥ case read ablative (from); genitive ('of') equivalent in sense — settled.
