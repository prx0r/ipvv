## samb-p02-v18 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Where does genitive-relation settle?

**Premises.**
- Genitive-stated (ṣaṣṭhyādivācyaḥ) relation (saṃbandhaḥ), settled (viniviṣṭaḥ) in qualifier (viśeṣaṇe).
- Reaching qualified-oneness (viśeṣyaikatvamāpanne), yet not abandoning (anujjhati) own form (svaṃ rūpam).

**Inference.** Genitive-relation settles in the qualifier, reaches oneness, keeps own form.
