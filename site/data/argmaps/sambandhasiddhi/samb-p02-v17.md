## samb-p02-v17 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How many thinkings?

**Premises.**
- 'The king's man' (rājñaḥ puruṣaḥ iti) — this (eṣaḥ), after (paścāt), in relation's range (saṃbandhagocaraḥ).
- Is to be thought (kalpyaḥ) by this (anena); or the former two to be thought (kalpyau) — those three (trayaḥ) in every way (sarvathā).
- Prose: whole-thought after relation, or part-thoughts — three thinkings, all told.

**Inference.** Three thinkings: whole after relation, or the former two — all told.
