## samb-p01-v05 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is uninterruptedness the relation — and what positively is it?

**Premises.**
- Not uninterruptedness (na ānantaryam): it misses relation (asaṃbandhāt) — else it would hold of iron bars (ayaḥśalākayoḥ).
- Prose (p.2): distant father-son lack it, adjacent iron bars (even pervading substances) have it — neither necessary nor sufficient.
- Hence (tena): fusion (śleṣaḥ) of the many's form — that alone is one-selfhood (aikātmyam eva).

**Inference.** Third reduction fails (with the iron-bars counterexample); positive thesis: relation is the many's form-fused one-selfhood.


### Open
- asaṃbandhāt parsed as 'missing relation' (the mark fails to track); the grammar also allows 'from non-relation' — sense identical.
