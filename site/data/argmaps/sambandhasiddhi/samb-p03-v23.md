## samb-p03-v23 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** What apparatus closes?

**Premises.**
- Told (uktaḥ), self-awareness-proved (svasaṃvitsiddhaḥ), this (ayaṃ): unrefuted (nirbādhaḥ), useful (upayogavān)...
- relation (saṃbandhaḥ) in recognition (pratyabhijñāyām) — full (pūrṇaḥ) apparatus (parikaraḥ) besides (punaḥ).

**Inference.** Seal: told, proved, unrefuted, useful — relation in recognition, full apparatus besides.
