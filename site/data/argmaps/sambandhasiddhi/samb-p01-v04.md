## samb-p01-v04 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** Is 'residing-in-two' the relation?

**Premises.**
- From stating self-obtaining of non-beings (asatām ātmalābhokteḥ), two-seatedness (dviṣṭhatā) is likewise abandoned (hīyate).
- Form-fusion alone (rūpasaṃśleṣa eva) thus stands (sthitaḥ) as relation's sense (saṃbandhārthaḥ).
- Prose (p.2): duality and mutual separateness would be relations too; 'residing-in-two' is said of the pair, not the relation — state it plainly in its own form.

**Inference.** Second reduction fails: two-seatedness describes relata, never the relation; form-fusion stands in its place.
