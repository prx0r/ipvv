## samb-p02-v16 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How do the two reflections divide?

**Premises.**
- Reflection (vimarśaḥ) 'of king' (rājñaḥ iti) is one (ekaḥ), facing own-generality (svasāmānyonmukhaḥ).
- The other (paraḥ) — 'man' (puruṣaḥ iti), this (ayam), with master-generality-relation (svāmisāmānyasaṃbandhasahaḥ).

**Inference.** 'King' faces own-generality; 'man' faces the master's — two reflections, one relation.
