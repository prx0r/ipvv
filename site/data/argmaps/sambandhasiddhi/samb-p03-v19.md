## samb-p03-v19 — argument map

**Speaker.** Utpaladeva (siddhāntin)

**Question.** How do secondaries fit the chief?

**Premises.**
- With one short (ekahānyā) — by the pure (śuddhena), by the chief (pradhānena) — mutual fitness (anyānyayogitā) would be (syāt) of the secondary (guṇasya).
- As (yathā) the king's (rājñaḥ) man (puruṣaḥ), and (ca) the brahmin's.
- Prose: chief/secondary pairs measured against king/man, brahmin/[kṣatriya?].

**Inference.** Secondaries fit the chief one-short of pure: king's man, brahmin's.
