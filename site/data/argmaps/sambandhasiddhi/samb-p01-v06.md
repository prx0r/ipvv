## samb-p01-v06 — argument map

**Speaker.** Opponent (Dharmakīrtian; SP 5cd per MacCracken/Jha)

**Question.** Do the many need any unity at all?

**Premises.**
- No barring (bādhaḥ) of the many even in one's unity (ekasyaikye'pi) — they say, as others do.
- Things unmixed in themselves (amiśrāḥ svayaṃ bhāvāḥ) — thought (kalpanā) joins them (yojayati).
- Witness: Dharmakīrti's Sambandhaparīkṣā v.5cd (Jha Philosophy of Relations; MacCracken) — the opponent's proof-text, quoted against him.

**Inference.** The Buddhist position in its own voice: unmixed things, thought-joined; unity needs no seat.
