## tsa-a01-v01 — argument map

**Speaker.** Abhinavagupta (maṅgala voice, invoking)

**Question.** Whom invoke, and what flash forth?

**Premises.**
- Stainless-art-abode, new-creation-great Mother; full-bodied, five-hidden-faced Father.
- Their yamal-burst-forth being: the Heart — deathless clan beyond all — flash for me.

**Inference.** Mother-Father flash forth the Heart; the book promises plain essence; the lineage-lotus is to be gathered for worship.
