## tsa-a01-v04 — argument map

**Speaker.** Abhinavagupta (siddhānta voice, establishing)

**Question.** What liberates, and by what authority?

**Premises.**
- Knowledge frees as opponent of bondage-cause ignorance.
- Ignorance twofold: intellect-located (doubt-natured, wrong-conviction-natured); personal (thought-natured, contracted; root of saṃsāra).
- Personal ignorance removable by initiation — but initiation needs prior shun-and-take determination, impossible while intellect cannot decide.
- Therefore determinative intellect-dwelling knowledge is principal; practiced, it kills personal ignorance too (practice ends in thought-free).
- Śāstra hierarchy: only Lord-spoken scripture fully authoritative (absorbs others discriminatively, out-argues them); others liberate partially.
- Essence-chain: five streams, sixfold scriptures, Mālinīvijaya — whose contained content no gathering can expound.
- Without expounded nature no freedom or freeing (only pure knowing so-natured); supreme goal rooted in well-practiced knowing — hence this book begins.
- Ignorance declared bondage-cause, called stain; at full-knowing-digit rise it goes rootless; when self-consciousness rises with stains destroyed — liberation; so with this book all knowables revealed.

**Inference.** Determination-knowledge (śāstra-grounded) removes both ignorances; the Lord's scripture alone fully frees; its essence-chain ends in the Mālinīvijaya, whose content no gathering exhausts.
