## bh-p02 — argument map

Disclosure first: all this (etad), become of You (tvanmayam), without remainder (aśeṣam), now (idānīm) appears (bhāti) to me (mama) — by the power of Your grace (tvadanugrahaśaktyā). Then declaration: You (tvaṃ), Maheśa, always (sadaiva) my self (mamātmā); made of my own self (svātmamayaṃ), all (samastam) is mine (mama) through Him (tena). Grace is the cause of both disclosure and declaration; merit is not. Appearance, then ownership — the two-step is the pratyabhijñā structure.

### Open
- Whether 'svātmamaya' means 'made of my self' or 'self-identical' is unresolved.
- Whether 'tena' refers to grace or to Maheśa is unresolved.
