## bh-p09 — argument map

This consciousness of mine (saṃvid iyaṃ mama) dances (nṛtyati), sings (gāyati), deeply (gāḍham) rejoices (hṛṣyati) — O Bhairava-Lord. Having obtained (āpya) You, the dear (priyam): the beautiful vision alone (sudarśanam ekam), hard to get by others (durlabham anyajanaiḥ). Addressed to, and fenced to, the knower of the samaya (samayajña). Joy first, then its cause named: obtaining = vision, rare by structure.

### Open
- Whether 'samayajña' is vocative or qualifier converging on the same fence is unresolved.
- Whether 'sudarśanam ekam' means 'the one beautiful vision' or 'beautiful vision alone' is unresolved.
