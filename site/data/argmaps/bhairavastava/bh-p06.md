## bh-p06 — argument map

Arisen (prodita) true (satya) awakening (vibodha): its rays (marīci) reveal (prekṣita) the true essence (satattva) of world-objects (viśvapadārtha). On that ground — in You (tvayi), being-full (bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇa), I the self (aham ātmani) enter (emi) bliss (nirvṛtim). Awakening discloses; entry is bliss; pūrṇa (full/complete) is the Lord's nature. Bliss is ingress into fullness, not a feeling produced.
