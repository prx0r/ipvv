## bh-p04 — argument map

Prayer first: O Ender (antaka), do not (mā) direct (vinidhehi) toward me (māṃ prati) this (enām) most wrathful-terrible (krodhakarālatamām) gaze (dṛśam). Then the ground: firm (dhīra) in Śaṅkara-service (sevana) and contemplation (cintana), filled (maya) with terrifying, awe-full Bhairava-power (bhīṣaṇabhairavaśakti) — asmi (I am). Power first invoked against death's gaze, then declared as state. Śaṅkara here = Śiva, not Śaṅkarācārya — the context of Bhairava-service requires it.

### Open
- Whether 'asmi' is a declaration of achieved state or a performative utterance is unresolved.
- Whether 'śaṅkara' could refer to Śaṅkarācārya is resolved as Śiva by context.
