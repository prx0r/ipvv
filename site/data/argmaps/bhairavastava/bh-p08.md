## bh-p08 — argument map

Truly (satyam), O Śaṅkara: vow (vrata), giving (dāna), bathing (snāna), austerity (tapas) — dispels (vidāri) becoming's heat (bhavatāpa). Rites relativized, not despised: heat dispelled by rites. Contemplation (cintā) of Your (tāvaka) scripture (śāstra) as supreme nectar (parāmṛta), in mind (cetasi), streams (syandati) a bliss-stream (nirvṛtidhārā). Heat dispelled by rites; bliss streamed by contemplation.
