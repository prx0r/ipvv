## bh-p03 — argument map

Fear-talk denied: with You as Lord (nāthe) pervading (viśvagaye) own self (svātmani), therefore (tena) no talk of transmigration-fear exists (na saṃsṛtibhītikathā asti). Affliction conceded in the same breath: even though (satsvapi) hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts. No-fear and full-suffering together — the concession framed by the denial, resolved within the verse, not deferred. The non-dual recognition does not deny affliction; it denies fear of it: suffering is real, fear of it is not, because there is no 'other' to fear.

### Open
- Whether 'prāyeṇa' (mostly) softens the claim or qualifies it is unresolved.
- Whether 'karmagaṇa' means masses of karma or hosts of karmic beings is unresolved.
