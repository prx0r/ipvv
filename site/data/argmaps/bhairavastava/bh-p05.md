## bh-p05 — argument map

Great darkness already dispelled (dārita bhūri tamisra) by rays (dīdhiti) of awareness (saṃvid) consisting of world-becoming (bhavanmaya). Then the catalogue — death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — met with salutation (namo 'stu) alone. Never (na jātu) fear (bibhemi): awareness burned the dark before the terrors were named. Salutation finishes what light began: the terrors are met with salutation, not weapons.

### Open
- Whether 'bhavanmaya' (world-becoming) modifies awareness or rays is unresolved.
- Whether the four terrors are a complete list or exemplary is unresolved.
