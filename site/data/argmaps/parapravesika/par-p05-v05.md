## par-p05-v05 — argument map

Already-proved: indicators and causes cannot operate on the before-proved Lord; even the injunction-making knower's standing needs no proof (tādrūpyeṇa sattvāt). Two self-answering questions: thing's being assented; such-nature proved by the asker's own form (svasaṃvedana). Proof's remaining office: ordained for māyā-dealings only; all else, inert, cannot light itself — how settle another? Seal (taduktam): proofs stretch as things' life; beyond them the further Life, the Lord.
