## kram-p01 (1)
[and]-ayaṃ duḥkhavrātavrataparigame pāraṇavidhir mahāsaukhyāsāraprasaraṇarase durdinam idam | yad anyanyakkṛtyā viṣamaviśikhaploṣaṇaguror vibhoḥ stotre śaśvat pratiphalati ceto gatabhayam // AgKs_1

## kram-p02 (2)
[and]-anāmṛṣṭaḥ svātmā na hi bhavati bhāvapramitibhāj anāmṛṣṭaḥ svātmety api hi na vinā 'marśanavidheḥ | śivaś cāsau svātmā sphurad akhilabhāvaikasarasas tato 'ham tvatstotre pravaṇahṛdayo nityasukhitaḥ // AgKs_2

## kram-p03 (3)
[and]-vicitrair jātyādibhramaṇaparipāṭīparikarair avāptam sārvajñaṃ hṛdaya yad ayatnena bhavatā | tad antas tvadbodhaprasarasaraṇībhūtamahasi sphuṭam vāci prāpya prakaṭaya vibhoḥ stotram adhunā // AgKs_3
