# L200 — Śaktavijñāna (30 verses, 3 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Śaktavijñāna (30 verses) is Somananda's (9th-10th c.) work on Śākta knowledge. It establishes: Śākta vijñāna = thirteenfold supreme knowledge (place/entry/form/mark/characteristic/rising/awakening/wheel-rest/rest/transformation/going); indicated by Śambhu in all Trika śāstras; kanda is wheel-place; conquered breath leads to middle. Rising: meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess; throat = imperishable, directly Rudra, palate = abiding; having awakened, go quickly to unattached Śiva-state. Close: fear not in those; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births; stage-going declared, final state; knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; from navel-kanda, pulsing; from navel-wheel, exiting; when śakti awakens.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Śākta vijñāna = thirteenfold supreme knowledge, indicated by Śambhu.
- C2: Kanda is wheel-place; conquered breath leads to middle.
- C3: Rising = meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess.
- C4: Throat = imperishable, directly Rudra, palate = abiding; go to unattached Śiva-state.
- C5: Close = fear not; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births.
- C6: Knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; from navel-kanda, pulsing; when śakti awakens.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Śākta vijñāna as non-supreme.
