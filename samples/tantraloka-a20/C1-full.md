# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 20

## tal-a20-p01–p02 — initiation (dīkṣā) and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 20: thus I declare initiation, giving breath to the deluded. In triangle, fire-abode, fire-colored-radiant, protected. By-wind-purified, in-left-hand, brilliant. Taking some seed, thus in heart, also in hand burning, thinking, with japa united. By-fire-ignited-phaṭ-stream-burning-pained. Seed becomes seedless, unable to give birth. Burned, not for growth, by that very pratyaya. Mala-Māyā-karma by mantra-meditation-action-power. Burned, not for own work, seedless-pratyaya, this. That Śrī, well-pleased, me, Śaṃbhunātha revealed. Of seed also here work and arrangement by guru's grace. Because initiation by great radiance liberates even immobile. Who guru by japa-homa-arcā-dhyāna-accomplished in self. Knowing, initiation does, his initiation with-pratyaya is called. In avadhūta nirācāra tattva-knower, not this rule. With ācāra, initiation done, with visible-pratyaya-joined. In nirācāra initiation, pratyaya not spoken. Knowledge own-pratyaya because not worthy of other fruit. Dhyāna-etc from fruit to be-accomplished, thus siddhas said. Tulā-śuddhi-test or do, pratyaya-yoginī. As Śrī-tantra-sadbhāva said by supreme Lord. Śrī-pūrva-śāstra also this indicated by supreme Lord. Ānanda, arising, trembling, sleep, drowsiness, fifth. Thus saying, śakti-gradation-named. Arising by light-nature, body-grasping-disappearance. Body indeed earthly, chief, then chiefness abandons. By future-lightness-mantra, disciple meditated, suddenly-arisen. Actions there all, previously-said, should-do, guru. Said this tulā-śuddhi-initiation, pratyaya-giving.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes initiation (dīkṣā): giving breath to deluded; in triangle fire-abode; seed becomes seedless; mala-Māyā-karma burned by mantra-meditation; initiation by great radiance liberates even immobile; who guru by japa-homa-arcā-dhyāna-accomplished; his initiation with-pratyaya is called; in avadhūta nirācāra tattva-knower, not this rule; with ācāra, initiation done, with visible-pratyaya-joined; knowledge own-pratyaya because not worthy of other fruit; dhyāna-etc from fruit to be-accomplished; tulā-śuddhi-test; ānanda, arising, trembling, sleep, drowsiness, fifth; arising by light-nature; by future-lightness-mantra, disciple meditated, suddenly-arisen; actions there all, previously-said, should-do, guru; said this tulā-śuddhi-initiation, pratyaya-giving.
