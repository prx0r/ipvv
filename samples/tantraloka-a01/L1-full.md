# Tantrāloka Ahnika 1 — L1 controlled

## tal-a01-p01 (1.1-1.10)
tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṃ hṛdayamanuttarāmṛtakulaṃ mama sasphuratāt mātṛmānaprameyāṃśaśūlāmbujakṛtāspadām prāvṛṇmeghaghanavyomavidyullekhāvilāsinīm stājjñānaśūlaṃ satpakṣavipakṣotkartanakṣamam tadeva devītrayamantarāstāmanuttaraṃ me prathayatsvarūpam devīsuto gaṇapatiḥ sphuradindukāntiḥ samyaksamucchalayatānmama saṃvidabdhim kalombhitaṃ bāhyapathe cakāra stānme sa macchandavibhuḥ prasannaḥ pūrve jayanti guravo guruśāstrasindhukallolakelikalanāmalakarṇadhārāḥ tadaparamūrtirbhagavān maheśvaro bhūtirājaśca jayanti saṃvidāmodasandarbhā dikprasarpiṇaḥ

## tal-a01-p02 (1.11-1.20)

## tal-a01-p03 (1.21-1.30)

## tal-a01-p04 (1.31-1.40)
na mokṣo nāma tannāsya pṛthaṅnāmāpi gṛhyate taduttarottaraṃ jñānaṃ tattatsaṃsāraśāntidam itthaṃ samāsavyāsābhyāṃ jñānaṃ muñcati tāvataḥ amukta eva muktastu sarvāvacchedavarjitaḥ avacchedairna tatkutrāpyajñānaṃ satyamuktidam dvidhā pauruṣabauddhatvabhidoktaṃ śivaśāsane svapūrṇacitkriyārūpaśivatāvaraṇātmakam tadajñānaṃ na budghyaṃśo@dhyavasāyādyabhāvataḥ ṣaṭkañcukābilāṇūtthapratibimbanato yadā bauddhaṃ tasya ca tatpauṃsnaṃ poṣaṇīyaṃ ca poṣṭṛca

## tal-a01-p05 (1.41-1.50)

## tal-a01-p06 (1.51-1.61)

## tal-a01-p07 (1.62-1.71)

## tal-a01-p08 (1.72-1.81)

## tal-a01-p09 (1.82-1.91)

## tal-a01-p10 (1.92-1.101)

## tal-a01-p11 (1.102-1.111)

## tal-a01-p12 (1.112-1.121)

## tal-a01-p13 (1.122-1.131)

## tal-a01-p15 (1.143-1.152)

## tal-a01-p21 (1.203-1.212)
