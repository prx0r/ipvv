## tal-a01-p01 (1.1-1.10)
[and]-tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṃ hṛdayamanuttarāmṛtakulaṃ mama sasphuratāt mātṛmānaprameyāṃśaśūlāmbujakṛtāspadām prāvṛṇmeghaghanavyomavidyullekhāvilāsinīm stājjñānaśūlaṃ satpakṣavipakṣotkartanakṣamam tadeva devītrayamantarāstāmanuttaraṃ me prathayatsvarūpam devīsuto gaṇapatiḥ sphuradindukāntiḥ samyaksamucchalayatānmama saṃvidabdhim kalombhitaṃ bāhyapathe cakāra stānme sa macchandavibhuḥ prasannaḥ pūrve jayanti guravo guruśāstrasindhukallolakelikalanāmalakarṇadhārāḥ tadaparamūrtirbhagavān maheśvaro bhūtirājaśca jayanti saṃvidāmodasandarbhā dikprasarpiṇaḥ

## tal-a01-p02 (1.11-1.20)
[and]-gurorlakṣmaṇaguptasya nādasaṃmohinīṃ numaḥ sa śrīcukhulako diśyādiṣṭaṃ me gururuttamaḥ yadudīritaśāsanāṃśubhirme prakaṭo@yaṃ gahano@pi śāstramārgaḥ anuttaraṣaḍardhārthakrame tvekāpi nekṣyate arthito racaye spaṣṭāṃ pūrṇārthā prakriyāmimām bodhānyapāśaviṣanuttadupāsanotthabodhojjvalo@bhinavagupta idaṃ karoti devadevena nirdiṣṭaṃ svaśabdenātha liṅgataḥ tatsāraṃ trikaśāstraṃ hi tatsāraṃ mālinīmatam adṛṣṭa prakaṭīkurmo gurunāthājñayā vayam trinayanacaraṇasaroruhacintanalabdhaprasiddhiriti

## tal-a01-p03 (1.21-1.30)
[and]-abhinavaguptahṛdambujametadvicinuta maheśapūjanahetoḥ ajñānaṃ saṃsṛterheturjñānaṃ mokṣaikakāraṇam iti proktaṃ tathā va śrīmalinīvijayottare saṃbhāvanāṃ nirasyaitadabhāve mokṣamabravīt sa hi loṣṭādike@pyasti na ca tasyāsti saṃsṛtiḥ jñānameva tadajñānaṃ śivasūtreṣu bhāṣitam saṃśleṣetarayogāśyāmayamarthaḥ pradarśitaḥ anākṣipraviśeṣaṃ sadāha sūtre purātane bruvatā tasya cinmātrarūpasya dvaitamucyate tata eva samucchedyamityāvṛttyānirūpitam
