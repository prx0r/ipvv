# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 1

## tal-a01-p01–p04 — anuttara and jñāna/mukti
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka opens: supreme, abode of vimalakalā, abhinava-creation, great mother; full-bodied, five-faced-gupta, lovely father; couple-shining-state-emission-made, heart, unsurpassed-nectar-kula, may it shine. I bow to consciousness-pratibhā, goddess, supreme, Bhairava-yoginī; measureless, part, trident-lotus-made-abode. I bow to goddess abiding in body, of dancing Bhairava-form; rain-cloud-dense-sky, lightning-streak, lovely. Blazing-light-flame-burned-threefold-bond, shining; knowledge-trident, capable of cutting favorable/unfavorable. Guru Lakṣmaṇagupta, nāda-deluding, I bow; by teaching-rays, śāstra-path revealed. Abhinava's heart-lotus, examine this, for Maheśa-worship. Ignorance is cause of saṃsāra, knowledge is sole cause of liberation. Thus said, and in Śrīmalinīvijayottara. Possibility removed, without this, liberation declared. As in clod etc. Liberation not named, not separately named, not grasped. Higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra. By summary/expansion, knowledge frees that much. Unliberated indeed liberated, free from all limitation.

**FUNCTION.** Opens Tantrāloka: anuttara (couple-shining-state-emission-made, unsurpassed-nectar-kula); Śiva-Śakti as father/mother; consciousness-pratibhā as supreme Bhairava-yoginī; knowledge-trident cuts bonds; guru = Lakṣmaṇagupta; jñāna = sole cause of liberation; mukti = not separately named, higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra.

**KEY TERMS.** anuttara (unsurpassed), vimalakalā (vimalakalā), abhinavasṛṣṭi (abhinava-creation), yāmala (couple), visarga (emission), kula (kula), citpratibhā (consciousness-pratibhā), bhairavayoginī (Bhairava-yoginī), jñānaśūla (knowledge-trident), guru (guru), lakṣmaṇagupta (Lakṣmaṇagupta), nāda (nāda), śāstra (śāstra), jñāna (knowledge), mokṣa (liberation), mukti (mukti), avaccheda (limitation).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First four passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in four steps. (1) Anuttara: supreme, abode of vimalakalā, abhinava-creation, great mother; full-bodied, five-faced-gupta, lovely father; couple-shining-state-emission-made, heart, unsurpassed-nectar-kula, may it shine; I bow to consciousness-pratibhā, goddess, supreme, Bhairava-yoginī; measureless, part, trident-lotus-made-abode; I bow to goddess abiding in body, of dancing Bhairava-form; rain-cloud-dense-sky, lightning-streak, lovely; blazing-light-flame-burned-threefold-bond, shining; knowledge-trident, capable of cutting favorable/unfavorable. (2) Guru: guru Lakṣmaṇagupta, nāda-deluding, I bow; that Śrīcukhulaka, shown, my supreme guru; by whose teaching-rays, this deep śāstra-path revealed. (3) Jñāna: Abhinava's heart-lotus, examine this, for Maheśa-worship; ignorance is cause of saṃsāra, knowledge is sole cause of liberation; thus said, and in Śrīmalinīvijayottara; possibility removed, without this, liberation declared; as in clod etc. (4) Mukti: liberation not named, not separately named, not grasped; higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra; by summary/expansion, knowledge frees that much; unliberated indeed liberated, free from all limitation.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.40.

## tal-a01-p05–p09 — vijñāna and sādhana
**SUMMARY.** Vijñāna = blooming, personal, thought-free. That Buddhist, that personal, as before, nourished and nourisher. Yet, at body's end, that knowledge is clearly revealed. Then dissolves, jīvanmukti, in hand. Continues through sādhana, siddhi, fruit, close of ahnika 1.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes vijñāna (blooming, personal, thought-free) and continues through sādhana, siddhi, fruit, close.

**KEY TERMS.** vijñāna (higher knowledge), pauruṣa (personal), nirvikalpa (thought-free), jīvanmukti (liberated while living), sādhana (practice), siddhi (attainment).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p04; continues through ahnika 1.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves through vijñāna (blooming, personal, thought-free; Buddhist/personal as before, nourished and nourisher; at body's end, knowledge clearly revealed; then dissolves, jīvanmukti in hand) and continues through sādhana, siddhi, fruit, close of ahnika 1.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.41–1.90.
