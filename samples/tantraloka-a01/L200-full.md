# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 1 (331 verses, 34 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 1 (331 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) opening ahnika. It establishes: anuttara = couple-shining-state-emission-made, heart, unsurpassed-nectar-kula; Śiva-Śakti as father/mother; consciousness-pratibhā as supreme Bhairava-yoginī; knowledge-trident cuts bonds. Guru = Lakṣmaṇagupta, supreme; by teaching-rays, śāstra-path revealed. Jñāna = sole cause of liberation; ignorance = cause of saṃsāra. Mukti = not separately named; higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra; unliberated indeed liberated, free from all limitation. Vijñāna = blooming, personal, thought-free; at body's end, knowledge clearly revealed; then dissolves, jīvanmukti in hand. Continues through sādhana, siddhi, fruit, close of ahnika 1.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)

- C3: Knowledge-trident cuts bonds; guru = Lakṣmaṇagupta.
- C4: Jñāna = sole cause of liberation; ignorance = cause of saṃsāra.
- C5: Mukti = not separately named; higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra.
- C6: Vijñāna = blooming, personal, thought-free; jīvanmukti in hand.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Anuttara as non-couple.
- R2: Jñāna as non-cause of liberation.
- R3: Mukti as separately named.
