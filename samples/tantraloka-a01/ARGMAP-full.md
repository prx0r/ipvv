# Tantrāloka Ahnika 1 — ARGMAP

## tal-a01-p01 (1.1–1.10)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional/philosophical voice.
- question: What is the unsurpassed (anuttara)?
- premises: Vimalakalāśrayābhinavasṛṣṭimahā jananī (supreme, abode of vimalakalā, abhinava-creation, great mother). Bharitatanuśca pañcamukhaguptarucirjanakaḥ (full-bodied, five-faced-gupta, lovely father). Tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṃ hṛdayamanuttarāmṛtakulaṃ mama sasphuratāt (that both-couple-shining-state-emission-made, heart, unsurpassed-nectar-kula, may it shine for me). Naumi citpratibhāṃ devīṃ parāṃ bhairavayoginīm (I bow to consciousness-pratibhā, goddess, supreme, Bhairava-yoginī). Mātṛmānaprameyāṃśaśūlāmbujakṛtāspadām (measureless, part, trident-lotus-made-abode). Naumi devīṃ śarīrasthāṃ nṛtyato bhairavākṛte (I bow to goddess abiding in body, of dancing Bhairava-form). Prāvṛṇmeghaghanavyomavidyullekhāvilāsinīm (rain-cloud-dense-sky, lightning-streak, lovely). Dīptajyotiśchaṭāpluṣṭabhedabandhatrayaṃ sphurat (blazing-light-flame-burned-threefold-bond, shining). Stājjñānaśūlaṃ satpakṣavipakṣotkartanakṣamam (that knowledge-trident, capable of cutting favorable/unfavorable). Etc.
- inference: Anuttara = couple-shining-state-emission-made, heart, unsurpassed-nectar-kula; Śiva-Śakti as father/mother; consciousness-pratibhā as supreme Bhairava-yoginī; knowledge-trident cuts bonds.
- conclusion: Anuttara doctrine opened (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## tal-a01-p02 (1.11–1.20)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: What is the guru's role?
- premises: Gurorlakṣmaṇaguptasya nādasaṃmohinīṃ numaḥ (guru Lakṣmaṇagupta, nāda-deluding, I bow). Sa śrīcukhulako diśyādiṣṭaṃ me gururuttamaḥ (that Śrīcukhulaka, shown, my supreme guru). Yadudīritaśāsanāṃśubhirme prakaṭo 'yaṃ gahano 'pi śāstramārgaḥ (by whose teaching-rays, this deep śāstra-path, revealed to me). Anuttaraṣaḍardhārthakrame tvekāpi nekṣyate arthito r... (in unsurpassed-six-half-meaning-course, even one not seen, asked...).
- inference: Guru = Lakṣmaṇagupta, supreme; by teaching-rays, śāstra-path revealed; unsurpassed-six-half-meaning-course.
- conclusion: Guru doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## tal-a01-p03 (1.21–1.30)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: What is jñāna (knowledge)?
- premises: Abhinavaguptahṛdambujametadvicinuta maheśapūjanahetoḥ (Abhinava's heart-lotus, examine this, for Maheśa-worship). Ajñānaṃ saṃsṛterheturjñānaṃ mokṣaikakāraṇam (ignorance is cause of saṃsāra, knowledge is sole cause of liberation). Iti proktaṃ tathā va śrīmalinīvijayottare (thus said, and in Śrīmalinīvijayottara). Saṃbhāvanāṃ nirasyaitadabhāve mokṣamabravīt (possibility removed, without this, liberation declared). Sa hi loṣṭādike... (as in clod etc.).
- inference: Jñāna = sole cause of liberation; ignorance = cause of saṃsāra; without knowledge, no liberation; as in clod etc.
- conclusion: Jñāna doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## tal-a01-p04 (1.31–1.40)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: What is mukti (liberation)?
- premises: Na mokṣo nāma tannāsya pṛthaṅnāmāpi gṛhyate (liberation not named, not separately named, not grasped). Taduttarottaraṃ jñānaṃ tattatsaṃsāraśāntidam (that, higher and higher knowledge, that-saṃsāra-pacifying). Itthaṃ samāsavyāsābhyāṃ jñānaṃ muñcati tāvataḥ (thus by summary/expansion, knowledge frees that much). Amukta eva muktastu sarvāvacchedavarjitaḥ (unliberated indeed, liberated, free from all limitation). Avacchedairna tatkutrā... (by limitations, not that, where...).
- inference: Mukti = not separately named; higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra; by summary/expansion, knowledge frees; unliberated indeed liberated, free from all limitation.
- conclusion: Mukti doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## tal-a01-p05 (1.41–1.50)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: What is vijñāna (higher knowledge)?
- premises: Vikasvaraṃ tadvijñānaṃ pauruṣaṃ nirvikalpakam (blooming, that vijñāna, personal, thought-free). Tadbauddhaṃ yasya tatpauṃsnaṃ prāgvatpoṣyaṃ ca poṣṭṛ ca (that Buddhist, that personal, as before, nourished and nourisher). Tathāpi taccharīrānte tajjñānaṃ vyajyate sphuṭam (yet, at body's end, that knowledge is clearly revealed). Vilīyate tadā jīvanmuktiḥ karatale sthitā (then dissolves, jīvanmukti, in hand). Tenata... (therefore...).
- inference: Vijñāna = blooming, personal, thought-free; Buddhist/personal as before, nourished and nourisher; at body's end, knowledge clearly revealed; then dissolves, jīvanmukti in hand.
- conclusion: Vijñāna doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.
