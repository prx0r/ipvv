# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 7

## tal-a07-p01–p08 — rise of cakras and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 7: thus paramarahasya, this, cakras, said, abhyudaya. Thus effortless-said, yatnaja, tu, nigadyate. All, seed-piṇḍa-natured, consciousness, spanda-nature. Doing, para-saṃvitti, upāya, iti, varṇitam. As, ratha-ghaṭa-cakra-agra-ghaṭī-yantra-augha-vāhanam. By-one-connection-effort, citra, yantra-rise, bhajet. By-one-connection-strength, when, mantra-rise, aniśam. That-mantra-devatā, by-effort, tādātmya, prasīdati. In-sky, rasa, one-akṣi, nityottha, that-half, dvi-kapiṇḍake. In-trike, seven-thousand, two-hundred, udaya, mataḥ. In-four, thousand, five, ca, eva, four-hundred. In-five, ocean-thousand, three-hundred, twenty, tathā. In-six, thousand-three, six-hundred, udayo, bhavet. In-seven, three-thousand, tu, sixty-eight, adhikaṃ, smṛtam. By-hundreds, tu, twenty-seven... In-thirteen, sixty-two, hundred, kila, sixteen. Thirty-four, fifteen, iti, bhuvana-ārṇake. Fourteen-hundred, kha-abdhi, syāt, fifteen-varṇake. Thirteen-hundred, sārdha... By-sixteen-part-sthiti, subtle, ca, aro, abhilakṣyate. Thus, by-effort-restrained, prāṇa-course, of-yogin. Gradually, prāṇa-course, grāsa, eva, upajāyate. Prāṇa-grāsa-krama-avāpta... Vindānā, nirvikalpā, api, vikalpo, bhāva-gocare. Spanda-antaraṃ, na, yāvat, tad-uditaṃ, tāvat, eva, saḥ. Tāvān, eko, vikalpaḥ, syāt, vividhaṃ, vastu, kalpayan. Ye, tu, itthaṃ, na... Pada-mantra-akṣare, cakre, vibhāgaṃ, śakti-tattva-gam. Padeṣu, kṛtvā, mantrajñaḥ, japādau, phala-bhāg, bhavet. Dvi-tri-sapta-aṣṭa-saṃkhyātaṃ, lopayet, śakti-kodayam. Iti, śakt... Cāra-aṣṭa-bhāgāṃs, trīn, atra, kathayanti, adhikān, budhāḥ. Aṣṭāṣṭake, dvādaśite, pādārdhaṃ, viṃśatiṃ, vasūn. Udayaḥ, sapta-śatikā, sāṣṭā, ṣaṣṭiḥ, yato, hi, saḥ. Eṣa, cakroda... Viśet, cārdha-ardhika-yogāt, tad-ukta-ardha-udayo, bhavet. Yadā, pūrṇa-udaya-ātmā, tu, samaḥ, kālaḥ, trike, sphuret. Praveśa-viśrānti-ullāse, syāt, svatryaṃśa-udayaḥ, tadā. Etyeṣa, kā... Viśve, sṛṣṭi-layās, te, tu, citrā, vāyu-antara-kramāt. Ityeṣa, sūkṣma-parimarśana-śīlanīyaḥ, cakrodayo, 'nubhava-śāstra-dṛśā, mayā, uktaḥ.
