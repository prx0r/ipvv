# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 7 (71 verses, 8 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 7 (71 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on the rise (abhyudaya) of cakras. It establishes: thus paramarahasya, this, cakras, said, abhyudaya; thus effortless-said, yatnaja, tu, nigadyate; all, seed-piṇḍa-natured, consciousness, spanda-nature; doing, para-saṃvitti, upāya, iti, varṇitam; as, ratha-ghaṭa-cakra-agra-ghaṭī-yantra-augha-vāhanam; by-one-connection-effort, citra, yantra-rise, bhajet; by-one-connection-strength, when, mantra-rise, aniśam; that-mantra-devatā, by-effort, tādātmya, prasīdati; in-sky, rasa, one-akṣi, nityottha, that-half, dvi-kapiṇḍake; in-trike, seven-thousand, two-hundred, udaya, mataḥ; in-four, thousand, five, ca, eva, four-hundred; in-five, ocean-thousand, three-hundred, twenty, tathā; in-six, thousand-three, six-hundred, udayo, bhavet; in-seven, three-thousand, tu, sixty-eight, adhikaṃ, smṛtam; by-hundreds, tu, twenty-seven... In-thirteen, sixty-two, hundred, kila, sixteen; thirty-four, fifteen, iti, bhuvana-ārṇake; fourteen-hundred, kha-abdhi, syāt, fifteen-varṇake; thirteen-hundred, sārdha... By-sixteen-part-sthiti, subtle, ca, aro, abhilakṣyate; thus, by-effort-restrained, prāṇa-course, of-yogin; gradually, prāṇa-course, grāsa, eva, upajāyate; prāṇa-grāsa-krama-avāpta... Vindānā, nirvikalpā, api, vikalpo, bhāva-gocare; spanda-antaraṃ, na, yāvat, tad-uditaṃ, tāvat, eva, saḥ; tāvān, eko, vikalpaḥ, syāt, vividhaṃ, vastu, kalpayan; ye, tu, itthaṃ, na... Pada-mantra-akṣare, cakre, vibhāgaṃ, śakti-tattva-gam; padeṣu, kṛtvā, mantrajñaḥ, japādau, phala-bhāg, bhavet; dvi-tri-sapta-aṣṭa-saṃkhyātaṃ, lopayet, śakti-kodayam; iti, śakt... Cāra-aṣṭa-bhāgāṃs, trīn, atra, kathayanti, adhikān, budhāḥ; aṣṭāṣṭake, dvādaśite, pādārdhaṃ, viṃśatiṃ, vasūn; udayaḥ, sapta-śatikā, sāṣṭā, ṣaṣṭiḥ, yato, hi, saḥ; eṣa, cakroda... Viśve, sṛṣṭi-layās, te, tu, citrā, vāyu-antara-kramāt. Ityeṣa, sūkṣma-parimarśana-śīlanīyaḥ, cakrodayo, 'nubhava-śāstra-dṛśā, mayā, uktaḥ.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Rise (abhyudaya) of cakras: thus paramarahasya, this, cakras, said, abhyudaya.
- C2: Thus effortless-said, yatnaja, tu, nigadyate; all, seed-piṇḍa-natured, consciousness, spanda-nature.
- C3: Doing, para-saṃvitti, upāya, iti, varṇitam; as, ratha-ghaṭa-cakra-agra-ghaṭī-yantra-augha-vāhanam.
- C4: By-one-connection-effort, citra, yantra-rise, bhajet; by-one-connection-strength, when, mantra-rise, aniśam.
- C5: That-mantra-devatā, by-effort, tādātmya, prasīdati; in-sky, rasa, one-akṣi, nityottha, that-half, dvi-kapiṇḍake.
- C6: In-trike, seven-thousand, two-hundred, udaya, mataḥ; in-four, thousand, five, ca, eva, four-hundred.
- C7: In-five, ocean-thousand, three-hundred, twenty, tathā; in-six, thousand-three, six-hundred, udayo, bhavet.
- C8: In-seven, three-thousand, tu, sixty-eight, adhikaṃ, smṛtam; by-hundreds, tu, twenty-seven...
- C9: In-thirteen, sixty-two, hundred, kila, sixteen; thirty-four, fifteen, iti, bhuvana-ārṇake.
