# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 12

## tal-a12-p01–p02 — application of adhvā and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 12: thus application of adhvā is revealed. Thus adhvā entire as in consciousness established. By that, empty-intellect, prāṇa, nāḍī, cakra, body. Outside, liṅga, icon, fire, sthaṇḍila, etc., everywhere. So established, entire and scattered, by gradual and non-gradual. From consciousness-tattva to external, this adhvā established. There, there, fitting form, own, by autonomy, reveals. All, everywhere, form, and his also not not appears. Not limitation anywhere, in dream even, we bear. Thus world-adhva-full, time-activity-painted. Place-time-maya-spanda-abode, body, observe. Thus observed, he, world-internal-deity-made. Meditated, worshipped, tarpya, then entered, liberated. Thus pot, cloth, liṅga, sthaṇḍila, book, water. Whatever, wherever, sees, there, tanmayatā, go. There, offering of things, by nondifference, worship, held. Thus by full-form-investigation, meditation, called. By fullness-investigation, imperishable, firmness, bringing. Thus internal-japa-yoga, examining, japa-vessel. There, of offered beings, own-difference-dissolution. Doing, that-ray-true-being, give, homa-action. Thus doing, all, equal-vision, seeing. Imperishable, vow, pure, equality, Nandiśikhodita. Thus arcana-japa-dhyāna-homa-vrata-rule-course. Full, state, said, samādhi, gurus, before. Here, in arcana-japa-etc, external-internal-two-abiding. In substance-mass, no rule, no prohibition. Thought-purity-sandhyā-etc, no use, here, any. Said, Śrī-Trika-sūtra, ca, arises, worship, regarding. Ignorant-of-rules, knower-of-rules, etc., well-expanded. When, how, by-what, where, own, consciousness, pleased. Then, thus, by-that, there, that-enjoyable, rule, and, he. Laukika-alaukika, all, by-that, here, employ. In-imperishable, trembling, but, trembling, reduce, by-strength. As, by-what-means, gradually, non-gradually, or. Doubt, dissolves, end, thus, effortful, become. Intellect-action-eye-gone, goddesses, by-forbidden, tarpyet. Vīra-vrata, ca, abhinandet, thus, Bharga-śikhā-vacaḥ. Thus, hi, doubt, impurity, languor, contraction, etc. Saṃsāra-prison-antah, gross-pillar, like-ghaṭa. Mantras, varṇa-nature, ye, dravya, what, five-elemental. What consciousness-self, being-born, there, what, mixture, how. Mixture-absence, what, doubt, in-that, also, clear. Not, doubt, thus, doubt, dissolves, by-neglect. Śrī-sarvācāra-vīrālī, night-walking-course, etc. In-śāstras, spread, this, there, there, said, because. By-doubt, arises, languor, by-doubt, obstacle-becomes. Said, Utpaladeva, ca, Śrī, our-guru's-guru. All-doubt-destroyer, path, bow, to-Maheśvara, tv, iti. Anuttara-padapraptaye, tad, idam, āṇavaṃ, darśita, abhyupāya, ativistarāt, nanu, vidāṃ, kurudhvam, budhāḥ.
