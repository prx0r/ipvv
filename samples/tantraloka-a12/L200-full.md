# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 12 (26 verses, 2 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Application of adhvā is revealed; adhvā entire as in consciousness established.
- C2: By that, empty-intellect, prāṇa, nāḍī, cakra, body; outside, liṅga, icon, fire, sthaṇḍila, etc., everywhere.
- C3: So established, entire and scattered, by gradual and non-gradual; from consciousness-tattva to external, this adhvā established.
- C4: There, there, fitting form, own, by autonomy, reveals; all, everywhere, form, and his also not not appears.
- C5: Not limitation anywhere, in dream even, we bear; thus world-adhva-full, time-activity-painted.
- C6: Place-time-maya-spanda-abode, body, observe; thus observed, he, world-internal-deity-made.
- C7: Meditated, worshipped, tarpya, then entered, liberated; thus pot, cloth, liṅga, sthaṇḍila, book, water.
- C8: Whatever, wherever, sees, there, tanmayatā, go; there, offering of things, by nondifference, worship, held.
- C9: Thus by full-form-investigation, meditation, called; by fullness-investigation, imperishable, firmness, bringing.
