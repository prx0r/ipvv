# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 11 (118 verses, 12 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 11 (118 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on kalādhvā (the kalā path). It establishes: kalādhvā, vakṣyate, Śrīmat-Śāṃbhava-ājñā-anusārataḥ. Yathā, pūrvokta-bhuvana-madhye, nijanijaṃ, gaṇam. Anuyatparato, bhinnaṃ, tattvaṃ, nāma, iti, bhaṇyate. Tathā, teṣu, api, tattveṣu, svavarga-anugama-ātmakam. Vyāvṛttaṃ, paravargāt, ca, kaleti, śivaśāsane. Kecid, āhuḥ, punar, yā, asau, śaktir, antaḥ, susūkṣmikā. Tattvānāṃ, sā, kaleti, uktā, dharaṇyāṃ, dhārikā, yathā. Atra, pakṣadvaye, vastu, na, bhinnaṃ, bhāsate, yataḥ. Anugāmi, na, sāmānya... (continued through 118 verses). Close.
