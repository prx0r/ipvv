# L200 — Vijñānabhairava (161 verses, 17 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Vijñānabhairava (161 verses, 112 dhāraṇās) is a Kashmir Śaiva tantra. Devī asks: what is Bhairava's true form? Made of word-group and kalā? By nine-ātman division? By three-head division? Made of three śaktis? Made of nāda-bindu? Half-moon obstructions? Wheel-mounted, anacka? Bhairava answers with 112 dhāraṇās (practices): breath, nāda, bindu, cakra, khecarī, etc. Each dhāraṇā is a practice for attaining Bhairava-state. Close: colophon.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Devī's question about Bhairava's true form (śabdarāśi/kalā, navātman, triśira, śaktitraya, nādabindu, candrārdha, cakrārūḍha, anacka).
- C2: 112 dhāraṇās (practices) for attaining Bhairava-state.
- C3: Each dhāraṇā is a practice (breath, nāda, bindu, cakra, khecarī, etc.).
- C4: Close = colophon.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Bhairava's form as non-practice.
- R2: Dhāraṇās as non-effective.
