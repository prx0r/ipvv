# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 30 (123 verses, 13 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 30 (123 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on mantrakadambaka (mantra cluster). It establishes: thus mantrakadambaka, trika-kula-krama-yogi, nirūpyate. Tāvat, vimarśānārūḍha-dhiyāṃ, tāt-siddhaye, kramāt. Pratibuddhā, hi, te, mantrā, vimarśaika-svabhāvakāḥ. Svatantrasya, eva, ciddhāmnaḥ, svātantryāt, kartṛtāmayāḥ. Yam, āviśanti, ca, ācāryaṃ, taṃ, tādātmya-nirūḍhitaḥ. Svatantrīkurvate, yānti, karaṇāny, api, kartṛtām. Ādhāra-śaktau, hrīṃ, pṛthvī-prabhṛtau, tu, catuṣṭaye. Kṣlāṃ, kṣvīṃ, vaṃ, kṣam, iti, prāhuḥ, kramād, varṇa-catuṣṭayam. (Continued through 123 verses.) ... (close).
