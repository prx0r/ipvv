# Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi — ARGMAP

## ajad-p01 (1–9)
- speaker: Utpaladeva (pūrvapakṣa + vṛtti).
- question: Is the knower (pramātṛ) inert (jaḍa) or non-inert (ajaḍa)?
- premises: Jadājaḍānām apy evaṃ nāsty asāv iti niścayaḥ (even of inert/non-inert, thus it is not, this is certain). Naitat svarūpaṃ spṛśati vastuno jñātṛgāmy adaḥ (this own-nature does not touch the thing, knower-goable). Parāpekṣitayā naikarūpatve sattvalaṅghanāt (by dependence on other, not one-form, by surpassing being). Siddhyaty eva viśeṣo 'tra tāvatā ca prayojanam (distinction is accomplished here, by that much purpose). Tadapekṣāṃ vinā bhāvābhāvayor aviśiṣṭatā kuryān nānyas (without that dependence, being/non-being indistinguishable, no other).
- inference: The knower is non-inert (ajaḍa): inert/non-inert distinction collapses without the knower's dependence; distinction accomplished by the knower.
- conclusion: Knower is ajaḍa (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ajad-p02 (10–18)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: How does the knower abide?
- premises: Katham anyasya bhāvasya pratiṣṭhāspadatām iyāt (how would another thing attain support-status). Pratiṣṭhātmakatāṃ gacched aham ity ātmanā punaḥ (attains support-nature, "I" by self again). Vyavasthitiḥ pratiṣṭhā ca siddhir nirvṛttir ucyate (establishment, support, attainment, completion are said). Jaḍāḥ prakāśa evāsti svātmanaḥ svaparātmabhiḥ (inert ones are indeed light, of self, by own/other selves). Muktvānvayasya prakāśasya vimarśarahitātmanaḥ yā... (except connected light, of reflection-less self...).
- inference: The knower abides as "I" by self; inert ones are light of self; establishment/support/attainment/completion are said; reflection-less self is different.
- conclusion: Knower abides as "I" (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ajad-p03 (19–27)
- speaker: Utpaladeva (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Tat prāṇādi prameyaṃ syān na pramātānyato bhramāt (that, breath etc., is knowable, not knower, from delusion). Jīve niruddhā tatrāpi paramātmani sā sthitā (in jīva, restrained, there too, in paramātman, it abides). Sādhitā pratyabhijñāyām akhyāter jīvatā param (established in recognition, beyond non-manifestation, jīva's param). Prakāśasyātmaviśrāntir ahaṃbhāvo hi kīrtitaḥ (light's self-rest, "I"-ness, indeed proclaimed). Svātantryam atha kartṛtvaṃ mukhyam īśvaratāpi ca (autonomy, then agency, chief, īśvaratā too). Tadakh... (that...).
- inference: Close = breath etc. are knowable, not knower; in jīva, restrained, abides in paramātman; established in recognition; light's self-rest = "I"-ness; autonomy, agency, īśvaratā.
- conclusion: The text closes with autonomy/īśvaratā.
- unresolved: none.
