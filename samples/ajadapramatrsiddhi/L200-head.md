# L200 — Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi (27 verses, 3 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi (27 verses) is Utpaladeva's (10th c.) work establishing the knower (pramātṛ) as non-inert (ajaḍa). It argues: inert/non-inert distinction collapses without the knower's dependence; distinction accomplished by the knower. The knower abides as "I" by self; inert ones are light of self; establishment/support/attainment/completion are said. Close: breath etc. are knowable, not knower, from delusion; in jīva, restrained, abides in paramātman; established in recognition; light's self-rest = "I"-ness; autonomy, agency, īśvaratā.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Knower is ajaḍa (non-inert): inert/non-inert distinction collapses without the knower's dependence.
- C2: Distinction accomplished by the knower; without that dependence, being/non-being indistinguishable.
- C3: Knower abides as "I" by self; inert ones are light of self.
- C4: Establishment/support/attainment/completion are said.
- C5: Close: breath etc. are knowable, not knower; in jīva, restrained, abides in paramātman; light's self-rest = "I"-ness; autonomy, agency, īśvaratā.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Knower as inert (jaḍa).
- R2: Inert/non-inert distinction without knower.
