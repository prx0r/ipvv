# C1 — Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi

## ajad-p01–p03 — knower and close
**SUMMARY.** Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi establishes the knower (pramātṛ) as non-inert (ajaḍa). Even of inert/non-inert, thus it is not, this is certain. This own-nature does not touch the thing, knower-goable. By dependence on other, not one-form, by surpassing being. Distinction is accomplished here, by that much purpose. Without that dependence, being/non-being indistinguishable, no other. How would another thing attain support-status. Attains support-nature, "I" by self again. Establishment, support, attainment, completion are said. Inert ones are indeed light, of self, by own/other selves. Except connected light, of reflection-less self. That, breath etc., is knowable, not knower, from delusion. In jīva, restrained, there too, in paramātman, it abides. Established in recognition, beyond non-manifestation, jīva's param. Light's self-rest, "I"-ness, indeed proclaimed. Autonomy, then agency, chief, īśvaratā too.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the knower as non-inert (ajaḍa): inert/non-inert distinction collapses without the knower's dependence; the knower abides as "I" by self; close with autonomy/agency/īśvaratā.

**KEY TERMS.** ajaḍa (non-inert), pramātṛ (knower), pratiṣṭhā (support), prakāśa (light), vimarśa (reflection), paramātman (paramātman), svātantrya (autonomy), kartṛtva (agency), īśvaratā (īśvaratā).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Entire text; 3 passages.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Knower is ajaḍa: even of inert/non-inert, thus it is not; this own-nature does not touch the thing, knower-goable; by dependence on other, not one-form, by surpassing being; distinction accomplished here; without that dependence, being/non-being indistinguishable. (2) Knower abides as "I": how would another thing attain support-status; attains support-nature, "I" by self again; establishment/support/attainment/completion are said; inert ones are indeed light of self; except connected light, of reflection-less self. (3) Close: breath etc. are knowable, not knower, from delusion; in jīva, restrained, abides in paramātman; established in recognition; light's self-rest = "I"-ness; autonomy, agency, īśvaratā.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.
