# L200 — Sambandhasiddhi (21 verses, 3 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Sambandhasiddhi (21 verses) is a Kashmir Śaiva work on relation (saṃbandha). It examines: what is the meaning of "relation" — not explained by conjunction/contact/union. Relation operates by restraining activity from other thought; if touching other thought, mother-difference arises; by one-reflection, one-word-superimposition, many become one-meaning; relation is for help. Close: by relation-course, Māyā-knower-established; intellects of varied-meaning-view cause transaction; not all deluded every moment; established in infinite consciousness-reflection, called self-awareness; self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Saṃbandha = relation; word-meaning not explained by conjunction/contact/union.
- C2: World-self light supreme Lord victorious.
- C3: Relation operates by restraining activity from other thought; if touching other thought, mother-difference arises.
- C4: By one-reflection, one-word-superimposition, many become one-meaning; relation is for help.
- C5: Close = by relation-course, Māyā-knower-established; self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Relation as mere conjunction/contact/union.
- R2: Activity touching other thought (leads to mother-difference).
