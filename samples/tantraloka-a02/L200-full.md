# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 2 (50 verses, 5 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 2 (50 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on anupāya (no-means). It establishes: that first pad, unceasing, unsurpassed-knowledge-form. To determine that, this prakaraṇa, I begin, second. Anupāya, what form, what meaning, deśanā, then, vai. Once, deśanā, then, anupāyatva, said. Anupāya, this, tattva, thus, upāya, without, how. By-themselves, their, such, what, say, indeed, to-them. What fourfold-said, form, of-knowledge, of-vibhu, asau. Nature, indeed, to-be-thought, he, indeed, nityodita, vibhu. By-these, innumerable, natures, what, shines. Some, part-by-part, by-that, enter, others, without-part. There, also, by-upāya-etc-dependence, otherness-yoga. Of-upāya, also, not, to-be-prevented, that-otherness, variety. There, who, pure-selves, Bhairava, own-consciousness. Without-upāya, meditating, that-rule, praṇigadyate. There, tāvat, kriyā-yoga, not, upāyatva, deserves. He, hi, from-that, arisen, pratyuta, revealed. In-knowledge, upāya, indeed, syāt, iti, ce, knowledge, said. Prakāśatva, self-shining, tat, ca, there, other, how. (Continued through 50 verses covering anupāya doctrine.)

- C1: Anupāya = no-means: first pad, unceasing, unsurpassed-knowledge-form.
- C2: To determine that, this prakaraṇa, I begin, second.
- C3: Anupāya, what form, what meaning, deśanā, then, vai.
- C4: Once, deśanā, then, anupāyatva, said.
- C5: Anupāya, this, tattva, thus, upāya, without, how.
- C6: By-themselves, their, such, what, say, indeed, to-them.
- C7: What fourfold-said, form, of-knowledge, of-vibhu, asau.
- C8: Nature, indeed, to-be-thought, he, indeed, nityodita, vibhu.
