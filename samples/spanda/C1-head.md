# C1 — Spandakārikā

## span-p01–p02 — spanda and its operation
**SUMMARY.** Spandakārikā opens: to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise. Where all effect abides, from which it proceeds; by His uncovered nature, no obstruction anywhere. Though waking etc. differ, it flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature, from the knower. I am happy/sad/attached etc. consciousnesses; happiness etc. states, strung through, clearly abide elsewhere. Where no suffering/pleasure, no graspable/grasper, no delusion; that exists in supreme truth. Regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course. Non-being does not become being; nor is there delusion there; from contact with effort, "it was then" is determination. Therefore that is artificial, to be known, like deep sleep always; but thus, being remembered, that tattva is not attained.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes spanda = Śiva's opening/closing (unmeṣa/nimeṣa) = source of śakti-wheel; flows undivided; supreme truth beyond suffering/pleasure; spanda operates through presiding over own-nature.

**KEY TERMS.** spanda (vibration), unmeṣa (opening), nimeṣa (closing), śakticakra (śakti-wheel), śaṅkara (Śaṅkara), pralaya (dissolution), udaya (arising), paramārtha (supreme truth), adhiṣṭhātṛ (presiding), kusṛti (evil course).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Spanda: to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise; where all effect abides, from which it proceeds; by uncovered nature, no obstruction; though waking etc. differ, flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature; I am happy/sad/attached etc.; happiness etc. states strung through abide elsewhere; where no suffering/pleasure/grasper/delusion, that exists in supreme truth. (2) Operation: regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course; non-being does not become being; nor delusion there; from contact with effort, "it was then" is determination; therefore artificial, to be known, like deep sleep; being remembered, tattva not attained.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.
