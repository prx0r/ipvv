## span-p01 (2-10)
[and]-yatra sthitam idaṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ yasmāc ca nirgataṃ | tasyānāvṛtarūpatvān na nirodho 'sti kutracit jāgradādi vibhede 'pi tadabhinne prasarpati | nivartate nijānnaiva svabhāvād upalabdhṛtaḥ ahaṃ sukhī ca duḥkhī ca raktaś ca ityadisamvidaḥ | sukhādyavasthānusyūte vartante 'nyatra tāḥ sphuṭam na duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ yatra na grāhyam grāhakaṃ na ca | na cāsti mūḍhabhāvo 'pi tad asti paramārthataḥ yataḥ karaṇavargo 'yaṃ vimūḍho 'mūḍhavat svayam | sahāntareṇa cakreṇa pravṛttisthitisaṃhṛtiḥ labhate tatprayatnena parīkṣyaṃ tattvam ādarāt | yataḥ svatantratā tasya sarvatreyam akṛtrimā na hīcchānodanasyāyam prerakatvena vartate | api tv ātmabalasparśāt puruṣas tatsamo bhavet nijāśuddhyāsamarthasya kartavyeṣv abhilāṣiṇaḥ | yadā kṣobhaḥ pralīyeta tadā syāt paramam padam tadāsyākṛtrimo dharmo jñatvakartṛtvalakṣaṇah | yatas tadepsitaṃ sarvaṃ jānāti ca karoti ca

## span-p02 (11-19)
[and]-tam adhiṣṭhātṛbhāvena svabhāvam avalokayan | smayamāna ivāste yas tasyeyaṃ kusṛtiḥ kutaḥ nābhāvo bhāvyatām eti na ca tatrāsty amūḍhatā | yato 'bhiyogasaṃsparśāt tadāsid iti niścayah atas tatkṛtrimaṃ jñeyaṃ sauṣuptapadavat sadā | na tv evaṃ smaryamāṇatvaṃ tat tattvam pratipadyate avasthāyugalaṃ cātra kāryakartṛtvaśabditam | kāryatā kṣayiṇī tatra kartṛtvam punar akṣayam kāryonmukhaḥ prayatno yaḥ kevalaṃ so 'tra lupyate | tasmin lupte vilupto 'smīty abudhaḥ pratipadyate na tu yo 'ntarmukho bhāvah sarvajñātvaguṇāspadam | tasya lopaḥ kadācit syād anyasyānupalambhanāt tasyopalabdhiḥ satataṃ tripadāvyabhicāriṇī | nityaṃ syāt suprabuddhasya tadādyante parasya tu jñānajñeyasvarūpiṇyā śaktyā paramayā yutaḥ | padadvaye vibhur bhāti tadanyatra tu cinmayaḥ guṇādispandaniḥṣyandāḥ sāmānyaspandasaṃśrayāt | labdhātmalābhaḥ satataṃ syur jñasyāparipanthinaḥ

## span-p03 (20-3)
[and]-aprabudhadhiyas tv ete svasthitisthaganodyatāḥ | pātayanti duruttāre ghore saṃsāravartmani ataḥ satatam udyuktaḥ spandatattvaviviktaye | jāgrad eva nijam bhāvam acireṇādhigacchati atikruddhaḥ prahṛṣṭo vā kiṃ karomīti vā mṛśan | dhāvan va yat padaṃ gacchet tatra spandaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ yām avasthāṃ samālambya yad ayam mama vakṣyati | tadavaśyaṃ kariṣye 'ham iti saṃkalpya tiṣṭhati tām aśrityordhvamārgeṇa candrasūryāv ubhāv api | sauṣumne 'dhvanyastamito hitvā brahmā.ṅdagocaram tadā tasmin mahāvyomni pralīnaśaśibhāskare | sauṣuptapadavan mūḍhaḥ prabuddhaḥ syād anāvṛtaḥ dvitīyo niḥṣyandaḥ tadākramya balam mantrāḥ sarvajñābalaśālinaḥ | pravartante 'dhikārāya karaṇānīva dehinām tatraiva sampralīyante śāntarūpā nirañjanāḥ | sahārādhakacittena tenaite śivadharmiṇaḥ yasmāt sarvamayo jīvaḥ sarvabhāvasamudbhavāt | tatsaṃvedanarūpeṇa tādātmyapratipattitaḥ
