# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 18

## tal-a18-p01–p02 — abbreviated initiation and rule
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 18: thus abbreviated initiation, Śivahood-attainment, is said. No rajas, no adhivāsa, no bhū-kṣetra possession. Wherever place, having worshipped, guru, Śiva. Path by mind meditated, initiate, tattva-knower. From birth etc. devoid, by which by which path, guru. Does, he, one-tattva-ending, Śiva-state-single. Supreme-mantra; svāhā per tattva; full offering; by śakti hom. Initiation-act said by Maheśin. Rule: per mātṛkā-pair varṇas, purify tattvas; or by piṇḍa-mantra; guru does abbreviated karma, not otherwise; Śrībrahmayāmala, in-abbreviated, meditate; pervasion of all paths common, but in-yāga not expansion; abbreviated rule said by Śiva's grace.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes abbreviated initiation (saṃkṣiptadīkṣā): no rajas/adhivāsa/bhū-kṣetra; wherever place, worship Śiva; path meditated by mind; tattva-knower; Śiva-state-single; supreme-mantra; svāhā per tattva; full offering; by śakti hom; rule: per mātṛkā-pair varṇas, purify tattvas; or by piṇḍa-mantra; guru does abbreviated karma, not otherwise; Śrībrahmayāmala, in-abbreviated, meditate; pervasion of all paths common, but in-yāga not expansion; abbreviated rule said by Śiva's grace.
