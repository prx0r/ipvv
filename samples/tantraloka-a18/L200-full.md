# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 18 (11 verses, 2 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 18 (11 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on abbreviated initiation (saṃkṣiptadīkṣā). It establishes: abbreviated initiation, Śivahood-attainment, is said. No rajas, no adhivāsa, no bhū-kṣetra possession. Wherever place, having worshipped, guru, Śiva. Path by mind meditated, initiate, tattva-knower. From birth etc. devoid, by which by which path, guru. Does, he, one-tattva-ending, Śiva-state-single. Supreme-mantra; svāhā per tattva; full offering; by śakti hom. Initiation-act said by Maheśin. Rule: per mātṛkā-pair varṇas, purify tattvas; or by piṇḍa-mantra; guru does abbreviated karma, not otherwise; Śrībrahmayāmala, in-abbreviated, meditate; pervasion of all paths common, but in-yāga not expansion; abbreviated rule said by Śiva's grace.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Abbreviated initiation = no rajas/adhivāsa/bhū-kṣetra; wherever place, worship Śiva.
- C2: Path by mind meditated, initiate, tattva-knower; Śiva-state-single.
- C3: Supreme-mantra; svāhā per tattva; full offering; by śakti hom.
- C4: Rule: per mātṛkā-pair varṇas, purify tattvas; or by piṇḍa-mantra.
- C5: Guru does abbreviated karma, not otherwise; Śrībrahmayāmala, in-abbreviated, meditate.
- C6: Pervasion of all paths common, but in-yāga not expansion.
- C7: Abbreviated rule said by Śiva's grace; after-initiation, in-kairaṇa, in-śāsana.
