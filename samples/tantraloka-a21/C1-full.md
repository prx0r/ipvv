# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 21

## tal-a21-p01–p07 — parokṣadīkṣā (indirect initiation) and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 21: thus indirect-initiation, karma, said. Bhukti-mukti-prasiddhi-artha, nīyate, sadguru, prati. Ityasmin, Mālinī-vākye, pratiḥ, sāṃmukhya-āvācakaḥ. Sāṃmukhyaṃ, ca, asya, śiṣyasya, tatkṛpāspadatātmakam. Tam, ārādhyeti, vacanaṃ, kṛpā-hetu-upalakṣaṇam. Tatsaṃbandhāt, tataḥ, kaścit, tatkṣaṇāt, apavṛjyate. Ityasya, ayam, api, hi, arthaḥ, mālinī-vākya-sanmaṇeḥ. Tatkṣaṇāt, iti, nāsya, asti, yiyāsādi-kṣaṇāntaram. Kiṃ tu, evam, eva, karuṇā... Guru, dīkṣāṃ, mṛtoddhārīṃ, kurvīta, śivadāyinīm. Śrī-Mṛtyuñjaya-siddhādau, tad, uktaṃ, parameśinā. Adīkṣite, nṛpatyādāv, alase, patite. Yena, saṃdṛṣṭamātre, siddha-mātra-pada-dvayāt. Ākṛtiḥ, dīpta-rūpā, yā, mantraḥ, tadvat, sudīptikaḥ. Śiṣṭaṃ, spaṣṭam, ato, neha, kathitaṃ. Adhikāri-śarīratvāt, mānuṣye, tu, śarīragaḥ. Na, tadā, mucyate, dehāt, dehānte, tu, śivaṃ, vrajet. Tasmin, dehe, tu, kāpy, asya, jāyate, śāṅk... Sapratyayā, tv, iyaṃ, yatra, spandate, darbhajā, tanuḥ. Tatra, prāṇa-mantra-arpaṇa-yogāt, tathā, bhavet. Sābhyāsasya, tad, api, uktaṃ, ba... Agniḥ, ciṭiciṭā-śabdaṃ, sadhūmaṃ, pratimuñcati. Dhatte, nīlāmbuda-cchāyāṃ, muhur, jvalati, śāmyati. Vistaro, ghorarūpaś, ca, mahīṃ, dhāv... Nirayaṃ, varjayet, tasmāt, iti, dīkṣottare, vidhiḥ. Eṣā, parokṣadīkṣā, dvidhoditā, jīvaditarabhedena.
