# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 21 (61 verses, 7 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 21 (61 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on parokṣadīkṣā (indirect initiation). It establishes: thus indirect-initiation, karma, said; bhukti-mukti-prasiddhi-artha, nīyate, sadguru, prati; ityasmin, Mālinī-vākye, pratiḥ, sāṃmukhya-āvācakaḥ; sāṃmukhyaṃ, ca, asya, śiṣyasya, tatkṛpāspadatātmakam; tam, ārādhyeti, vacanaṃ, kṛpā-hetu-upalakṣaṇam; tatsaṃbandhāt, tataḥ, kaścit, tatkṣaṇāt, apavṛjyate; ityasya, ayam, api, hi, arthaḥ, mālinī-vākya-sanmaṇeḥ; tatkṣaṇāt, iti, nāsya, asti, yiyāsādi-kṣaṇāntaram; kiṃ tu, evam, eva, karuṇā... Guru, dīkṣāṃ, mṛtoddhārīṃ, kurvīta, śivadāyinīm; Śrī-Mṛtyuñjaya-siddhādau, tad, uktaṃ, parameśinā; adīkṣite, nṛpatyādāv, alase, patite. Yena, saṃdṛṣṭamātre, siddha-mātra-pada-dvayāt; ākṛtiḥ, dīpta-rūpā, yā, mantraḥ, tadvat, sudīptikaḥ; śiṣṭaṃ, spaṣṭam, ato, neha, kathitaṃ. Adhikāri-śarīratvāt, mānuṣye, tu, śarīragaḥ; na, tadā, mucyate, dehāt, dehānte, tu, śivaṃ, vrajet; tasmin, dehe, tu, kāpy, asya, jāyate, śāṅk... Sapratyayā, tv, iyaṃ, yatra, spandate, darbhajā, tanuḥ; tatra, prāṇa-mantra-arpaṇa-yogāt, tathā, bhavet; sābhyāsasya, tad, api, uktaṃ, ba... Agniḥ, ciṭiciṭā-śabdaṃ, sadhūmaṃ, pratimuñcati; dhatte, nīlāmbuda-cchāyāṃ, muhur, jvalati, śāmyati; vistaro, ghorarūpaś, ca, mahīṃ, dhāv... Nirayaṃ, varjayet, tasmāt, iti, dīkṣottare, vidhiḥ; eṣā, parokṣadīkṣā, dvidhoditā, jīvaditarabhedena.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Parokṣadīkṣā: thus indirect-initiation, karma, said.
- C2: Bhukti-mukti-prasiddhi-artha, nīyate, sadguru, prati.
- C3: Ityasmin, Mālinī-vākye, pratiḥ, sāṃmukhya-āvācakaḥ.
- C4: Sāṃmukhyaṃ, ca, asya, śiṣyasya, tatkṛpāspadatātmakam.
- C5: Tam, ārādhyeti, vacanaṃ, kṛpā-hetu-upalakṣaṇam.
- C6: Tatsaṃbandhāt, tataḥ, kaścit, tatkṣaṇāt, apavṛjyate.
- C7: Ityasya, ayam, api, hi, arthaḥ, mālinī-vākya-sanmaṇeḥ.
- C8: Tatkṣaṇāt, iti, nāsya, asti, yiyāsādi-kṣaṇāntaram.
- C9: Kiṃ tu, evam, eva, karuṇā...
