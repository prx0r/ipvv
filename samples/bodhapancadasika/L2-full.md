# Bodhapañcadaśikā — L2 READ

## bod-p01–p06 — the one reality and its play
Un-set sun-light-form of powers and darknesses too — who is one within, whose end are powers and darknesses. He alone is own-nature of all beings, supreme Lord; the host of things is of him alone power, Lordship-made. Power too from power-possessor-form difference does not desire; identity of these two is eternal, as fire and burning. He alone is Bhairava god, world-bearing-characterized; in own-self-vision whole is reflected by power. Of him alone is supreme goddess, own-nature-grasping-eager; fullness in all beings, neither little nor excess. This god by this goddess always play-joy-eager; varied creation-dissolution he does simultaneously, all-pervading. The stotra opens with the one reality: Bhairava as own-nature of all beings, with his power (śakti) eternally identical to him (like fire and burning), reflecting the whole world in self-vision, with the supreme goddess as own-nature-grasping-eager fullness — and his play is simultaneous creation and dissolution, all-pervading.

## bod-p07–p09 — autonomy, inertness, and creation
Hard-to-accomplish of it is unsurpassed indeed; that itself is autonomy, lordship, awareness-form. Limited-light-ness of inert indeed is mark; from inert different is awareness since not measured. Thus of it autonomous by own-power-distinction — own-self creation-dissolution as nature stand. Autonomy is the unsurpassed; it is lordship and awareness-form. Inertness is limited light; awareness is unmeasured, different from inert. Creation and dissolution are the autonomous self's own nature — not effects of an external cause.

## bod-p10–p11 — variety and non-knowledge
In them variety extreme up-down across what; worlds, their parts, pleasure-pain-mind what. What of it non-knowledge — that its autonomy thus stated; it indeed saṃsāra of fools who is terrifying. Extreme variety in worlds, parts, and minds; non-knowledge of this autonomy is saṃsāra — the terrifying state of fools.

## bod-p12–p14 — attainment, liberation, and non-difference
From that grace-sap or from teacher-scripture or from scripture of supreme Lord from which from which approached. What of reality full-knowledge — that is liberation from supreme Lord. That fullness of awakened, living-liberation that is remembered. These two bondage and liberation from supreme-Lord-nature. Not they differ; no difference indeed in truth in supreme Lord. Three paths to the reality (grace, teacher-scripture, Lord's scripture); full knowledge of reality = liberation = living liberation; bondage and liberation are not ultimately different in the Lord.

## bod-p15 — Bhairava as own-nature (close)
Thus will-power art knowledge power trident lotus resorted — Bhairava of all things own-nature is examined. The Lord (Bhairava) is the own-nature of all things, resorted to by will, art, knowledge, power (trident-lotus). The stotra closes with Bhairava = svabhāva.
