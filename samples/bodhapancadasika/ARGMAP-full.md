# Bodhapañcadaśikā — ARGMAP

## bod-p01 (1)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the one reality?
- premises: Un-set-sun-light-form of powers and darknesses; who is one within, whose end are powers and darknesses.
- inference: One reality encompasses both light and darkness.
- conclusion: One reality (continued in p02).
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p02 (2)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the Lord's nature?
- premises: He alone is own-nature of all beings, supreme Lord; host of things is His power, Lordship-made.
- inference: All things = His power; He = own-nature.
- conclusion: The Lord is own-nature; things are His power.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p03 (3)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How do power and power-possessor relate?
- premises: Power does not desire difference from power-possessor; identity eternal, like fire and burning.
- inference: Power and power-possessor are eternally identical (like fire and its burning).
- conclusion: No difference between śakti and śaktiman.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p04 (4)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is Bhairava?
- premises: He alone is Bhairava-god, world-bearing-characterized; in own-self-vision whole reflected by power.
- inference: Bhairava = world-bearer, reflected by His power.
- conclusion: Bhairava is the world-bearer.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p05 (5)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the supreme goddess?
- premises: Of Him alone is supreme goddess, own-nature-grasping-eager; fullness in all beings, neither little nor excess.
- inference: Supreme goddess = own-nature-grasping-eager, fullness in all beings.
- conclusion: The supreme goddess is fullness in all beings.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p06 (6)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What does the Lord do?
- premises: This god by this goddess always play-joy-eager; varied creation-dissolution does simultaneously, all-pervading.
- inference: Lord plays (creates/dissolves) simultaneously, all-pervading.
- conclusion: The Lord's play = simultaneous creation-dissolution.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p07 (7)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is autonomy?
- premises: Hard-to-accomplish of it is unsurpassed indeed; that itself is autonomy, lordship, awareness-form.
- inference: Autonomy = lordship = awareness-form; unsurpassed.
- conclusion: Autonomy = awareness-form.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p08 (8)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is inertness?
- premises: Limited-light-ness of inert indeed is mark; from inert different is awareness since not measured.
- inference: Inert = limited light; awareness = unmeasured, different from inert.
- conclusion: Inertness vs. awareness distinguished.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p09 (9)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How do creation and dissolution stand?
- premises: Thus of it autonomous by own-power-distinction — own-self creation-dissolution as nature stand.
- inference: Creation/dissolution are the autonomous self's nature.
- conclusion: Creation and dissolution = self's nature.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p10 (10)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the variety in the world?
- premises: In them variety extreme up-down across what; worlds, their parts, pleasure-pain-mind what.
- inference: Extreme variety in worlds, parts, and minds.
- conclusion: Variety is extreme (continued in p11).
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p11 (11)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is non-knowledge?
- premises: What of it non-knowledge — that its autonomy thus stated; it indeed saṃsāra of fools who is terrifying.
- inference: Non-knowledge = saṃsāra (of fools); autonomy is what non-knowledge obscures.
- conclusion: Non-knowledge = saṃsāra.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p12 (12)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How does one attain the reality?
- premises: From that grace-sap or from teacher-scripture or from scripture of supreme Lord from which from which approached.
- inference: Attainment via grace-sap, teacher-scripture, or Lord's scripture.
- conclusion: Three paths to the reality (continued in p13).
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p13 (13)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is liberation?
- premises: What of reality full-knowledge — that is liberation from supreme Lord. That fullness of awakened, living-liberation that is remembered.
- inference: Full knowledge of reality = liberation; fullness = living liberation.
- conclusion: Full knowledge = liberation = living liberation.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p14 (14)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: Are bondage and liberation different?
- premises: These two bondage and liberation from supreme-Lord-nature. Not they differ; no difference indeed in truth in supreme Lord.
- inference: Bondage and liberation are not ultimately different (in the Lord).
- conclusion: No ultimate difference between bondage and liberation.
- unresolved: none.

## bod-p15 (15)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice (close).
- question: What is the Lord's nature?
- premises: Thus will-power art knowledge power trident lotus resorted — Bhairava of all things own-nature is examined.
- inference: The Lord (Bhairava) is the own-nature of all things, resorted to by will/art/knowledge/power.
- conclusion: Bhairava = own-nature of all things (close).
- unresolved: none.
