# L200 — Paramārthasāra (104 kārikās, 12 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Paramārthasāra (104 kārikās) is Abhinavagupta's summary of Kashmir Śaivism (Śiva-śāsana view). It opens with resort to Śambhu as supreme. It establishes the supreme tattva (pure, thought-free, 36 tattvas like mirror), senses/elements/tanmātras, bondage (delusion, I-am-not-self), saṃkoca (Paramaśiva as if impure by Māyā, śaktipāta required), five knower categories (Śiva/Mantramaheśvara/Mantreśvara/Mantrī/vijñānakala/pralayākala/sakala), liberation (Śivahood, three upāyas: anupāya/śāmbhava/śākta/āṇava), world (Śiva's own-nature, threefold), body (Śiva's garment, winds/channels), mind (fourfold inner instrument), soul (threefold: jīva/ātman/paramātman). It closes with all-is-Śiva.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Supreme = Śambhu (Śiva); universe = fourfold (śakti/māyā/prakṛti/pṛthvī) manifested by Lord.
- C2: Supreme tattva = pure, peaceful, thought-free; 36 tattvas shine like mirror-image; Śiva-Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-Vidyā state.
- C3: Senses (5 cognitive + 5 conative) → tanmātras (5) → elements (5); creation from prakṛti covers consciousness like husk covers grain.
- C4: Bondage = delusion causing I-am-not-self in non-self things; self wrapped in net of body/breath/reflection.
- C5: Saṃkoca = Paramaśiva shines as if impure by Māyā; without śaktipāta, no liberation; Guru's grace liberates.
- C6: Five knowers: Śiva (pure), Mantramaheśvara/Mantreśvara/Mantrī (contracted by Māyā/vidyā/kalā), vijñānakala (pure after Māyā-mala), pralayākala (pure after kalā-mala), sakala (both malas).
- C7: Liberation = Śivahood attainment; three upāyas: anupāya (śaktipāta alone), śāmbhava (will), śākta (knowledge), āṇava (action).
- C8: World = Śiva's own-nature; threefold (gross/subtle/supreme).
- C9: Body = Śiva's garment; five winds, three channels.
