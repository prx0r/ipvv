# C1 — Paramārthasāra

## param-p01–p03 — supreme, tattva, senses
**SUMMARY.** Paramārthasāra opens with resort to Śambhu as supreme, abiding in depths, beginningless, one, lodged in many caves, abode of all. Student, revolving in wheel of suffering from womb to death, asked the Lord the supreme truth. Guru spoke the essence in support-kārikās; Abhinava speaks by Śiva-śāsana view. By own-power-glory-weight, fourfold universe in division: power, Māyā, prakṛti, earth, manifested by Lord; therein, this world, varied body-instrument-world-continuum; and there, experiencer, embodied one; Śiva indeed. Supreme tattva = free from all thought, pure, peaceful, free from dissolution/rise; in which shines the 36-fold world. As in mirror-image, city/village undivided, shines in division, and mutually, from mirror too; from purest supreme Bhairava-knowledge, thus though divisionless, mutually and from that, divided, this world shines: Śiva-Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-Vidyā tattva-state; five Śaktis' divided nature. Senses: ear/skin/eye/tongue/nose = cognitive; speech/hand/foot/anus/genitals = conative. Their object, subtle, free from division = five tanmātras: sound, touch, form, taste, smell. By combination, gross object = five elements: space, air, fire, water, earth. As husk covers grain, creation from prakṛti; up to earth, this consciousness.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śambhu as supreme, the supreme tattva (pure/thought-free, 36 tattvas like mirror), and senses/elements/tanmātras.

**KEY TERMS.** śambhu (Śambhu), paratattva (supreme principle), ṣaṭtriṃśat (36), darpaṇa (mirror), buddhīndriya (cognitive sense), karmendriya (conative sense), tanmātra (subtle element), bhūta (gross element), prakṛti (prakṛti).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First three passages; sets the metaphysical frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Supreme: I resort to Śambhu; student asks about supreme truth; Guru speaks essence; Abhinava by Śiva-śāsana view; fourfold universe (śakti/māyā/prakṛti/pṛthvī) manifested by Lord; world-continuum; experiencer embodied; Śiva. (2) Supreme tattva: free from thought, pure, peaceful; 36-fold world shines; mirror-image analogy; Śiva-Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-Vidyā state; five Śaktis. (3) Senses/elements: 5 cognitive + 5 conative senses; 5 tanmātras; 5 elements; creation from prakṛti covers consciousness like husk covers grain.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.
