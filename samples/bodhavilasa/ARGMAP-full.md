# Bodhavilāsa — ARGMAP

## bodh-p01 (2–10)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja, summarizing Pratyabhijñā.
- question: What is citi (consciousness)?
- premises: Citiḥ svatantrā viśvasya hetur (consciousness is free, cause of world). Svātantryarūpā jñātā ca siddhīr vā saṃprayacchate (free-natured, knower, or bestows attainments). Sā svabhittau svecchayaiva viśvasyonmīlanāya ca (on own screen, by own will, for world's unfolding). Nānānurūpavaicitryād grāhyagrāhakabhedataḥ (by varied fitting, object/subject division). Viśvasya tasya vaicitryaṃ grāhyabhedena saṃsthitam (world's variety stands by object-division).
- inference: Citi = free consciousness, cause of world; unfolds world by own will on own screen; world varies by object/subject division.
- conclusion: Citi doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bodh-p02 (11–20)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What is the contracted state?
- premises: Pramātṛsaptakatvena ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasaṅkhyayā (by seven knowers, 36 tattvas). Bhāvitaḥ sa maheśāno mokṣalakṣmīḥ pravartate (that Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates). Tadbhūmikā darśanādyās sarvā eva (those-grounded, all views). Bhūmikāḥ kṛtrimā yadvan naṭo gṛhṇāti svecchayā (stages artificial, like actor assumes by own will). Cidānandasamullāsollasitātmā virājate (consciousness-bliss-delight-shining self shines). Cidvatsvaśaktisaṅkocāt... (like consciousness, by own-power-contraction...).
- inference: Contracted state = seven knowers, 36 tattvas; Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates; stages artificial like actor; consciousness-bliss self shines.
- conclusion: Saṃkoca doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bodh-p03 (21–29)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What is liberation?
- premises: Sadvimarśabalenaivaṃ nityaṃ yaḥ pariśīlate (by true-reflection-strength, one who constantly practices). Sa muktaḥ sarvabandhebhyo nityānandamayo bhavet (he is liberated from all bonds, becomes bliss-filled). Cidvahnir grasate so 'yaṃ suprabuddho 'sty asau yadā (consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha). Tadāsau vimalo jñeyaś cidvahniḥ sarvabhakṣakaḥ (then stainless, knowable, consciousness-fire, all-devouring). Etadbalena saṃyukto yogī nirvāṇalakṣaṇaḥ (united with this strength, yogī has nirvāṇa-character). Padaṃ prāpno... (gains state...).
- inference: Liberation = by true-reflection-strength, constant practice → liberated from all bonds, bliss-filled; consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha, stainless, all-devouring; yogī has nirvāṇa-character.
- conclusion: Liberation doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bodh-p04 (30–38)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What is the upāya (means)?
- premises: Nirmalā suprabhā śuddhā nityānandamayī parā (stainless, radiant, pure, bliss-filled, supreme). Nirdehacchedajālāpi jñānadṛṣṭir vijṛmbhate (even body-cutting-net, knowledge-vision unfolds). Madhyadhāmavikāse tu upāyo balavatsthitiḥ (in middle-abode-expansion, means = strong-state). Vikalpakṣayasaṅkocavikāsādyā anekaśaḥ (thought-destruction, contraction, expansion, etc., many). Akiñciccintanād eva vikalpānāṃ kṣayo bhavet (from thinking nothing, thought-destruction occurs). Cidbodhād vilasacchaktī raktāsau... (from consciousness-knowledge, shining-power, attached...).
- inference: Upāya = in middle-abode-expansion, strong-state; thought-destruction from thinking nothing; consciousness-knowledge → shining-power.
- conclusion: Upāya doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bodh-p05 (39–42)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Tatrāsau vilasan yogī mantreśvarapade sthitaḥ (there, shining yogī, abiding in Mantreśvara-state). Tatra śakto vibhur yadvat krīḍate śaktibṛṃhitaḥ (there, powerful, lord, plays, power-augmented). Evaṃ svatantratā tasya vyāpakasya jagadvibhoḥ (thus autonomy of that, all-pervading, world-lord). Nānābhedajuṣaś citrapadasthitasyākhaṇḍitā (of one enjoying varied differences, abiding in wonder-state, undivided). Etad advayam ity uktaṃ jñānāmṛtam anuttamam (this non-dual, said, knowledge-nectar, unsurpassed). Yaḥ pibec chāmyate... (who drinks, is pacified...).
- inference: Close = shining yogī in Mantreśvara-state; plays power-augmented; autonomy of all-pervading world-lord; undivided in wonder-state; non-dual knowledge-nectar unsurpassed; who drinks is pacified.
- conclusion: The text closes with non-dual knowledge-nectar.
- unresolved: none.
