# L200 — Bodhavilāsa (40 verses, 5 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Bodhavilāsa (40 verses) is Kṣemarāja's (11th c.) work on consciousness and liberation. It establishes: citi = free consciousness, cause of world; unfolds world by own will on own screen; world varies by object/subject division. Contracted state = seven knowers, 36 tattvas; Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates; stages artificial like actor; consciousness-bliss self shines. Liberation = by true-reflection-strength, constant practice → liberated, bliss-filled; consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha, stainless, all-devouring; yogī has nirvāṇa-character. Upāya = in middle-abode-expansion, strong-state; thought-destruction from thinking nothing. Close = shining yogī in Mantreśvara-state; plays power-augmented; autonomy of all-pervading world-lord; undivided in wonder-state; non-dual knowledge-nectar unsurpassed; who drinks is pacified.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Citi = free consciousness, cause of world; unfolds by own will; world varies by object/subject.
- C2: Contracted state = seven knowers, 36 tattvas; Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates; stages artificial like actor.
- C3: Liberation = by true-reflection-strength, constant practice → liberated, bliss-filled.
- C4: Consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha, stainless, all-devouring; yogī has nirvāṇa-character.
- C5: Upāya = in middle-abode-expansion, strong-state; thought-destruction from thinking nothing.
- C6: Close = shining yogī in Mantreśvara-state; non-dual knowledge-nectar unsurpassed.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Citi as non-free.
