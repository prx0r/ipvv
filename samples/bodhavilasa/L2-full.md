# Bodhavilāsa — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## bodh-p01–p02 — citi and saṃkoca
**SUMMARY.** Citiḥ svatantrā viśvasya hetur — consciousness is free, cause of world. Svātantryarūpā jñātā ca siddhīr vā saṃprayacchate — free-natured, knower, or bestows attainments. Sā svabhittau svecchayaiva viśvasyonmīlanāya ca — on own screen, by own will, for world's unfolding. Nānānurūpavaicitryād grāhyagrāhakabhedataḥ — by varied fitting, object/subject division. Viśvasya tasya vaicitryaṃ grāhyabhedena saṃsthitam — world's variety stands by object-division. Pramātṛsaptakatvena ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasaṅkhyayā — by seven knowers, 36 tattvas. Bhāvitaḥ sa maheśāno mokṣalakṣmīḥ pravartate — that Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates. Tadbhūmikā darśanādyās sarvā eva — those-grounded, all views. Bhūmikāḥ kṛtrimā yadvan naṭo gṛhṇāti svecchayā — stages artificial, like actor assumes by own will. Cidānandasamullāsollasitātmā virājate — consciousness-bliss-delight-shining self shines.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes citi (free consciousness, cause of world, unfolds by own will) and saṃkoca (seven knowers, 36 tattvas, artificial stages like actor).

**KEY TERMS.** citi (consciousness), svatantra (free), viśva (world), unmīlana (unfolding), pramātṛ (knower), tattva (principle), mokṣa (liberation), lakṣmī (Lakṣmī), naṭa (actor).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Citi: consciousness is free, cause of world; free-natured, knower, or bestows attainments; on own screen, by own will, for world's unfolding; by varied fitting, object/subject division; world's variety stands by object-division. (2) Saṃkoca: by seven knowers, 36 tattvas; that Maheśāna, mokṣa-Lakṣmī operates; those-grounded, all views; stages artificial, like actor assumes by own will; consciousness-bliss-delight-shining self shines.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.20.

## bodh-p03–p04 — liberation and upāya
**SUMMARY.** Sadvimarśabalenaivaṃ nityaṃ yaḥ pariśīlate — by true-reflection-strength, one who constantly practices. Sa muktaḥ sarvabandhebhyo nityānandamayo bhavet — he is liberated from all bonds, becomes bliss-filled. Cidvahnir grasate so 'yaṃ suprabuddho 'sty asau yadā — consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha. Tadāsau vimalo jñeyaś cidvahniḥ sarvabhakṣakaḥ — then stainless, knowable, consciousness-fire, all-devouring. Etadbalena saṃyukto yogī nirvāṇalakṣaṇaḥ — united with this strength, yogī has nirvāṇa-character. Nirmalā suprabhā śuddhā nityānandamayī parā — stainless, radiant, pure, bliss-filled, supreme. Nirdehacchedajālāpi jñānadṛṣṭir vijṛmbhate — even body-cutting-net, knowledge-vision unfolds. Madhyadhāmavikāse tu upāyo balavatsthitiḥ — in middle-abode-expansion, means = strong-state. Vikalpakṣayasaṅkocavikāsādyā anekaśaḥ — thought-destruction, contraction, expansion, etc., many. Akiñciccintanād eva vikalpānāṃ kṣayo bhavet — from thinking nothing, thought-destruction occurs.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes liberation (by true-reflection-strength, constant practice → liberated, bliss-filled; consciousness-fire swallows; suprabuddha → stainless, all-devouring; yogī has nirvāṇa-character) and upāya (in middle-abode-expansion, strong-state; thought-destruction from thinking nothing).

**KEY TERMS.** mokṣa (liberation), nityānanda (eternal bliss), cidvahni (consciousness-fire), suprabuddha (suprabuddha), nirvāṇa (nirvāṇa), upāya (means), madhyadhāma (middle-abode), vikalpa (thought).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (citi/saṃkoca); p03–p04 establish liberation/upāya.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Liberation: by true-reflection-strength, constant practice → liberated from all bonds, bliss-filled; consciousness-fire swallows; when suprabuddha, stainless, knowable, all-devouring; united with this strength, yogī has nirvāṇa-character. (2) Upāya: stainless, radiant, pure, bliss-filled, supreme; even body-cutting-net, knowledge-vision unfolds; in middle-abode-expansion, means = strong-state; thought-destruction, contraction, expansion, etc., many; from thinking nothing, thought-destruction occurs.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.21–1.38.

## bodh-p05 — close
**SUMMARY.** Tatrāsau vilasan yogī mantreśvarapade sthitaḥ — there, shining yogī, abiding in Mantreśvara-state. Tatra śakto vibhur yadvat krīḍate śaktibṛṃhitaḥ — there, powerful, lord, plays, power-augmented. Evaṃ svatantratā tasya vyāpakasya jagadvibhoḥ — thus autonomy of that, all-pervading, world-lord. Nānābhedajuṣaś citrapadasthitasyākhaṇḍitā — of one enjoying varied differences, abiding in wonder-state, undivided. Etad advayam ity uktaṃ jñānāmṛtam anuttamam — this non-dual, said, knowledge-nectar, unsurpassed. Yaḥ pibec chāmyate — who drinks, is pacified.

**FUNCTION.** Closes the text with non-dual knowledge-nectar.

**KEY TERMS.** mantreśvara (Mantreśvara), svatantra (autonomy), citrapada (wonder-state), advaya (non-dual), jñānāmṛta (knowledge-nectar).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p03–p04 (liberation/upāya).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in one step. Close: shining yogī in Mantreśvara-state; plays power-augmented; autonomy of all-pervading world-lord; undivided in wonder-state; non-dual knowledge-nectar unsurpassed; who drinks is pacified.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.39–1.42.
