# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 19

## tal-a19-p01–p06 — sadyaḥsamutkrānti (instant liberation) and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 19: thus instant-liberation-giving initiation is said. At-that-moment or by-enjoyment or at-death, Śiva goes. Thus-said, in-Mālinī-śāstra, indicated, by-Maheśin. At-death, near, by-śaktipāta-clearness. Having-attained, Śāṃkarī, initiation, therefore initiation-moment, supreme. Śiva-goes, iti, artha, here, by-pūrva-apara-vivecana.
