# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 26 (76 verses, 8 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 26 (76 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on śeṣavṛtti (remaining practice). It establishes: thus śeṣavṛtti, for-living, useful. Initiation, many-kinds, this, śrāddha-ending, that, said. That, saṃskriyā, for-liberation, for-bhoga, or, for-both. There, saṃskāra-siddhi, which, initiation, directly, not, liberating. By-anusaṃdhi, which, directly-liberating, sabījikā. Those, both, initiated, their, ājīva-vartanam. To-be-said, putraka-etc, for-tanmayatva-prasiddhi.
