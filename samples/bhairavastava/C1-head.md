# C1 — Bhairavastava

## bh-p01 — veneration
**SUMMARY.** Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate. The stotra opens with the devotee's stance: total surrender of mind to Bhairava, who is the refuge of those without refuge. Veneration is not ritual but mental absorption — the mind becoming His.

**FUNCTION.** Opening verse: establishes the devotee's stance (mind-become-Yours) and the object of veneration (Bhairava as refuge).

**KEY TERMS.** bhairavanātha (Bhairava-Lord), anāthaśaraṇya (refuge of the helpless), tvanmayacitta (mind-become-Yours), vande (I venerate).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First verse of the stotra; sets the tone for all ten.

**EXPLANATION.** The verse has two parts. First, the object: Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham), who is the refuge of the helpless (anāthaśaraṇyaṃ) — the one who shelters those with no shelter. Second, the act: by mind-become-Yours (tvanmayacittatayā), in heart (hṛdi), I venerate (vande). Veneration here is not ritual offering but mental absorption — the devotee's mind becoming His. This is the stotra's opening gesture: total surrender of the mind to Bhairava.
