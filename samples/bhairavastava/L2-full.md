# Bhairavastava — L2 READ

## bh-p01 — veneration
Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate. The stotra opens with the devotee's stance: total surrender of mind to Bhairava, who is the refuge of those without refuge.

## bh-p02 — identity
You, Maheśa, are always my self; by You all is mine. The Lord is not other than the self; through Him, everything becomes mine.

## bh-p03 — suffering acknowledged
Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, and terror-causing karmas are present — the devotee does not flinch. The reality of suffering is acknowledged, not denied.

## bh-p04 — power-filled
By Śaṅkara-service, firm in contemplation, I am filled with terrifying Bhairava-power. Service and firm contemplation produce the power-filled state.

## bh-p05 — fearlessness
Death, Yama, the ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never do I fear. Salutation to the Lord removes all fear of death and danger.

## bh-p06 — bliss
In being-full in You — I, the self, enter bliss. Bliss is entering the fullness of the Lord.

## bh-p07 — nondifference and hymn-rain
Lord, then alone is my nondifference with You; the supreme-nectar-rain of the hymn arises. Nondifference manifests when the hymn-rain arises.

## bh-p08 — scripture-contemplation
Your scripture's supreme-nectar-contemplation in the mind — the bliss-stream flows. Contemplating scripture produces the bliss-stream in mind.

## bh-p09 — obtaining the Lord
You, dear, having been obtained — beautiful vision alone, hard to get by others, samaya-knower. Obtaining the Lord is the rare beautiful vision of the samaya-knower.

## bh-p10 — the compassionate Lord
By whom the Lord becomes, the world's great heat is pacified instantly — compassionate. The Lord, becoming, pacifies the world's suffering instantly, compassionately.
