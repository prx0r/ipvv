# Bhairavastava — L1 controlled

## bh-p01 (1)
Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless, by mind-become-Yours, in heart I venerate. // abhts_1

## bh-p02 (2)
You, Maheśa, always my self; by You all is mine. // abhts_2

## bh-p03 (3)
Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts. // abhts_3

## bh-p04 (4)
By Śaṅkara-service, contemplation-firm, I am terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled. // abhts_4

## bh-p05 (5)
Death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never I fear. // abhts_5

## bh-p06 (6)
In being-full in You, I, self, bliss I enter. // abhts_6

## bh-p07 (7)
Lord, then alone my with-You-nondifference; hymn-supreme-nectar-rain arises. // abhts_7

## bh-p08 (8)
Your-scripture supreme-nectar-contemplation in mind, bliss-stream flows. // abhts_8

## bh-p09 (9)
You, dear, having-obtained, beautiful-vision alone, hard-to-get-by-others, samaya-knower. // abhts_9

## bh-p10 (10)
By whom the Lord becomes, world's great-heat pacifies instantly, compassionate. // abhts_10
