# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 27

## tal-a27-p01–p06 — liṅgapūjā and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 27: thus liṅgapūjā said, by Mālinī-śāstra. These, ūrdhva-śāstra-said-mantras, not established. Externalize, therefore, these, by-secret, siddhi-giving. By-own-vīrya-ānanda-māhātmya-praveśa-vaśa-śālinīm. Who siddhi give, their externalness, form-vicyuti. Kiṃ ca, said, samāveśa-pūrṇa, bhoktrātmaka, śivaḥ. Bhoga-lāmpaṭya-bhāg, bhoga-vicchede, nigraha-ātmakaḥ. Śānta-tva, anya-kriya-udbhūta, jighatsā-vṛṃhitaṃ, vapuḥ. Svayaṃ, pratiṣṭ... Guru, ca, atra, nirodhākhye, kāla, itthaṃ, vibhau, vadet. Jīvati, asmin, phalāntaṃ, tvaṃ, tiṣṭhet, jīvāvadhī, iti, vā. Liṅgaṃ, ca, bāṇaliṅgaṃ, vā, ratnajaṃ, vā, atha, mauktikam. Paṅkti-cakra-kaśūla-abja-vidhinā, tūram, āśrayet. Tallakṣaṇaṃ, bruve, śrīmat, picuśāstre, nirūpitam. Tūre, yogaḥ, sadā, śastaḥ, siddhido, doṣavarjite. Jālakair, jarjarai... Akṣasūtraṃ, kramotkṛṣṭaṃ, raudrākṣaṃ, vā, viśeṣataḥ. Śataṃ, tithyuttaraṃ, yadvā, sāṣṭaṃ, yadvā, tadardhakam. Tadardhaṃ, vātha, pañcāśadyuktaṃ, tatparikalpayet. Tasyāpyeṣa, vidhiḥ, sarvaḥ, pratiṣṭhādau, prakīrtitaḥ. Tanniṣkamparasaiḥ, pūrṇaṃ, kṛtvā, asmin, pūjayet, kramam. Adhomukhaṃ, sadā, sthāpyaṃ, pūjitaṃ, pūjane, punaḥ. Ta... Ata, eva, yadā, bhūridinaṃ, maṇḍalakalpanam. Tadā, dine, dine, kuryād, vibhavaistarpaṇaṃ, bahu. Pratiṣṭhāyāṃ, ca, sarvatra, guruḥ, pūrvoditaṃ, param. Satattvamanusa... (close).
