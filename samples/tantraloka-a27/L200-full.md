# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 27 (59 verses, 6 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 27 (59 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on liṅgapūjā (liṅga worship). It establishes: thus liṅgapūjā said, by Mālinī-śāstra; these ūrdhva-śāstra-said-mantras not established; externalize, therefore, these, by-secret, siddhi-giving; by-own-vīrya-ānanda-māhātmya-praveśa-vaśa-śālinīm; who siddhi give, their externalness, form-vicyuti; kiṃ ca, said, samāveśa-pūrṇa, bhoktrātmaka, śivaḥ; bhoga-lāmpaṭya-bhāg, bhoga-vicchede, nigraha-ātmakaḥ; śānta-tva, anya-kriya-udbhūta, jighatsā-vṛṃhitaṃ, vapuḥ; svayaṃ, pratiṣṭ... Guru, ca, atra, nirodhākhye, kāla, itthaṃ, vibhau, vadet; jīvati, asmin, phalāntaṃ, tvaṃ, tiṣṭhet, jīvāvadhī, iti, vā; liṅgaṃ, ca, bāṇaliṅgaṃ, vā, ratnajaṃ, vā, atha, mauktikam. Paṅkti-cakra-kaśūla-abja-vidhinā, tūram, āśrayet; tallakṣaṇaṃ, bruve, śrīmat, picuśāstre, nirūpitam; tūre, yogaḥ, sadā, śastaḥ, siddhido, doṣavarjite; jālakair, jarjarai... Tasyāpyeṣa, vidhiḥ, sarvaḥ, pratiṣṭhādau, prakīrtitaḥ; tanniṣkamparasaiḥ, pūrṇaṃ, kṛtvā, asmin, pūjayet, kramam; adhomukhaṃ, sadā, sthāpyaṃ, pūjitaṃ, pūjane, punaḥ; ta...
