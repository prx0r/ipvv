# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 24

## tal-a24-p01–p02 — antyeṣṭi (funeral rite) and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 24: thus Śāmbhava-śāsana-said, secret, listen, antyeṣṭi-saṃskāra. Of all lower-station, up to guru, clearly. By śaktipāta, before-said, do, antyeṣṭi-dīkṣaṇa. Of ūrdhva-śāsana-goers, samaya-damaged-self. Antyeṣṭi-dīkṣā, to-be-done, by guru, tattva-knower. Samayācāra-faults, by-negligence, fallen. Antyeṣṭi-dīkṣā, to-be-done, Śrī-dīkṣottara-śāsana. Whatever said before, in mṛtoddhāra-vidhi. In icon, that very here, all, in corpse-body, do. Śrī-Siddha-tantra-said, vidhi, this, determined. Final which becomes first, doing that, final, first. Enclosing one by one, iṣṭi, that antyeṣṭi, dvitayī, held. Pūjā-dhyāna-japa-pluṣṭa-samaye, not, for sādhaka. Piṇḍapāta-etc, freed, khecaro or, becomes, dear. Ācārya, tattva-endowed, where, there, died, sati. Antyeṣṭi not, vidyeta, pure-consciousness-head. Mantra-yoga-etc by, killed, in hell, those. To-be-done, their, here, antyeṣṭi, by-guru, very-compassionate. Not maṇḍala-etc, here, would-be, in-śamāśānika-vidhi. Some, that also, to-be-done, said, in-pretasadman. Having-worshipped, vibhu, all, nyāsa, as-before, do. By-saṃhāra-krama-yoga, from-feet, to-head-backward. Thus, awaken, him, by-action-knowledge-samādhi. By-bindu, restrain, tattva, by-śakti-bīja, pierce. By-ghaṭṭana, nāda-place, tu, by-triśūla, strike. By-suṣumnā-internal, by-visarga, again, again. Strike, kalās, all, trembles, he, then, paśu. Raise, left-hand, or, then, him, unite, supreme. By-pratyaya, without, liberation, indeed, incredible, to-deluded. For-that, this, declared, not, liberation-means, this. Thus, said, Parameśa, Śrī-Kula-gahvara-śāsana. Sādhya, measurable, mokṣa-etc, by-pratyaya, beyond-senses. After-initiation, and, puryaṣṭa-offering, here, declared. That-rule, in-śruti-patra, in-lotus, middle, deva, Sadāśiva. Īśa-Rudra-Hari-Brahma, four, from-east, etc. Having-worshipped, śruti-sparśa, rasa, gandha, vapu-dvaya. Dhyā, ahaṃkṛti, manaś, ceti, to-Brahma-etc, offer, in-order. Of-these, tarpaṇa, having-done, by-hundred-homa, daiśika. This, sāṃnyāsikī, initiation, puryaṣṭaka-purifying. Puryaṣṭaka, absence, and, no, svarga-naraka, etc. Thus, having-done, not, to-be-done, laukika, anything, also. Said, Śrī-Mādhava-kula, śāsana-stha, in-dead, also. Piṇḍapāta-udakādi, laukika, avoid. Śiva, having-worshipped, cakra-arcā, as-able, do. By-gradual, three-ten, thirty, thirty, year, day. Thus, said, antyeṣṭi-yāga, this, by-Parameśvara, spoken.
