# L200 — Bhairavānukaraṇastotra (47 verses, 4 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Bhairavānukaraṇastotra (47 verses) is Kṣemarāja's (11th c.) devotional stotra. It establishes: cidbhairava = supreme, nectar-form, one, radiant; Śiva swallows all by radiant instrument-wheel; praise/praiser/praised not different; all becomes consciousness-form. Pāśas = bonds vicious, excluded always; Māyā-cloth/passion thrown in supreme sky; Lord reveals internal-power-knife cutting saṃsāra. Śaktis = supreme-first-triad, beautiful, great; Lord imitates gods outside by trident-bearing pretense; by own will-śakti, three lower realms revealed; carries own supreme diamond-made six-spoked śakti; entire world subdued by śakti. Close = nakedness = uncovered self-form; paramabrahma-sun not covered by Māyā; destroyer of all suns; stainless autumn-sky-radiance body; paramabrahma when resorting to Bhairava.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
