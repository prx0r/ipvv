## bhair-p01 (2-13)
[and]-cidbhairavam eva paraṃ paramāmṛtarūpam ekam atidīptam | ullasitakaraṇacakragrastasamastaṃ śivaṃ vande || stutyaḥ stotā stutir iti yad api vibhinnaṃ na kiṃcid astīha | mṛṣati yathā yad rūpaṃ cidrūpatayā tathā bhavaty etat || vigalitasarvavibhedaṃ sarvavibhedātma cidghanānandam | yat tava tattvaṃ bhagavaṃs tasyānukṛtiṃ tvadākṛtiṃ vande || rekhāpuruṣaḥ puruṣaṃ varṇalipir varṇasaṃcayaṃ yadvat | tadvat vicitrarūpaṃ tvām ākāro vyanakti paramātman || tatrāvikalpam eva tvāṃ cidrūpaṃ samāviśya | vimṛśāmaḥ kim api manāṅ na hi tattvaṃ kalpanāviṣayaḥ || svātmaprabhāṃ vidhāya vāgdevīṃ viṣayakusumakḷptārcaḥ | prathamonmeṣaṃ gurum atha vanditvā hṛdayam āviśya || staumi vimṛśan maheśvaram ātmānaṃ svaṃ cidekaghanam | srāvitapāśakadambakam etasmin dehadevagṛhe || cityānandecchāvitkaraṇākhyāḥ śaktayo jayanti vibho | sūkṣmasthūlabhidāptā vaktratvaṃ brahmamuṇḍabhidhā || sarvajñatādiviṣayaṃ hṛdayādikam aṅgaṣaṭkaṃ te | sakalaṃ sadāśivāntaṃ pretātmatayā tavāsanaṃ svāmin || paramaprakāśavapuṣo vimarśaśaktiḥ prabho parā devī | asyā eva prasaraḥ sarvā brāhmyādikā devyaḥ || dahanajalāmṛtalekhāviṣādi bibhrat tvam ādiśasy etat | viśvaṃ viruddham api saccidrūpe mayi virudhyate naitat || saṃvitsūtranilīno niḥsāro mātṛnivaho 'yam | karaṇamuṇḍamālabhārin darśayasītīva naḥ svāmin ||

## bhair-p02 (14-25)
[and]-paśyata paśyata pāśā viṣamās ta ime bahiṣkṛtāḥ śaśvat | ity antradhāraṇamiṣat kathayaṃs tvaṃ naḥ samābhāsi || māyāpaṭaḥ sarāgo vyomni pare 'smin sa eṣa nikṣiptaḥ | iti gajacarma saraktaṃ mūrdhni vahan kathayasīśa || antaḥśaktikṛpāṇīm vyanakṣi saṃsṛtivibhedinīm asinā | nijaśaktimahimasvīkṛtasamastaviśvā hi vīravarāḥ || bhavabhayahantā so 'ham sānāthye 'vasthito 'smi mā bhaiṣṭa | ity āsphoṭitakheṭakadarśanato diśasi naḥ svāmin || nijaśaktipāśavalitaś cidvapuṣā kalyate mayā kālaḥ | iti pāśadhāraṇavaśāt prathayasi naḥ kālakālas tvam || bhedamayam akhilam etan nijaśaktyaivākṣipāmi saṃhartum | ity aṅkuśadhāraṇataḥ sphuṭayati parabhairavo 'smākam || kodaṇḍārūdhaśarapradarśanād brahmaviṣṇurudreśān | sasadākhyaśivāñ chaktyā kāraṇahariṇaṃ bhinatsi mṛgavat tvam || varadena pāṇinā tvaṃ viśvavibhūtipradatvam abhinayasi | na khalu paratattvaniṣṭho vibhavamalaiḥ spṛśyate jātu || abhayena ca bhavabhayāni unmūlayatā prakāśyate satatam | viśvānugrahakāraṇasvabhāvatā tava karatalena || akhyātirūpam asamaṃ māyāyāḥ śakalitaṃ śiro 'traitat | parabodhamayena mayā muṇḍaṃ bibhrat prakāśayasy evam || niḥśeṣahṛtasārā mayy eva jagatsthitiś cidekaghane | iti khaṭvāṅgakaraṅkodvahanacchalato dadāsy ājñām || vīṇāghaṇtāḍamarūn uḍḍāmara vādayann idaṃ diśasi | vṛttitribhedabhinnaṃ nādaṃ śṛṇvan sthito 'smīti ||

## bhair-p03 (26-37)
[and]-kiṃ ca parādikaśaktitritayārāsundarām mahāśaktim | devānukaroṣi bahis triśūladhāraṇamiṣeṇa nityaṃ tvam || icchādikanijaśaktiprakāśitādhaḥsthagocaratritayām | svām eva parāṃ śaktiṃ vajramayīṃ vahasi ṣaḍarāṃ tvam || jagad akhilaṃ macchaktyā damitaṃ sarvavyāvasthitīr dhatte | iti daṇḍadhāraṇavaśād vyanakṣi cidbhairavāsmākam || mudgaraparaśū bibhrad baindavanādānukārarūpau tvam | bhedavibhedanaśakalanaparatvam īśāna nirdiśasi || darśanabhedāt svāminn anyānyām āhṛtiṃ dadhānas tvam | bhairavaparabodhamayaṃ prathayasi nijadhāma sarvatra || śāktāṇḍakhaṇḍamadhye viśvarasāḥ sarvam ahaṃ pibāmi sadā | vyañjayasi narakapālagarudhiramiṣād etad iva me 'ntaḥ || abhimānahṛdayabandhānutkhātau tāv imau saśikhau | mūrtāv iva muṇdābjau prathayan dhatse mahākālaḥ || akhilajagadbījabhuvaṃ prathamasthām anukaroṣi bodhanidhe | karatalagabījapūrakadarśanalīlāyitād deva || bhairavarūpo 'py asi yat tatrāpi kare 'kṣamālikāṃ dhatse | tat sarvākṣavikāsād viśvaṃ parivartayasy antaḥ || asthikaramuṇḍamālābhasmābharaṇo nṛraktaruciḥ | viśvaṃ brahmamayatvāc chuddham itīśaḥ samādiśasi || svātmārāmamayānāṃ viṣamā api viṣayabudbudhāḥ kim amī | smaradahanam alikanayanam prathayan paramāgnirūpam ādiśasi || antarnibaddhalakṣyaḥ pravikāsikaraṇo 'pi yat sadā bhavasi | tat prathayasi sarvadaśaṃ gatamohakalaṅkam ātmānam ||
