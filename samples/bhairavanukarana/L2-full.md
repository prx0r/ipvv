# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## bhair-p01–p02 — cidbhairava and pāśas
**SUMMARY.** Cidbhairavam eva paraṃ paramāmṛtarūpam ekam atidīptam — consciousness-Bhairava alone, supreme, nectar-form, one, very radiant. Ullasitakaraṇacakragrastasamastaṃ śivaṃ vande — I bow to Śiva, swallowing all by the radiant instrument-wheel. Stutyaḥ stotā stutir iti yad api vibhinnaṃ na kiṃcid astīha — worthy of praise, praiser, praise — though distinguished, nothing is different here. Mṛṣati yathā yad rūpaṃ cidrūpatayā tathā bhavaty etat — as it appears, so it becomes consciousness-form. Paśyata paśyata pāśā viṣamās ta ime bahiṣkṛtāḥ śaśvat — see, see, these bonds are vicious, excluded always. Ity antradhāraṇamiṣat kathayaṃs tvaṃ naḥ samābhāsi — saying "by internal-bearing pretense," you address us. Māyāpaṭaḥ sarāgo vyomni pare 'smin sa eṣa nikṣiptaḥ — the Māyā-cloth, passion, in supreme sky, this is thrown. Iti gajacarma saraktaṃ mūrdhni vahan kathayasīśa — thus elephant-skin, bloodied, on head, carrying, you say, Lord. Antaḥśaktikṛpāṇīm vyanakṣi saṃsṛti — you reveal the internal-power-knife, saṃsāra.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes cidbhairava (supreme, nectar-form, one, radiant; Śiva swallows all; praise/praiser/praised not different) and pāśas (bonds vicious, excluded always; Māyā-cloth/passion thrown in supreme sky; internal-power-knife cuts saṃsāra).

**KEY TERMS.** cidbhairava (consciousness-Bhairava), paramāmṛta (supreme nectar), atidīpta (very radiant), pāśa (bond), māyāpaṭa (Māyā-cloth), antaḥśakti (internal power), kṛpāṇī (knife).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Cidbhairava: consciousness-Bhairava alone, supreme, nectar-form, one, very radiant; I bow to Śiva swallowing all by radiant instrument-wheel; praise/praiser/praised not different; as it appears, becomes consciousness-form. (2) Pāśas: see, see, these bonds vicious, excluded always; by internal-bearing pretense, you address us; Māyā-cloth, passion, in supreme sky, thrown; elephant-skin, bloodied, on head, carrying, you say, Lord; you reveal internal-power-knife, saṃsāra.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.25.

## bhair-p03–p04 — śaktis and close
**SUMMARY.** Kiṃ ca parādikaśaktitritayārāsundarām mahāśaktim — moreover, supreme-first-śakti-triad, beautiful, great śakti. Devānukaroṣi bahis triśūladhāraṇamiṣeṇa nityaṃ tvam — you always imitate gods outside, by trident-bearing pretense. Icchādikanijaśaktiprakāśitādhaḥsthagocaratritayām — by own śakti starting with will, three lower realms revealed. Svām eva parāṃ śaktiṃ vajramayīṃ vahasi ṣaḍarāṃ tvam — your own supreme śakti, diamond-made, six-spoked, you carry. Jagad akhilaṃ macchaktyā damitaṃ sar... Deva digambaratā te vadati nirāvaraṇam ātmano rūpam — Lord, nakedness says uncovered self-form. Paramabrahmamayārkān māyāvaraṇam kathaṃ vṛṇuyāt — paramabrahma-sun, how would Māyā-covering cover. Saṃhartā sarveṣām arkādīnām ahaṃ tamobhūmiḥ — destroyer of all suns etc., I am darkness-ground. Nirmalaśāradagaganaprabheṇa vapuṣeti darśayasi — by stainless autumn-sky-radiance body, you show. Paramabrahmamayas tvaṃ deva yadāśrayasi bhairavāk... — paramabrahma you are, Lord, when you resort to Bhairava-...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes śaktis (supreme-first-triad, beautiful, great; Lord imitates gods outside by trident-bearing pretense; by own will-śakti, three lower realms revealed; carries own supreme diamond-made six-spoked śakti) and close (nakedness = uncovered self-form; paramabrahma-sun not covered by Māyā; destroyer of all suns; stainless autumn-sky-radiance body; paramabrahma when resorting to Bhairava).

**KEY TERMS.** śakti (power), parā (supreme), icchā (will), vajra (diamond), ṣaḍara (six-spoked), digambara (naked), paramabrahma (paramabrahma), tamobhūmi (darkness-ground).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p01–p02 (cidbhairava/pāśas).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Śaktis: moreover, supreme-first-śakti-triad, beautiful, great śakti; you always imitate gods outside by trident-bearing pretense; by own śakti starting with will, three lower realms revealed; your own supreme śakti, diamond-made, six-spoked, you carry; entire world subdued by śakti. (2) Close: Lord, nakedness says uncovered self-form; paramabrahma-sun, how would Māyā-covering cover; destroyer of all suns; by stainless autumn-sky-radiance body, you show; paramabrahma you are, Lord, when you resort to Bhairava.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.26–1.48.
