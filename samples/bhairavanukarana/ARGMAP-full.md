# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra — ARGMAP

## bhair-p01 (2–13)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja, devotional voice.
- question: What is cidbhairava (consciousness-Bhairava)?
- premises: Cidbhairavam eva paraṃ paramāmṛtarūpam ekam atidīptam (consciousness-Bhairava alone, supreme, nectar-form, one, very radiant). Ullasitakaraṇacakragrastasamastaṃ śivaṃ vande (I bow to Śiva, swallowing all by the radiant instrument-wheel). Stutyaḥ stotā stutir iti yad api vibhinnaṃ na kiṃcid astīha (worthy of praise, praiser, praise — though distinguished, nothing is different here). Mṛṣati yathā yad rūpaṃ cidrūpatayā tathā bhavaty etat (as it appears, so it becomes consciousness-form). Vigalitasarvavibhedaṃ sarv... (all difference dissolved...).
- inference: Cidbhairava = supreme, nectar-form, one, radiant; Śiva swallows all by instrument-wheel; praise/praiser/praised are not different; all becomes consciousness-form.
- conclusion: Cidbhairava doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bhair-p02 (14–25)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What are the pāśas (bonds)?
- premises: Paśyata paśyata pāśā viṣamās ta ime bahiṣkṛtāḥ śaśvat (see, see, these bonds are vicious, excluded always). Ity antradhāraṇamiṣat kathayaṃs tvaṃ naḥ samābhāsi (saying "by internal-bearing pretense," you address us). Māyāpaṭaḥ sarāgo vyomni pare 'smin sa eṣa nikṣiptaḥ (the Māyā-cloth, passion, in supreme sky, this is thrown). Iti gajacarma saraktaṃ mūrdhni vahan kathayasīśa (thus elephant-skin, bloodied, on head, carrying, you say, Lord). Antaḥśaktikṛpāṇīm vyanakṣi saṃsṛti... (you reveal the internal-power-knife, saṃsāra...).
- inference: Bonds are vicious, excluded always; Māyā-cloth/passion thrown in supreme sky; Lord reveals internal-power-knife cutting saṃsāra.
- conclusion: Pāśa doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bhair-p03 (26–37)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What are the śaktis?
- premises: Kiṃ ca parādikaśaktitritayārāsundarām mahāśaktim (moreover, supreme-first-śakti-triad, beautiful, great śakti). Devānukaroṣi bahis triśūladhāraṇamiṣeṇa nityaṃ tvam (you always imitate gods outside, by trident-bearing pretense). Icchādikanijaśaktiprakāśitādhaḥsthagocaratritayām (by own śakti starting with will, three lower realms revealed). Svām eva parāṃ śaktiṃ vajramayīṃ vahasi ṣaḍarāṃ tvam (your own supreme śakti, diamond-made, six-spoked, you carry). Jagad akhilaṃ macchaktyā damitaṃ sar... (entire world, by my śakti, subdued...).
- inference: Śaktis = supreme-first-triad, beautiful, great; Lord imitates gods outside by trident-bearing pretense; by own will-śakti, three lower realms revealed; carries own supreme diamond-made six-spoked śakti; entire world subdued by śakti.
- conclusion: Śakti doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## bhair-p04 (38–48)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Deva digambaratā te vadati nirāvaraṇam ātmano rūpam (Lord, nakedness says uncovered self-form). Paramabrahmamayārkān māyāvaraṇam kathaṃ vṛṇuyāt (paramabrahma-sun, how would Māyā-covering cover). Saṃhartā sarveṣām arkādīnām ahaṃ tamobhūmiḥ (destroyer of all suns etc., I am darkness-ground). Nirmalaśāradagaganaprabheṇa vapuṣeti darśayasi (by stainless autumn-sky-radiance body, you show). Paramabrahmamayas tvaṃ deva yadāśrayasi bhairavāk... (paramabrahma you are, Lord, when you resort to Bhairava-...).
- inference: Close = nakedness = uncovered self-form; paramabrahma-sun not covered by Māyā; destroyer of all suns; stainless autumn-sky-radiance body; paramabrahma when resorting to Bhairava.
- conclusion: The text closes with paramabrahma-Bhairava identity.
- unresolved: none.
