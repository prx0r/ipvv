# C1 — Bhairavānukaraṇastotra

## bhair-p01–p02 — cidbhairava and pāśas
**SUMMARY.** Bhairavānukaraṇastotra opens: consciousness-Bhairava alone, supreme, nectar-form, one, very radiant. I bow to Śiva, swallowing all by the radiant instrument-wheel. Worthy of praise, praiser, praise — though distinguished, nothing is different here. As it appears, so it becomes consciousness-form. See, see, these bonds are vicious, excluded always. Saying "by internal-bearing pretense," you address us. The Māyā-cloth, passion, in supreme sky, this is thrown. Thus elephant-skin, bloodied, on head, carrying, you say, Lord. You reveal the internal-power-knife, saṃsāra.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes cidbhairava (supreme, nectar-form, one, radiant; Śiva swallows all; praise/praiser/praised not different) and pāśas (bonds vicious, excluded always; Māyā-cloth/passion thrown in supreme sky; internal-power-knife cuts saṃsāra).

**KEY TERMS.** cidbhairava (consciousness-Bhairava), paramāmṛta (supreme nectar), atidīpta (very radiant), pāśa (bond), māyāpaṭa (Māyā-cloth), antaḥśakti (internal power), kṛpāṇī (knife).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Cidbhairava: consciousness-Bhairava alone, supreme, nectar-form, one, very radiant; I bow to Śiva swallowing all by radiant instrument-wheel; praise/praiser/praised not different; as it appears, becomes consciousness-form. (2) Pāśas: see, see, these bonds vicious, excluded always; by internal-bearing pretense, you address us; Māyā-cloth, passion, in supreme sky, thrown; elephant-skin, bloodied, on head, carrying, you say, Lord; you reveal internal-power-knife, saṃsāra.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.
