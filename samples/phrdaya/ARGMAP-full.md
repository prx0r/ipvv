# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — ARGMAP

## phr-p01 (1–5)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja, summarizing Pratyabhijñā.
- question: What is citi (consciousness)?
- premises: Sūtra 1: citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ (consciousness is free, cause of world's accomplishment). Sūtra 2: svecchayā svabhittau viśvam unmīlayati (by own will, on own screen, unfolds world). Sūtra 3: tan nānā anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt (that [world], varied, by fitting object/subject division). Sūtra 4: citisaṃkocātmā cetano 'pi saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ (consciousness-contracted self, even the conscious one, is contracted-world-filled). Sūtra 5: cittam (mind).
- inference: Citi = free consciousness, cause of world; unfolds world by own will; world varies by subject/object; consciousness contracts into saṃsārī; mind is this contraction.
- conclusion: Citi doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## phr-p02 (6–10)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What is the contracted state?
- premises: Sūtra 6: tanmayo māyāpramātā (that [consciousness]-filled is Māyā-knower). Sūtra 7: saptapañcakasvabhāvaḥ (seven-septenary nature). Sūtra 8: tadbhūmikāḥ sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ (those-grounded are all-view states). Sūtra 9: cidvat tac chaktisaṃkocāt malāvṛtaḥ saṃsārī (like consciousness, by power-contraction, mala-covered, worldly). Sūtra 10: tathāpi tadvat pañcakṛtyāni karoti (yet, like that, does five acts).
- inference: Contracted state = Māyā-knower; seven-septenary; all views grounded in it; by power-contraction, mala-covered, worldly; yet does five acts.
- conclusion: Saṃkoca doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## phr-p03 (11–15)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja.
- question: What is liberation?
- premises: Sūtra 11: tāni (those [five acts]). Sūtra 12: saṃsāritvam (worldliness). Sūtra 13: cetanapadādhyārohāt citiḥ (by ascending to consciousness-status, consciousness). Sūtra 14: meyendhanaṃ pluṣyati (burns the fuel of knowables). Sūtra 15: balalābhe viśvam ātmasātkaroti (on gaining power, makes world one's own).
- inference: Liberation = ascending to consciousness-status; citi burns fuel of knowables; on gaining power, makes world one's own.
- conclusion: Liberation doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## phr-p04 (16–20)
- speaker: Kṣemarāja (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Sūtra 16: cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ jīvanmuktiḥ (firmness in consciousness-oneness = jīvanmukti). Sūtra 17: madhyavikāsāc cidānandalābhaḥ (by middle-expansion, bliss of consciousness). Sūtra 18: iha upāyāḥ (here, means). Sūtra 19: cidaikyāmarśān nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ (by consciousness-unity-reflection, gain of ever-risen samādhi). Sūtra 20: bhavatīti śivam (it becomes Śiva).
- inference: Close = jīvanmukti = firmness in consciousness-oneness; middle-expansion gives bliss; means here; consciousness-unity-reflection gives ever-risen samādhi; it becomes Śiva.
- conclusion: The text closes with "it becomes Śiva."
- unresolved: none.
