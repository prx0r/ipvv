# L200 — Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya (20 sūtras, 4 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya (20 sūtras) is Kṣemarāja's summary of the Pratyabhijñā system. It establishes citi (consciousness) as free, cause of world's accomplishment; unfolds world by own will on own screen; world varies by fitting object/subject division; consciousness contracts into saṃsārī (Māyā-knower, seven-septenary, worldly yet does five acts). Liberation = ascending to consciousness-status; citi burns fuel of knowables; on gaining power, makes world one's own. Close = jīvanmukti (firmness in consciousness-oneness); middle-expansion gives bliss of consciousness; means here; consciousness-unity-reflection gives ever-risen samādhi; it becomes Śiva.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Citi = free consciousness, cause of world's accomplishment.
- C2: World unfolds by citi's own will on own screen; varies by subject/object division.
- C3: Consciousness contracts into saṃsārī (Māyā-knower, seven-septenary, mala-covered, worldly) yet does five acts.
- C4: Liberation = ascending to consciousness-status; citi burns fuel of knowables; on gaining power, makes world one's own.
- C5: Jīvanmukti = firmness in consciousness-oneness; middle-expansion gives bliss; means here; consciousness-unity-reflection gives ever-risen samādhi; it becomes Śiva.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Citi as non-free.
- R2: World as not unfolded by citi's will.
- R3: Saṃsārī without contraction.
- R4: Liberation without ascending to consciousness-status.
- R5: Close without "it becomes Śiva."

## 4. What the text leaves unresolved
- U1: The relation to the fuller IPK (Torella).
- U2: Whether "seven-septenary" is a technical term or a list.
- U3: The exact dating (11th c., Kṣemarāja).
- U4: The relation to the Bodhavilāsa (Kṣemarāja's own commentary).
