# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## phr-p01–p02 — citi and saṃkoca
**SUMMARY.** Citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ — consciousness is free, cause of world's accomplishment. Svecchayā svabhittau viśvam unmīlayati — by own will, on own screen, unfolds world. Tan nānā anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt — that [world], varied, by fitting object/subject division. Citisaṃkocātmā cetano 'pi saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ — consciousness-contracted self, even the conscious one, is contracted-world-filled. Cittam — mind. Tanmayo māyāpramātā — that [consciousness]-filled is Māyā-knower. Saptapañcakasvabhāvaḥ — seven-septenary nature. Tadbhūmikāḥ sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ — those-grounded are all-view states. Cidvat tac chaktisaṃkocāt malāvṛtaḥ saṃsārī — like consciousness, by power-contraction, mala-covered, worldly. Tathāpi tadvat pañcakṛtyāni karoti — yet, like that, does five acts.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes citi (free consciousness, cause of world, unfolds by own will) and saṃkoca (contracted state, Māyā-knower, seven-septenary, worldly yet does five acts).

**KEY TERMS.** citi (consciousness), svatantra (free), viśva (world), unmīlana (unfolding), saṃkoca (contraction), māyāpramātṛ (Māyā-knower), saptapañcaka (seven-septenary), pañcakṛtya (five acts).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the metaphysical frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Citi: consciousness is free, cause of world's accomplishment; by own will, on own screen, unfolds world; world varies by fitting object/subject division; consciousness-contracted self is contracted-world-filled; mind. (2) Saṃkoca: that [consciousness]-filled is Māyā-knower; seven-septenary nature; those-grounded are all-view states; like consciousness, by power-contraction, mala-covered, worldly; yet does five acts.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.10.

## phr-p03–p04 — liberation and close
**SUMMARY.** Tāni — those [five acts]. Saṃsāritvam — worldliness. Cetanapadādhyārohāt citiḥ — by ascending to consciousness-status, consciousness. Meyendhanaṃ pluṣyati — burns the fuel of knowables. Balalābhe viśvam ātmasātkaroti — on gaining power, makes world one's own. Cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ jīvanmuktiḥ — firmness in consciousness-oneness = jīvanmukti. Madhyavikāsāc cidānandalābhaḥ — by middle-expansion, bliss of consciousness. Iha upāyāḥ — here, means. Cidaikyāmarśān nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ — by consciousness-unity-reflection, gain of ever-risen samādhi. Bhavatīti śivam — it becomes Śiva.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes liberation (ascending to consciousness-status, burning fuel of knowables, making world one's own) and close (jīvanmukti, middle-expansion bliss, means, ever-risen samādhi, becomes Śiva).

**KEY TERMS.** saṃsāritva (worldliness), cetanapada (consciousness-status), meyendhana (fuel of knowables), balalābha (gaining power), jīvanmukti (liberated while living), madhyavikāsa (middle-expansion), cidānanda (bliss of consciousness), upāya (means), nityoditasamādhi (ever-risen samādhi).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p01–p02 (citi/saṃkoca).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Liberation: those [five acts]; worldliness; by ascending to consciousness-status, consciousness; burns fuel of knowables; on gaining power, makes world one's own. (2) Close: firmness in consciousness-oneness = jīvanmukti; by middle-expansion, bliss of consciousness; here, means; by consciousness-unity-reflection, gain of ever-risen samādhi; it becomes Śiva.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.11–1.20.
