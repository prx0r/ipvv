## pary-p01 (1-7)
[and]-śrī gaṇapataye namaḥ | ādyantāntargatānantaśaktiviśrāntinirbharām | anuccāryām ahaṃ vācam adhīye hṛdayaṃ vibhoḥ sa eva bhairavo devas sarve devāś ca yanmayāḥ | saivāsau bhairavī sattāsattāpi ca yanmayī tad eva bhairavaṃ jñānam ajñānam api yanmayam | sa hi bhairavasiddhāntaḥ pūrvapakṣo 'pi yanmayaḥ aikadhyam adhvanāṃ ṣa.ṅnāṃ yatra sā bhairavī sthitiḥ | paryante sarvatantrāṇām avācyā paramā gatiḥ sā cidānandamātrātmamagnecchājñānasatkriyā | hṛdayaṃ devadevasya bhairavasyāvibhāgabhūḥ citsvābhāvyād asau devaḥ svātmanā vimṛṣan prabhuḥ | anāśritādibhūmyantā bhūmikāḥ pratipadyate tatra vācakavācyātmaspandayor ekaśaḥ prabhoḥ | sthūlasūkṣmaparābhāsakramayoḥ ṣaḍvidhādhvatā

## pary-p02 (8-14)
[and]-kramo yo deśakālātmā bhedaprāṇas sa saṃvidām | vicchedād eṣa vijñātur vicitrābhāsatākṛtaḥ ubhayo tatra vai rītiḥ śuddhāśuddhātmabhedataḥ | śuddhir bahiṣkṛtārthānāṃ svāhantāyāṃ nimajjanam aśuddhir api magnānāṃ naivaṃ hṛdayaviśramaḥ | atrāntare 'py anantās syuḥ sthitayas tāratamyataḥ ucitocitameyānāṃ pramātṝṇāṃ prakāśane | sādhāraṇānyathābhāvau bhidyete mātṛmeyayoḥ avikalpasphuṭābhāsā sā sṛṣṭiḥ śāmbhavī matā | abhedenātmanirmāṇe svātmaiva karaṇīkṛtaḥ kriyājñānātmacicchaktir bhedāsūtraṇakāraṇāt | kalāvidyāpadaṃ prāpya bhedodbhede punar bhavet pramāṇabuddhiprakarā yatkriyāyā vikalpanāḥ | saiva vicchinnavicchinnakriyā jñānodyamā yadā

## pary-p03 (15-21)
