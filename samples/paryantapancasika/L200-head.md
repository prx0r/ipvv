# L200 — Paryantapañcāśikā (51 verses, 8 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Paryantapañcāśikā (51 verses) is an early stotra by Abhinavagupta. It establishes the unsurpassed (anuttara) as Bhairava/Bhairavī, encompassing knowledge, ignorance, pūrvapakṣa, all six courses, and all tantras. It then treats the course (adhvā) as place-time-self with pure/impure division, the bound soul (paśu) as contracted consciousness, the entry course (five acts, "I am Śiva"), the yāmala (Śiva-Śakti couple), the power-unfolding (śaktis), and the speech-levels (paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī). It closes with resort to the unsurpassed glory (mantra-seal secret).

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: The unsurpassed = Bhairava/Bhairavī, encompassing knowledge, ignorance, pūrvapakṣa, all six courses, all tantras.
- C2: The course (adhvā) = place-time-self; pure/impure divide measurer/known; creation = undivided shining.
- C3: Bound soul (paśu) = contracted consciousness with particular creation; unperturbed in self.
- C4: Entry = own-consciousness immersion, five acts, "I am Śiva" belief, teacher's reckoning.
- C5: Yāmala = Śiva-Śakti couple with bliss-will-sovereignty triad; full Bhairava body.
- C6: Four powers within + three from will-power (three-formed) + emission manifest; agitation only from outside.
- C7: paśyantī = agency (Lord-state); madhyamā = sequential gross speech; Māyā limits both.
- C8: Close = mantra-seal secret; resort to unsurpassed glory; teachers of old victorious.
