# Paryantapañcāśikā — ARGMAP

## pary-p01 (1–7)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice (early stotra).
- question: What is the unsurpassed (anuttara)?
- premises: Śrī Gaṇapati maṅgala. Ādyantāntargatānantaśaktiviśrāntinirbharā (fullness of unlimited power resting in beginning-and-end). Anuccāryā (unutterable) vāc (speech), adhīye hṛdayaṃ vibhoḥ (I study the heart of the Lord). Sa eva bhairavo devaḥ (He alone is Bhairava god); sarve devāś ca yanmayāḥ (all gods are of Him). Saivāsau bhairavī sattāsattāpi ca yanmayī (that goddess Bhairavī, being/non-being too of Him). Tad eva bhairavaṃ jñānam ajñānam api yanmayam (that Bhairava knowledge, ignorance too of Him). Sa hi bhairavasiddhāntaḥ pūrvapakṣo 'pi yanmayaḥ (He is Bhairava-siddhānta; even pūrvapakṣa of Him). Aikadhyam adhvanāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ (union of six courses) yatra sā bhairavī sthitiḥ (where that Bhairavī abides); paryante sarvatantrāṇām (at the end of all tantras) avācyā paramā gatiḥ (unutterable supreme goal).
- inference: The unsurpassed = Bhairava/Bhairavī, encompassing knowledge, ignorance, pūrvapakṣa, all six courses, and all tantras.
- conclusion: Anuttara = Bhairava (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p02 (8–14)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the course (adhvā) and its division?
- premises: Kramo yo deśakālātmā bhedaprāṇas (course which is place-time-self, life of difference) sa saṃvidām (of consciousnesses); vicchedād eṣa vijñātur vicitrābhāsatākṛtaḥ (from division, of knower, varied-manifestation-making). Ubhayo tatra vai rītiḥ śuddhāśuddhātmabhedataḥ (there two courses: pure/impure self-difference). Śuddhiḥ bahiṣkṛtārthānāṃ svāhantāyāṃ nimajjanam (purity = immersion of outer things in I-ness); aśuddhir api magnānāṃ naivaṃ hṛdayaviśramaḥ (impurity = of immersed, no heart-rest). Atrāntare 'py anantās syuḥ sthitayas tāratamyataḥ (between, endless states by gradation). Ucitocitameyānāṃ pramātṝṇāṃ prakāśane (of fitting measurers, knowers' manifestation). Sādhāraṇānyathābhāvau bhidyete mātṛmeyayoḥ (common/other natures divide measurer/known). Avikalpasphuṭābhāsā sā sṛṣṭiḥ śāmb... (undivided manifest shining = creation, Śāmbhava).
- inference: Course = place-time-self; pure/impure divide measurer/known; creation = undivided shining.
- conclusion: Course doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p03 (15–21)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the bound soul (paśu)?
- premises: Karmabuddhyakṣatāṃ prāptā tadāsau paśur ucyate (karma-intellect-sense attained = bound soul). Itīcchayā sa bhagavān bhairavaḥ parimīyate (by will, Lord Bhairava is measured). Mitavṛttis satī sā ca śaktis tam anuvartate (limited-function, that power follows him). Tasya vidyeśasādākhyaśivatās saṃkucatsthiteḥ (of him, vidyeśa/sādākhya/Śiva-state by contraction). Dehaprāṇabuddhiśūnyapramātṛtvam upāgaman (body-breath-intellect-void knowerhood attained). Tasyāsādhāraṇī sṛṣṭis tattannānāvikalpanā (his particular creation = varied thought). Śaivasargotthasaṃskārasaṃskṛtasya tadāśrayā (Śaiva-creation-born, refined by sacrament, its support). Pramātaivāyam etāvatprakāro 'vagrahotsave (this knower, so much, in sacrament-festival). Avyagro vartate svātmany avikalpe cidātmak... (unperturbed, in self, thought-free, consciousness-natured).
- inference: Bound soul = contracted consciousness with particular creation; unperturbed in self.
- conclusion: Paśu doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p04 (22–28)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How does one enter the unsurpassed?
- premises: Anantaitāvadākārasvīkāre 'py ekalakṣaṇām (even in accepting endless forms, one mark). Tāṃ svasaṃvidam āviśya vikalpān na vikalpayet (entering own-consciousness, do not think thoughts). Khecaryādicatussrotovāhapūrṇamahāhradām (sky-wandering four-stream-full great lake). Vyomeśvarīm ātmacitiṃ vigāhya broḍayej jaḍam (entering sky-Lady own-consciousness, churn the inert). Sargasthāpanasaṃhāranigrahānugrahakramāt (creation-establishment-dissolution-restraint-grace course). Pañcakṛtyāni kurvāṇaḥ śivo 'ham iti viśvaset (doing five acts, "I am Śiva" believe). Kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyā[naga]tinādakrameṇa vā (by throwing-knowledge-count-[nāga]-course). Guruvaktreṇa tāny ātmany ākalayya kṛtī bhavet (by teacher's mouth, in self, reckoning, become accomplished). Vikalpayitvā viśvātmā bhairavo 'haṃ mamaiva t... (thinking, world-self Bhairava I, mine alone).
- inference: Entry = own-consciousness immersion, five acts, "I am Śiva" belief, teacher's reckoning.
- conclusion: Entry course stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p05 (29–35)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the unsurpassed's nature (yāmala)?
- premises: Antaḥkārabahiṣkārāv akālakramam ātmanaḥ (inner-outer, timeless course of self). Aparādhīnam ādhatse yat tvaṃ prābhavanirbharaḥ (unsubdued you hold, splendor-full). Anuttaravisargātmaviśramodayayāmalam (unsurpassed-emission-nature rest-rise couple). Saṃghaṭṭaś śivaśaktyos (collision of Śiva-Śakti). Tadantare cānyaśaktayaḥ (between them other powers). Ānandeccheśanonmeṣā ūnatā ceśanecchayoḥ (bliss-will-sovereignty-unfolding, deficiency of sovereignty-will). Asphuṭasphuṭabhedena vedyollāsas tayor vidaḥ (unmanifest-manifest difference, knowable-surge of those knowers). Ānandānuttarābhyāṃ ca sandhībhāvo 'tha vedanā (bliss-unsurpassed and, junction, then knowing). Vedyānāṃ prāṇabhūteti pūrṇaṃ tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ (of knowables, life-essence, full, that Bhairava body).
- inference: Unsurpassed = Śiva-Śakti couple with bliss-will-sovereignty triad; full Bhairava body.
- conclusion: Yāmala doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p06 (36–42)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How do the powers (śaktis) unfold?
- premises: Vedyollāso 'yam icchāyām īśane caikataḥ (knowable-surge, in will and sovereignty, one-sided). Sṛjyasaṃyogād dvirūpād ubhayātmanaḥ (from creation-union, twofold, both-natured). Icchāsvarūpād unmeṣād anyathānuttarāyutāt (from will-nature unfolding, otherwise unsurpassed-united). Catasraś śaktyo 'ntaḥsthāḥ puṃso 'syāntaś ca dhārikāḥ (four powers within, of person within, supporters). Cidvyomno jaḍabhūmeś ca māyāmayyas triśaṅkuvat (of consciousness-void and inert-ground, Māyā-made, triśaṅku-like). Icchāśaktes trirūpāyās tisro 'nyāś śaktyo 'bhavan (of will-power three-formed, three other powers became). Visargo 'yam athātyarthaṃ sphuṭo 'bhūt sṛjyavistarāt (this emission, then very manifest, from creation-expanse). Etat sṛṣṭvātmaśaktīnām itaretaramiśrāt (this creating, of own-powers, mutually mixed). Bhavet kṣobhāntaraṃ nāsmād visargād bāhyato bhavet (there would be other agitation, not from this emission, from outside). Iyatsarvaṃ s... (all this ...).
- inference: Four powers within + three from will-power (three-formed) + emission manifest; agitation only from emission.
- conclusion: Power-unfolding stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p07 (43–49)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How do paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī function?
- premises: Bheditāntaḥkṛtākhaṇḍabhāvāvekṣā parāparā (divided-inner-made-unbroken-nature-regarding = parāpara). Kartṛtā saiva paśyantī (agency itself = paśyantī) tasyaiveśadaśāśrayā (of him, Lord-state-abiding). Madhyamā sakramā sthūlā antaḥkaraṇavartinī (madhyamā, sequential, gross, inner-instrument-abiding). Vikalpanābhinnavācyā prāṇavṛttivibheditā (thought-joined, speech-worded, breath-function-divided). Svavāsanātādavasthyād daśayoḥ pūrvayor api (own-trace-identity-state of the two prior). Māyāmātur mitatvaṃ syāt paśyantyāś ca kramo hy aṇuḥ (Māyā-mother limited-ness; of paśyantī, sequence indeed atomic). Tayor āviśya daśayoḥ kramāc chāntavikalpayoḥ (entering those two, gradually, pacified-thought). Dhanyas svasaṃvidvistāraṃ sāścaryam anupaśyati (fortunate, own-consciousness-expanse, with-wonder, sees). Sā sthānakaraṇāghātapuṃvyāpārā... (that place-instrument-striking-person-function...).
- inference: paśyantī = agency (Lord-state); madhyamā = sequential gross speech (breath-divided); Māyā limits both; seeing the expanse with wonder = fortunate.
- conclusion: Speech-levels doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## pary-p08 (50–51)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice (close).
- question: What is the unsurpassed secret?
- premises: Ādyantāntargatānantavācyavācakanirbharam (beginning-end-indwelling-endless-speakable-spoken-full). Rahasyaṃ mantramudrāṇāṃ (secret of mantra-seals). Prapadye 'nuttaraṃ mahaḥ (I resort to unsurpassed glory). Śambhor abhinnahṛdayā jayanti guravaḥ purā (of Śambhu, nondifferent-heart, victorious teachers of old). Nirvyutthānasamādhānaprāptaparyantasaṃpadaḥ (from non-arising-samādhi-attained ultimate-wealth).
- inference: The unsurpassed secret = mantra-seal mystery; resort to unsurpassed glory; teachers of old victorious.
- conclusion: The stotra closes with resort to the unsurpassed.
- unresolved: none.
