# C1 — Paryantapañcāśikā

## pary-p01–p02 — the unsurpassed and the course
**SUMMARY.** Paryantapañcāśikā opens with Śrī Gaṇapati maṅgala. The unsurpassed (anuttara) is full of unlimited power resting in beginning-and-end; the unutterable speech is studied in the heart of the Lord. He alone is Bhairava god; all gods are of Him. That goddess Bhairavī — being and non-being too of Him. That Bhairava knowledge, ignorance too of Him. He is Bhairava-siddhānta; even pūrvapakṣa is of Him. Union of six courses where that Bhairavī abides; at the end of all tantras, the unutterable supreme goal. The course (adhvā) is place-time-self, life of difference; from division, of knower, varied-manifestation-making. Two courses: pure/impure self-difference. Purity = immersion of outer things in I-ness; impurity = of immersed, no heart-rest. Between, endless states by gradation. Of fitting measurers, knowers' manifestation. Common/other natures divide measurer/known. Undivided manifest shining = creation (Śāmbhava).

**FUNCTION.** Opens the stotra: establishes the unsurpassed (Bhairava/Bhairavī, all-encompassing) and the course (adhvā, pure/impure, creation).

**KEY TERMS.** anuttara (unsurpassed), bhairava (Bhairava), bhairavī (Bhairavī), adhvā (course), śuddhi/aśuddhi (pure/impure), pramātṛ (knower), sṛṣṭi (creation).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) The unsurpassed: Śrī Gaṇapati maṅgala; fullness of unlimited power in beginning-and-end; unutterable speech studied in Lord's heart; He alone is Bhairava; all gods of Him; Bhairavī (being/non-being of Him); knowledge/ignorance of Him; even pūrvapakṣa of Him; union of six courses where Bhairavī abides; at end of all tantras, unutterable supreme goal. (2) The course: place-time-self, life of difference; from division, knower's varied manifestation; two courses pure/impure; purity = immersion in I-ness, impurity = no heart-rest; endless states by gradation; fitting measurers' manifestation; common/other divide measurer/known; undivided manifest shining = creation.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.1–1.14.

## pary-p03–p04 — the bound soul and the entry
**SUMMARY.** Karma-intellect-sense attained = bound soul (paśu). By will, Lord Bhairava is measured. Limited-function power follows him. Of him, vidyeśa/sādākhya/Śiva-state by contraction. Body-breath-intellect-void knowerhood attained. His particular creation = varied thought. Śaiva-creation-born, refined by sacrament, its support. This knower, so much, in sacrament-festival. Unperturbed, in self, thought-free, consciousness-natured. Even accepting endless forms, one mark. Entering own-consciousness, do not think thoughts. Sky-wandering four-stream-full great lake. Entering sky-Lady own-consciousness, churn the inert. Creation-establishment-dissolution-restraint-grace course. Doing five acts, "I am Śiva" believe. By throwing-knowledge-count-[nāga]-course. By teacher's mouth, in self, reckoning, become accomplished. Thinking, world-self Bhairava I, mine alone.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the bound soul (paśu) and the entry course (five acts, "I am Śiva").

**KEY TERMS.** paśu (bound soul), parimāṇa (measuring), saṃkoca (contraction), pañcakṛtya (five acts), khecarī (sky-wandering), guruvaktra (teacher's mouth).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (unsurpassed/course); p03–p04 establish paśu and entry.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Bound soul: karma-intellect-sense = paśu; by will Bhairava measured; limited power follows; vidyeśa/sādākhya/Śiva by contraction; body-breath-intellect-void knowerhood; particular creation = varied thought; Śaiva-creation-born refined by sacrament; knower in sacrament-festival; unperturbed in self, thought-free, consciousness-natured. (2) Entry: even endless forms, one mark; entering own-consciousness, no thoughts; sky-wandering four-stream lake; entering sky-Lady own-consciousness, churn inert; five acts; "I am Śiva" believe; throwing-knowledge-count-course; teacher's mouth reckoning; world-self Bhairava I.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.15–1.28.

## pary-p05–p06 — yāmala and the powers
**SUMMARY.** Inner-outer, timeless course of self. Unsubdued you hold, splendor-full. Unsurpassed-emission-nature rest-rise couple (yāmala). Collision of Śiva-Śakti. Between them other powers. Bliss-will-sovereignty-unfolding, deficiency of sovereignty-will. Unmanifest-manifest difference, knowable-surge of those knowers. Bliss-unsurpassed junction, then knowing. Of knowables, life-essence, full, that Bhairava body. Knowable-surge in will and sovereignty, one-sided. From creation-union, twofold, both-natured. From will-nature unfolding, otherwise unsurpassed-united. Four powers within, of person within, supporters. Of consciousness-void and inert-ground, Māyā-made, triśaṅku-like. Of will-power three-formed, three other powers became. This emission, then very manifest, from creation-expanse. This creating, of own-powers, mutually mixed. There would be other agitation, not from this emission, from outside.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the yāmala (Śiva-Śakti couple) and the power-unfolding (śaktis).

**KEY TERMS.** yāmala (couple), śivaśakti (Śiva-Śakti), viśarga (emission), śakti (power), māyā (Māyā), triśaṅku (triśaṅku).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p03–p04 (paśu/entry); p05–p06 establish yāmala and powers.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Yāmala: inner-outer timeless course; unsubdued splendor-full; unsurpassed-emission-nature rest-rise couple; Śiva-Śakti collision; between them other powers; bliss-will-sovereignty triad; unmanifest-manifest difference; knowable-surge; bliss-unsurpassed junction; knowables' life-essence = full Bhairava body. (2) Powers: knowable-surge in will/sovereignty one-sided; creation-union twofold; will-nature unfolding; four powers within; consciousness-void/inert-ground Māyā-made triśaṅku-like; will-power three-formed → three other powers; emission manifest from creation-expanse; own-powers mutually mixed; agitation only from outside.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.29–1.42.

## pary-p07–p08 — speech-levels and close
**SUMMARY.** Divided-inner-made-unbroken-nature-regarding = parāpara. Agency itself = paśyantī, Lord-state-abiding. Madhyamā, sequential, gross, inner-instrument-abiding. Thought-joined, speech-worded, breath-function-divided. Own-trace-identity-state of the two prior. Māyā-mother limited-ness; of paśyantī, sequence indeed atomic. Entering those two, gradually, pacified-thought. Fortunate, own-consciousness-expanse, with-wonder, sees. That place-instrument-striking-person-function... Beginning-end-indwelling-endless-speakable-spoken-full. Secret of mantra-seals. I resort to unsurpassed glory. Of Śambhu, nondifferent-heart, victorious teachers of old. From non-arising-samādhi-attained ultimate-wealth.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the speech-levels (paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī) and closes with resort to the unsurpassed.

**KEY TERMS.** parāpara (para-apara), paśyantī (seeing), madhyamā (middle), vaikharī (spoken), māyā (Māyā), mantra (mantra), mudrā (seal).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the stotra; follows p05–p06 (yāmala/powers).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Speech-levels: parāpara = divided-inner-made-unbroken; paśyantī = agency (Lord-state); madhyamā = sequential gross speech (breath-divided); Māyā limits both; seeing the expanse with wonder = fortunate. (2) Close: beginning-end-indwelling-endless-speakable-spoken-full; secret of mantra-seals; resort to unsurpassed glory; teachers of old victorious; non-arising-samādhi ultimate-wealth.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.43–1.51.
