# L200 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 17 (122 verses, 13 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Tantrāloka Ahnika 17 (122 verses) is Abhinavagupta's (c.975-1025) ahnika on bhairavatādātmya (identity with Bhairava). It establishes: thus, Bhairava-identity-giving, process, I declare. Thus, maṇḍala-kumbha-agni-śiṣya-svātma, in-five. Having-taken, vyāpti, aikya, nyasya, adhvānaṃ, ca, śiṣyagaṃ. Karma-māyā-aṇu-malina-trayaṃ, bāhau, gale, tathā. Śikhāyām, ca, kṣipet, sūtra-granthi-yogena, daiśikaḥ. Tasya, atad-rūpa-tābhānaṃ, malo, granthiḥ, sa, kīrtyate. Iti, pratīti-dārḍhya-arthaṃ, bahir-granthi-upakalpanam. Bāhū, karma-āspadaṃ, viṣṇuḥ, māyā-ātmā, gala-saṃśritaḥ. Adhovahā, śikhā... (continued through 122 verses) ... (close).
