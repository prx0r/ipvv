# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 36

## tal-a36-p01–p02 — arrival of śāstra and close
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 36: arrival of śāstra said, by vasara-agata. Śrī-Siddhas indicated, by gurus determined. Bhairava, Bhairavī, devī, Svacchanda, Lakula, anu-ruler. Gahaneśa, Abja, Śakra, guru, by koṭi-descended. Nine, gradually, studied, nine-koṭi-expansion. By these, then guru, koṭi-mātra, foot gave. To Daksha etc., both feet, to Saṃvarta etc. Foot to Vāmana etc., half to Bhārgava. Foot-foot to Bali, foot-foot which other. To Siṃha half, then remaining, two parts to Vinatā. Foot to Vāsuki-nāga, parts seventeen these. From svarga half, Rāvaṇa then took, Rāma half from that. From Vibhīṣaṇa etc. obtained, guru-disciple-rule. By parts nineteen, divided, that became, then. Part part and eight-part, said, foot-etc-difference. Foot Mūloddhāra, uttara, vṛhaduttara, tathā kalpa. Sāṃhita-kalpa-skandha, anuttara, vyāpaka, threefold, three. Goddesses there indicated, by gradation, expanded form. Ninth foot, no count said, without break. From Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, from them Siddhas, from them Dānavas. Guhyakas from them, yogis, excellent men from them. Of them, by gradation, in middle, fallen, by time, when. Then by Śrīkaṇṭhanātha's command, Siddhas descended. Tryambaka, Amardaka, named Śrīnātha, in advaya, dvaya. In dvaya-advaya, skilled, by gradation, Śiva-śāsana. Of first, descendant born, second, by daughter-course. He also half-Tryambaka-named, lineage, well-established. Therefore half-four, here, maṭhikās, by lineage-course. By disciple-grand-disciple, expanded, hundred-branches, established. Having-done, lineage-stream, essence, rasas, gathered. Having-done, Tantrāloka this, flows, all rasas. Said arrival, upādeya-nature, determined, now.
