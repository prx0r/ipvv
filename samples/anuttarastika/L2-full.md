# Anuttarāṣṭikā — L2 READ

## anu-p01 — the frame: no method, just staying
Transition here is no method at all — not contemplation, not speech, not discussion, not visualization, not steadying, not japa-practice. So what is well-decided? Speak the supreme truth, and hear it: not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness as staying. The teaching opens by ruling out every technique and landing on a single stance: stay, and enjoy.

## anu-p02 — saṃsāra is delusion
Saṃsāra is not real for those with bodies; what news of bondage? Bondage for whom never is — for him, liberation-action is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Do not abandon anything; do not grasp; live healthy, staying as you are.

## anu-p03 — worship-difference is Māyā
Worship, worshipper, worshipped — what a difference-course in the unsurpassed? Transition of whom, by whom, who enters? This Māyā, though different from consciousness-nonduality in otherness, alas stands. All is own-experience-nature-pure. Thought in vain — do not do.

## anu-p04 — bliss is the forgotten treasure
Bliss here is not wealth in the middle (like enjoying women) nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendor. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, burden-lowering-like, is the attainment of the forgotten treasure of all-nonduality — the light-rise.

## anu-p05 — things are consciousness-form
Passion, aversion, pleasure, displeasure, rise, dissolution, I-ness, misery and the rest — these things shining-forth universe-bodied are not different-natured. Manifestation you see of whom suddenly — that-that one-self-natured consciousness-form having regarded — why do you not delight, full of contemplation?

## anu-p06 — things lack truth; awaken
Former non-being, being, action indeed suddenly things always in this existence — middle-formed transformation-confusion of them, whence truth? In non-truth, unsteady, manifold, dream-delusion, clever — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-transcended, awakened become.

## anu-p07 — the universe-glory is will-born
Of things no arising is; natural, by your contemplation they shine; non-true too truth they partake by experience-delusion for a moment. This universe-glory from your will-born, its birth from elsewhere not; hence you by splendor shine in worlds one many-selfed.

## anu-p08 — all shines in the consciousness-mirror; worship Lordhood
What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal-not-eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in consciousness-mirror shines. That all from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory, Lordhood, worship.
