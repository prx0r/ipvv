# Anuttarāṣṭikā — ARGMAP

## anu-p01 (1)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (maṅgala + frame).
- question: What is this teaching, and what should one do?
- premises: Transition (saṃkrāma) here is not contemplation, speech-connection, discussion, visualization, steadying, japa-practice. What is well-decided? Speak the supreme truth, hear it. Not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness as staying.
- inference: The teaching is neither renunciation nor possession; it is staying in one's own state.
- conclusion: The frame is set: enjoy happiness as staying.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p02 (2)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: Is saṃsāra real, and what is bondage?
- premises: Saṃsāra is not in truth for body-holders; bondage-news is what? Bondage of whom never is — for him liberation-action is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Do not abandon anything; do not grasp; live healthy as staying.
- inference: Saṃsāra is delusion (mithyāmoha), not reality; bondage is a false position.
- conclusion: Live healthy, staying as one is.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p03 (3)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What is worship, and what is the transition?
- premises: Worship-worshipper-worshipped difference-course — what speech in the unsurpassed? Transition indeed of whom, by whom does it happen, who is entry-course? This Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality in otherness, alas stands. All is own-experience-nature-pure. Thought in vain do not do.
- inference: Worship-difference is Māyā; the unsurpassed is consciousness-nondual; transition is not a process.
- conclusion: All is pure own-experience; vain thought should cease.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p04 (4)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What is bliss here?
- premises: Bliss here is not wealth-middle (like enjoyment of women) nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendor-multitude. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, burden-lowering-like, is of all-nonduality-state's forgotten-treasure attainment = light-rise.
- inference: Bliss = attainment of the forgotten treasure of nonduality, not pleasure or splendor.
- conclusion: Bliss is the light-rise of nonduality's treasure.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p05 (5)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What are the things that appear, and how should one regard them?
- premises: Passion, aversion, pleasure, displeasure, rise, dissolution, I-ness, misery and the rest — these things shining-forth universe-bodied are not different-natured. Manifestation you see of whom suddenly — that-that one-self-natured consciousness-form having regarded — why do you not delight, full of contemplation?
- inference: Things are not different in nature; they are one-self-natured consciousness-form.
- conclusion: Delight in them as consciousness-form.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p06 (6)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What is the truth of things, and how does one awaken?
- premises: Former non-being, being, action indeed suddenly things always in this existence — middle-formed transformation-confusion of them, whence truth? In non-truth, unsteady, manifold, dream-delusion, clever — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-transcended, awakened become.
- inference: Things lack truth (niḥsatya); the way out is transcending doubt-fear-stain-reasoning.
- conclusion: Awakened become (prabuddho bhava).
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p07 (7)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: Do things arise, and what is the universe's glory?
- premises: Of things no arising is; natural, by your contemplation they shine; non-true too truth they partake by experience-delusion for a moment. This universe-glory from your will-born, its birth from elsewhere not; hence you by splendor shine in worlds one many-selfed.
- inference: Things don't arise independently; they shine by contemplation; universe-glory is will-born.
- conclusion: You shine in worlds as one-many-selfed.
- unresolved: none.

## anu-p08 (8)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (close).
- question: What is true/untrue, and how does one attain Lordhood?
- premises: What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal-not-eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in consciousness-mirror shines. That all from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory, Lordhood, worship.
- inference: All (true/untrue, stained/pure) shines in consciousness-mirror; knowing this, worship Lordhood.
- conclusion: Own-experience-risen glory = Lordhood; worship.
- unresolved: none.
